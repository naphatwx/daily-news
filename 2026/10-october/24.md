# Daily News — 24 October 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Saturday, 24 October 2026. US stock markets were closed. The news looked back at a hard week and ahead to a big one. **Bitcoin slipped toward $80,000** after spot ETF outflows. **The Fed decides on Wednesday**, and **Microsoft, Alphabet and Amazon report earnings** the same week. ## Technology

### Anthropic's IPO filing

- **A draft S-1 shows first-half 2026 revenue of about $16.3 billion** (WSJ, via Investing.com).
  - That is up from about $1.1 billion in the first half of 2025.
  - **Net loss: about $80.7 billion.** About $72 billion of it is non-cash accounting charges.
  - About 20% of revenue came from two customers.
- **The IPO could come as soon as November** (Dealroom, Crypto Briefing).
- One report says the filing lists about $518 billion in planned compute and cloud deals (Yahoo Finance). Single source.
- The filing could not be confirmed on the SEC's EDGAR site yet.

&nbsp;

### Meta: $25 billion penalty in New Mexico

- **Judge Francis Mathew ordered Meta to pay $25 billion** to New Mexico (Source NM, ABC).
  - The case is about the Cambridge Analytica data scandal.
  - A jury found tens of millions of violations of the state's Unfair Practices Act.
  - The judge said the state could claim about $220 billion but cut the penalty to $25 billion.
  - Unpaid amounts grow at 15% interest a year.
- **Meta will appeal.** It called the penalty "shocking to the conscience."
- New Mexico was the only state that went to trial. 48 other states took an $18 billion settlement in August.
- The ruling came on Wednesday, 21 October. It stayed in the weekend headlines.

&nbsp;

### Security

- **Node.js fixed a remote code bug in its Brotli decoder** (CVE-2026-111497) (SecurityOnline, Konfirmity).
  - An out-of-bounds write that can let attackers run code.
  - Some reports call it "Node2Shell." Official Node.js notes do not use that name.
  - **Fixed versions: 22.23.4, 24.22.1 and 26.12.1.**
  - Node.js 20 and older get no fix.
  - The release fixed ten bugs in total, including three that weaken TLS hostname checks.
  - Reported CVE numbers differ in some news stories. Check the official Node.js post.
- **Microsoft Teams will get AI phishing detection** from early November (gHacks).

&nbsp;

### Chips and hardware

- **Nvidia and Tesla will invest about $2.8 billion in Samsung Electro-Mechanics** to expand chip substrate production (TheStreet, Crypto Briefing).
  - Nvidia: 2.6 trillion won. Tesla: 1.1 trillion won.
- **Intel says Tesla will be a foundry customer for its 14A process** (Not a Tesla App).
  - Neither side said which chip Intel will build.
- **Tesla cut its 2026 capex target to about $20 billion**, from "more than $25 billion" (Yahoo Finance).
- **Tesla's Terafab chip plant in Austin** is in early stages. Machine setup is planned for the first half of 2027.
