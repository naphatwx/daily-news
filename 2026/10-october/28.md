# Daily News — 28 October 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Wednesday, 28 October 2026. **The Fed held rates at 3.75%–4.00%**, with two officials voting for a hike. **US stocks closed slightly higher.** **Microsoft rose and Alphabet fell sharply after hours** on earnings. **OpenAI plans to buy Google TPUs.** **Bitcoin climbed back above $81,000.**

---

## Finance

### Fed and bonds

- **The Fed kept its range at 3.75%–4.00%** (CNN, Kiplinger).
  - Vote: 10–2. Governor Lisa Cook and Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack wanted another 0.25-point hike.
  - The hold follows a hike in September.
  - Chair Kevin Warsh said the Fed believes the "medicine" is working and stays committed to beating inflation.
- **Odds of a December hike rose to 85%**, from 78% the day before (CME FedWatch, via Newser).
- **10-year Treasury yield rose about 3 basis points to about 5.02%.**
- **Coming up:** September PCE inflation on 29 October.

&nbsp;

### Stocks

- **US stocks closed slightly higher** (Newser wire report, Xinhua):
  - S&P 500: +0.3% (+21.38) to 7,743.98.
  - Nasdaq Composite: about +0.3% to about 26,550.
  - Dow: +0.19% (+97.53) to 52,475.10.
  - The S&P 500 and Dow rose for a third day in a row (CNBC).
- **Microsoft earnings (fiscal Q1, quarter ended 30 September):**
  - Revenue: $92.3 billion, +19% (SEC 8-K).
  - Azure revenue: about $33.2 billion, +48%.
  - Quarterly capex: about $39.4 billion, below the roughly $46.6 billion expected.
  - Shares rose about 2.6%–4% after hours. Investors saw lower capex as a relief.
- **Alphabet earnings (Q3):**
  - Revenue: about $127.6 billion, +25%.
  - Google Cloud revenue: about $30.7 billion, +103%. Analysts expected about $28.7 billion.
  - Quarterly capex: $51.4 billion. 2026 capex kept at $195–205 billion. 2027 capex expected above $300 billion.
  - Free cash flow turned negative, about −$9.1 billion (Motley Fool). Search growth slowed.
  - Reports call it its first top- and bottom-line miss in nearly four years.
  - Shares fell about 8%–10% after hours.
- **SK Hynix reported record quarterly results** (Tech Startups).
  - Revenue about $75.5 billion. Operating profit near $63 billion.
  - It says its output is effectively sold through 2029.

## Technology

### AI chips and infrastructure

- **OpenAI plans to buy Google's TPU chips** (The Information, Bloomberg, CNBC).
  - OpenAI already rents TPUs. Buying them would cut its reliance on Nvidia.
  - OpenAI is also building its own chip with Broadcom.
  - Neither company has confirmed the deal.
- **Google is changing its cloud plan.** It will use partners to sell more of its AI chips (CNBC).
- **Broadcom gave details of the $63 billion financing vehicle** for Anthropic.
  - Backed by Blackstone and three big banks.
  - It buys TPUs and network gear and leases them to Anthropic for five years.
- **Nvidia and AMD reportedly put $4 billion into Meta** (single digest report; earlier reports said $3 billion from Nvidia).

&nbsp;

### AI models and labs

- **Google DeepMind's Genie 4 world model** adds native audio and real-time steering.
  - Trusted testers first. Google AI Ultra users later.
- **OpenAI says its models refuted a key computing conjecture.** Not yet checked by outside experts.
- **Anthropic expanded its GSA deal.** US government groups get discounted access to Claude.
- **Noyb filed complaints against Scale AI** over alleged surveillance of workers on its Outlier platform.

&nbsp;

### Security

- **PHP2Shell attack hit 25 servers** (The Hacker News).
  - A suspected China-linked group used an AI-assisted exploit chain.
  - It reached Active Directory in a US government-linked network.
  - Advice: patch PHP now (CVE-2026-109618, CVE-2026-109619).
- **CISA added new known exploited flaws** to its catalog:
  - Node2Shell Brotli flaw (CVE-2026-111497).
  - A Broadcom VMware ESX logging flaw.
  - A Linux kernel initialization flaw.
- **Oracle flaws under attack:**
  - E-Business Suite SQL injection (CVE-2026-101767) is being probed days after its patch.
  - The PeopleSoft zero-day used by ShinyHunters since May is three chained flaws.
- **Microsoft shipped emergency Windows updates** to block compromised eMudhra certificates.
- **Chrome 156 and Firefox 159** shipped large security updates.
- **GMO (Japan) offers up to $1 million** for tips on the attackers behind its cloud breach.
