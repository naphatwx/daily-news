# Daily News — 4 October 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Sunday, 4 October 2026. US markets were closed, so the finance section covers the weekend's news and the week ahead. **Asian stocks rose on Monday's open** after Friday's weak US jobs report eased Fed rate-hike bets; **the Nikkei rallied about 2%**. **Iraq sent a supertanker of crude through the Strait of Hormuz** for the first time in decades, while **the strait stays effectively closed** to normal traffic. **Analysts raised their 2026 oil forecasts**, and **Bitcoin held near $84,700**. In tech, **Meta AI said its Muse Spark model helped solve five open math problems**, **Nvidia launched an "Open Agent Safety Platform"**, **Apple is tightening AI agents' access to Mac data**, and **an Arizona court threw out a sentence swayed by an AI deepfake video**.

## Finance

### Asia opens higher as hike bets fade

- **MSCI's Asia Pacific index rose 0.5%** early in the week.
- **Japan's Nikkei 225 rallied about 2%**, led by **technology shares**.
- Driver: **September payrolls rose just 29,000** and **unemployment hit 4.2%**, cutting pressure on the Fed to keep raising rates.
- Markets now price in **less than one more Fed rate hike for the rest of 2026**.
- **Oil climbed** as Hormuz disruption dragged on.

- **Dow +0.49% (about +250 points) to 51,177.**
- Dow leaders: **Cisco +3.14%**, **Caterpillar +2.30%**, **UnitedHealth +1.77%**.
- Dow laggards: **Nike −3.61%**, **IBM −1.36%**, **Amgen −1.10%**.

&nbsp;

### Oil: Iraq runs the Hormuz gauntlet

- **Iraqi Oil Tankers Co. loaded a supertanker (VLCC) with 2 million barrels** and sent it **through the Strait of Hormuz**.
  - It is the **first time in decades** Iraq has shipped its own oil past the strait.
  - Goal: **move away from selling only at Basra** and get **better prices outside the Gulf**.
- **The strait remains effectively closed**: IMF PortWatch logged **1 transit on 27 September** versus about **85 a day** before the crisis.
- **A Kuwait-flagged tanker was hit by a projectile on 1 October**; reports say **seven tankers were targeted in five days**.
- **US Defense Secretary Hegseth called the US blockade "ironclad"**; this is not independently confirmed.
- **A Reuters poll now puts 2026 Brent at an average of $89.05 a barrel**, up from **$85.08** a month ago.

### AI spending worries

- Analysts warn AI infrastructure spending could reach **$30 trillion by 2050**, against about **$100 billion of AI revenue a year today**.
- Example cited: **Anthropic's planned $518 billion over 10 years** versus **$4.6 billion of 2025 revenue**.
- Estimate: the industry needs **$3.55 trillion of annual revenue by 2032** to earn a **10% return**.

## Technology

### Meta AI: model helps solve open math problems

- **Meta AI released six math papers** written with human mathematicians using **Muse Spark in Thinking Mode**.
- **Five of the six solve previously open problems.**

&nbsp;

### AI agent safety tightens

- **Nvidia announced the "Open Agent Safety Platform"** with **hundreds of partner companies** to **monitor and isolate rogue AI agents**.
- **Apple is preparing stricter Full Disk Access rules** for AI agents on Mac.
  - Aim: block unapproved access to **files, email, messages and browsing history**.
- **Apple, AWS and OpenAI have tightened agent permissions.**
- **OpenAI's probe into unauthorized agent access** costs **over $500,000 a day**, covers **50 petabytes of data**, and has **notified 100+ organizations**.
- **Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent proposed a US–China emergency channel** for AI incidents.

&nbsp;

### OpenAI and AI policy

- **OpenAI published docs for a `reasoning.effort` setting** with levels **low, medium, high and xhigh**, so developers can trade cost and speed for depth.
- Follow-up to Saturday: **safety-report lead David Robinson's exit** keeps drawing attention to OpenAI's safety culture.
- **Six companies — Nvidia, SpaceX, OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta and Alphabet — signed a one-page White House agreement**, continuing the self-regulation approach.
- **MITRE's "Project Meridian"**, co-chaired by **Elon Musk** with **Palmer Luckey and Newt Gingrich**, will study future warfare and autonomous systems.

&nbsp;

### AI misuse and the courts

- **Arizona's Court of Appeals vacated a 10.5-year manslaughter sentence**, ruling the trial judge was **improperly swayed by an AI-generated deepfake video**.
- **Anthropic reported an election-influence operation in Malaysia** that used Claude.
  - It ran **1,000+ fake X accounts**, a **fake outlet called "Malaysia Pulse"**, and fabricated documents.
- **A Rejetto HTTP File Server flaw (CVE-2026-61500)** found with **Anthropic's Mythos** lets attackers **forge admin sessions for full remote code execution**.

&nbsp;

### Elsewhere in tech

- **Apple is replacing some iPhone 18 Pro Max units** after **AT&T users lost cellular service**.
- **Microsoft rolled out Excel Canvas**, a Copilot feature that turns workbook data into **reports with charts and insights**.
- **Firefox 157** brings **Mozilla's new design** and the return of **Compact Mode**.
- **Nvidia raised the Shield TV Pro price by $100 to $299.99**, citing **memory costs**.
- **Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 added "Server Culling"** to stop wallhacks.
- **California's new AI workplace law** (signed 30 September) **limits AI-based firing decisions** and **biometric emotion detection**.
