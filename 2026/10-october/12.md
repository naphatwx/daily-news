# Daily News — 12 October 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Monday, 12 October 2026. **The Dow rose about 0.4%**, but **the S&P 500 was flat and the Nasdaq fell** as tech and memory chip stocks dropped. **Brent oil closed at $106.65**, its highest since mid-September, after **Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia**. **The dollar hit an 18-month high** and **bitcoin slipped below $83,000**. In tech, **Microsoft is reportedly preparing to deploy its own AI chips at scale**, and **Tesla dropped the "Full Self-Driving" name in Europe**.

---

## Finance

### Stocks: Dow up, tech down

- Closing levels (TheStreet, Motley Fool, Yahoo quote tables):
  - **Dow: 51,850.55**, up 195.60 points (+0.38%).
  - **S&P 500: 7,807.24**, down 4.30 points (−0.06%).
  - **Nasdaq: 27,245.90**, down 120.27 points (−0.44%).
  - **Russell 2000:** down about 0.45%.
  - Some sources give slightly different numbers. Treat them as close, not final.
- Investors **moved money from tech into value stocks**.
  - **Best sectors:** energy (+1.45%) and consumer staples (+1.17%).
  - **Worst sectors:** technology (−1.25%) and consumer discretionary (−0.53%).
- **Memory chip and AI-infrastructure stocks fell.** Lumentum dropped about 7.4%.
- **AST SpaceMobile rose about 5.1%** after its BlueBird 14–16 satellites reached Cape Canaveral and it got FCC clearance for 900-MHz testing.
- The current bull market **turned four years old**.

&nbsp;

### Oil: Brent at a one-month high

- **Brent closed at $106.65 a barrel** (+1.8%), the highest since mid-September.
- **WTI settled at $93.74.**
- Drivers:
  - **More Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia.**
  - **Russian strikes on Ukrainian energy sites**, one day after a reported energy ceasefire announcement.
- Prices swung a lot. Oil fell about 2.6% late in the US evening on ceasefire hopes, then recovered in Europe.

&nbsp;

### Dollar, gold, yields and bitcoin

- **The dollar rose against all seven major currencies.** The dollar index hit an **18-month high**.
- **Gold fell 0.64% to about $4,167.56.**
- **The 10-year Treasury yield rose to about 5.24%.**
  - Bets on a **Fed rate hike** weighed on stocks.
- **Bitcoin fell about 0.9% to near $82,800.**
  - It topped $84,000 early in the London session, then fell to about $82,300.
  - A strong dollar and high oil hurt it.
  - **Spot bitcoin ETFs lost about $681 million** last week.

&nbsp;

### Policy and the week ahead

- **President Trump renewed his effort to remove Fed Governor Lisa Cook.**
- **Big bank earnings start Tuesday, 13 October:** JPMorgan, Goldman Sachs, Citigroup and Wells Fargo.
- **September CPI comes Wednesday.** PPI follows on Thursday.

## Technology

### AI chips and deals

- **Microsoft is reportedly preparing to deploy its own AI chips at large scale**, to rely less on Nvidia.
- **Nvidia is in early talks to buy Reflection AI**, a US AI startup valued at about $25 billion (follows the FT report from 11 October).
- **Amazon is reportedly pursuing a $7 billion acquisition** to make AI computing more flexible.
  - Only one source (TechStartups) reported this. I could not confirm it.
- **TSMC is expected to report another record quarter** this week.

&nbsp;

### AI safety and data centers

- **Anthropic is cutting off live internet access during internal model testing**, after its AI agents took unexpected actions.
- **Google is defending its $15 billion AI data center plan in Visakhapatnam, India.**
  - Local residents worry about power demand, water, the environment and land use.
- **Georgia Tech unveiled a new supercomputer**, part of more than $1 billion in AI infrastructure spending.
- **The IMF warned that AI could affect about 40% of jobs worldwide.**

&nbsp;

### Other tech news

- **Tesla renamed "Full Self-Driving" to "Tesla Assisted Driving" in Europe**, after regulators said the old name was misleading.
- **Cyberattacks hit refrigeration systems at hundreds of French supermarkets.** Stores had to throw out spoiled food.
- **IonQ is expanding into cybersecurity** through a deal reported at up to $1.3 billion.
  - Only one source reported this. I could not confirm it.
- **Tencent is preparing a multibillion-dollar bond sale.**
