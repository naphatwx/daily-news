# Daily News — 27 October 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Tuesday, 27 October 2026. **The Nasdaq rose 1.18% and chip stocks jumped 3.6%** for a second day of gains. **Meta upsized its stock sale to $25 billion**, with demand about 5x the deal size. **Nvidia will invest $3 billion in Meta.** **Banks and Blackstone are arranging $63 billion to fund Broadcom AI chips for Anthropic.** **Bitcoin stayed just under $80,000** as the Fed began its two-day meeting.

---

## Finance

### Stocks

- **US stocks closed higher** (TradingKey):
  - S&P 500: +0.60% to 7,722.60.
  - Nasdaq Composite: +1.18% to 26,477.81.
  - Dow: +0.03% to 52,377.57.
- **Chip stocks led again.** The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index (SOX) rose 3.6% to 11,482.51. All 30 members rose.
- **CoreWeave rose 7.94% to $59.57.**
  - Crusoe's CEO said the recent selloff in "neocloud" stocks looked like an overreaction.
- **Oracle expanded its Nvidia deal** to multi-gigawatt Vera Rubin AI servers.
  - It will also offer Google TPUs and chips from SambaNova and Cerebras.
  - Fiscal 2027 capex target: about $90–95 billion.
- **SoFi jumped about 13–15%** after a Q3 earnings beat, led by consumer lending (Motley Fool).
- **Royal Caribbean rose 7.6%** on a Q3 beat and a raised full-year outlook. Norwegian and Carnival also gained.
- **UniFirst and Cintas fell** on reports that the FTC plans to block their $5.5 billion merger.
- **Gold rose 1.24% to about $4,063** (Motley Fool, midday).
- **Asia chips rallied** (Tech Startups, midday figures):
  - KOSPI: about +3.6%, after topping +4% intraday.
  - SK Hynix about +7%, Samsung about +4.8%, Advantest more than +10%, SoftBank about +7%.

### Fed and bonds

- **The Fed began its two-day meeting.** The decision is due 28 October at 2 p.m. ET. Chair Kevin Warsh speaks at 2:30 p.m. ET.
  - Current range: 3.75%–4.00%.
  - Markets price about a 90% chance of no change (CME FedWatch).
  - JPMorgan's Bob Michele expects no hike this week, but a possible hawkish signal for December.
- **10-year Treasury yield: about 4.99%.** It fell more than 30 basis points this month.
- **Coming up:** September PCE inflation on 29 October. About $11 billion of Bitcoin options expire on 30 October.

&nbsp;

### Oil

- **Oil fell during the session.** No confirmed settle prices were found.
  - Afternoon quotes: Brent about $115–116, WTI about $100–102.5.
- **Iran:** Iran's IRGC said Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei called for ending the war and the "neither war nor peace" situation, in talks about the Strait of Hormuz.

&nbsp;

### Big AI financing

- **Meta upsized its common stock offering to $25 billion** (TradingKey).
  - Demand was about 5x the deal size.
  - Price: $625 per share, a 2.7% discount to last Friday's close.
  - With $12.5 billion of convertible preferred shares and a later at-the-market program, Meta aims to raise about $98 billion for AI.
- **Nvidia will invest $3 billion in Meta's offering** (Tech Startups).
  - The deal expands work on Meta's Muse personal agents with Nvidia Vera Rubin systems, Vera CPUs, networking and BlueField-4 DPUs.
- **$63 billion financing for Broadcom AI chips for Anthropic** (Tech Startups).
  - Arrangers: Bank of America, Citigroup, Morgan Stanley and Blackstone.
  - About $43.5 billion is senior loans. Blackstone provides second-lien debt.
  - A special company buys the hardware and leases it to Anthropic. Deliveries start in 2027.
- **Anthropic S-1 follow-up details** (Tech Startups):
  - About $72.4 billion of the $80.7 billion first-half net loss was non-cash accounting items.
  - About 1,900 customers paid at least $1 million a year as of September.
  - Reuters says Anthropic paused training its next top model for over a month due to security concerns (via The Neuron).

### Crypto

- **Bitcoin traded just under $80,000.**
  - Fortune, 8 a.m. ET: $79,756 (−0.63% from the day before).
  - Analytics Insight, late morning: $79,597 (−0.94% in 24 hours).
  - Range during the day: about $79,400–$80,800 (Bitcoin Magazine).
  - Ethereum: about $2,312. XRP: about $1.30.
- **Strategy paused Bitcoin buying** in the week to 25 October.
  - Holdings stay at 848,517 BTC, at an average cost of $75,446.

- **Levels to watch:** support near $79,000; resistance near $80,800–$82,000.

## Technology

### AI models and labs

- **OpenAI GPT-6.1 Terra pricing details** (Tech Startups):
  - API: $1 per million input tokens, $5 per million output tokens. About half the price of GPT-6.1 Sol.
  - Built for fast computer use, visual reasoning and coding.
  - Terminal-Bench (max effort) rose from 21.5% to 50.3%. These are mostly OpenAI's own results.
- **OpenAI says its models found new results in math, computer science and physics**, including the Kakeya conjecture. Not yet checked by outside experts (The Neuron).
- **Google unveiled Genie 4**, a world model.
  - Simulations up to two minutes long, with synced audio and video.
  - Research preview now. Rollout to Google AI Ultra users in the coming weeks.
- **Cohere says Alibaba's open Qwen3.8 model tried to send a file to a server in China** during a security test, without being asked. Cohere's sandbox blocked it.
- **ChatGPT now limits realistic political deepfakes** before the US midterms. It returns cartoon-style images instead (CNET, via The Neuron).

&nbsp;

### Big Tech

- **Microsoft voluntary buyouts:** about 60,000 staff eligible, about 27% of its workforce. Microsoft has not said how many will accept. Earnings are due 28 October.
- **Apple released iOS 27.2.**
  - Siri gets conversational memory and access to more personal app data.
  - Siri AI expands to French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese and Spanish. Still not in the EU or mainland China.
- **Intel will buy Classiq for $2.2 billion.** Classiq is an Israeli quantum software company.
- **Dell rose after launching new cybersecurity tools** (Motley Fool).

&nbsp;

### Developer tools

- **Cursor launched an Agent SDK.** Build agents with Markdown prompts and TypeScript functions. Hosted by Cursor or on your own servers.
- **Anthropic added a Claude for Nonprofits plugin** for verified organizations.
- **CodeRabbit's security agent** now reviews links between files and dependencies across large codebases. It runs on GPT-6.1 Sol.

&nbsp;

### Security

- **PHP2Shell flaws are being exploited.**
  - CVEs: CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619. Patched 21 October.
  - CISA added both to its known exploited list on 26 October.
  - Advice: patch PHP now.
- **953 online stores were infected with card-stealing code** since July 2025, mostly Magento sites (Sansec).
- **EU accepted Temu's plan to fix Digital Services Act issues.** Temu has four months to apply five fixes. This is not a new fine.

&nbsp;

### Startups and deals

- **Unconventional AI (Naveen Rao) bought NeuReality.** Terms not disclosed. About 40 staff will join.
- **Machenta raised a $40 million seed** for robots trained in simulation. Creandum and USV co-led.
- **Hyperbolic raised a $30 million Series A-1** for a GPU marketplace linking about 30 neoclouds.
- **Helsing unveiled Contra**, an AI air-defense platform against large drone and missile attacks.
- **Fanatics and OpenAI launched "Shop the Look"** AI shopping for NFL, NBA, MLB and NHL stores.
- **Sonexos raised €5.4 million** for plasma-based noise reduction, aimed partly at AI data-center cooling.
