# Daily News — 5 October 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Monday, 5 October 2026. **The Nasdaq closed at a record**, led by AI stocks; **Nvidia hit a new all-time high** and **SpaceX jumped more than 7%**. **Schneider Electric agreed to buy PTC for $22.6 billion**, sending PTC up 33%. **ISM services slipped to 54.9**, but **prices paid hit the highest level since July 2022**, and **the 10-year Treasury yield rose to about 5.31%**. **Brent stayed above $102** while **Bitcoin slipped to about $83,000**. In tech, **OpenAI will test visual ads in ChatGPT image generation** as weekly users reach **1.2 billion**, **Huawei and Qualcomm signed a broad patent deal**, and **Foxconn posted a 47% jump in Q3 revenue**.

---

## Finance

### Wall Street: Nasdaq sets a record

- **Nasdaq +1.05% to 27,477.31** — a record close; the Nasdaq-100 also hit a record.
- **S&P 500 +0.66% to 7,773.99.**
- **Dow +0.18% to 51,267.90.**
- Leaders: **mega-cap tech and AI names**.
  - **SpaceX +7%+** after **Morgan Stanley called the stock "cheap and getting cheaper."**
  - **Meta almost +2%**, **Microsoft +1%+**.
- **Nvidia hit a new intraday high near $237–238**; market cap about **$5.7 trillion**.

&nbsp;

### Deal of the day: Schneider Electric buys PTC

- **Schneider Electric agreed to buy PTC for $22.6 billion in cash** ($205 a share, **a 42.3% premium**).
  - **Enterprise value: about $23.7 billion.**
  - It is **Schneider's largest acquisition ever**; the aim is **industrial AI** built on product and engineering data.
- **PTC shares soared 33%.** **Schneider shares fell sharply** in Paris.
- Funding: **about $5–6 billion of new equity** and **$16–17 billion of new debt**.
- Expected: **$250 million of yearly cost savings** and **about $800 million of revenue synergies**.
- **Closing targeted by Q3 2027**, pending PTC shareholder and regulatory approval.

&nbsp;

### Economy and bonds

- **ISM services PMI: 54.9** for September, down from **55.4**, and **just below forecasts**.
  - **27th straight month of expansion.**
  - **Business activity fell to 56.5** (from 61.7); **new orders 59.8**.
  - **Prices index rose to 74.0**, the **highest since July 2022** — an inflation warning for the Fed.
- **10-year Treasury yield rose 3+ bps to about 5.31%**; **30-year about 5.66%**.

&nbsp;

### Oil and crypto

- **Brent rose back above $102 a barrel** (about **$102.40**).
- **WTI fell about 0.5% to about $90.65.**
  - Brent keeps a **bigger risk premium** because of **Strait of Hormuz** threats.
  - **More loadings through Hormuz** and **Saudi Arabia's East-West pipeline** eased some supply fears.
- **Bitcoin traded near $83,000**, down from about $84,700 on Sunday.

&nbsp;

### Corporate AI numbers

- **Foxconn's Q3 revenue jumped 47% year on year to NT$3.03 trillion (about $95.4 billion)**, beating forecasts.
  - **September alone topped NT$1 trillion** for the first time.
  - Driver: **AI cloud and networking products**.
- **Deutsche Telekom expects about €2.5 billion ($2.8 billion) of savings by 2030** from AI and automation, shown at its **AI investor event**.

## Technology

### OpenAI brings ads to image generation

- **OpenAI will test labeled visual ads next to images users generate in ChatGPT.**
  - Starts **later in October**, **US free tier only**, with **a small group of advertisers**.
  - **Ads stay separate from the generated image**; OpenAI says ads **do not affect answers**.
- **ChatGPT now reaches 1.2 billion weekly users.**
- **ChatGPT Ads has hit a $1 billion annualized run rate** since its February 2026 launch.
- New partners: **DoubleVerify and Kochava** for measurement and brand safety.

&nbsp;

### Huawei–Qualcomm patent deal

- **Huawei and Qualcomm signed a multi-year cross-license** covering **5G, compute, AI and networking**.
- **Qualcomm becomes the net payer for the first time.**
- **Qualcomm will also buy some of Huawei's US patents.**
- It covers **680+ Huawei patents**, including **near-packaged optics** and **LogicFolding 3D chip architecture**.
  - Reported as the **first time a US chip firm has licensed a Chinese 3D chip architecture**.
- **Closing needs regulatory approval.**

&nbsp;

### AI models and platforms

- **Aleph Alpha released Kolibri**, an open-weight **MoE model with 78B parameters (3.46B active)** and a **1M-token context**, under **Apache 2.0**.
- **Reflection AI is preparing a US open-weight model** to compete with **DeepSeek and Qwen**.
- **Meta is building an "Agent Engine" (codename "Forge")** for its Meta API Platform.
- **Anthropic is inviting more users to 15-minute voice interviews** about how AI fits into their lives.
- **AI labs are pausing or patching advanced agent releases** after **containment failures**.

&nbsp;

### Elsewhere in tech

- **Elon Musk said SpaceXAI will rebrand to "SpaceXSI"** after **Trump ordered federal agencies to say "Super Intelligence" instead of "artificial intelligence."**
- **Finland is investigating forest clearing** tied to **a Google data center project**.
