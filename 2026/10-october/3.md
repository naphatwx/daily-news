# Daily News — 3 October 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Saturday, 3 October 2026. US markets were closed, so the finance section covers Friday's close and the weekend's news. **Stocks rose on Friday after a weak jobs report** cut the odds of a Fed rate hike, and **the Nasdaq hit a record**. **Only 29,000 jobs were added in September** and **unemployment rose to 4.2%**. **Oil eased** after the **G7 agreed to release 100 million barrels** from reserves, but the **Strait of Hormuz stays closed**. **Corporate bond spreads hit a six-month high** as companies borrow at a record pace. In tech, **OpenAI's safety-report lead David Robinson quit**, saying the company's culture is broken. **The White House named Jay Clayton to lead an AI "Super Intelligence Force"**, **Amazon ended data center NDAs and pledged $1 billion** to host communities, and **a federal judge called a Flock camera search "mass surveillance"**.

---

## Finance

### The tape (Friday 2 October close)

- **S&P 500 +0.73% to 7,722.77. Dow +0.49% (about +250 points) to about 51,177. Nasdaq +1.19% to a record 27,192.87.**
- **Russell 2000 +0.94% to 2,832.90.**
- **VIX fell 6% to 15.40.**
- For the week, the **Nasdaq and S&P 500 finished higher**; the **Dow gave back some ground** amid the global bond rout.
- The **10-year Treasury yield fell sharply** after the jobs data, then **recovered to about 5.28%**.
- The **dollar fell** as rate-hike bets faded.

&nbsp;

### Jobs report: hiring nearly stalls

- **Nonfarm payrolls rose just 29,000 in September**, versus forecasts of **84,000–90,000**.
- **Unemployment rose to 4.2%** from 4.1%.
- **Wage growth slowed to 3.0% a year**, below the **3.2%** expected.
- **Prior months were revised down**; **July now shows a loss of 10,000 jobs**.
- **Labor force participation edged up to 61.8%.**
- Odds of an **October Fed rate hike fell from about 30% to about 20%**.

&nbsp;

### Corporate bonds wobble

- The **gap between corporate and government bond yields jumped the most since March**, to a **six-month high**.
- Cause: **companies are borrowing at a record pace**, much of it to fund **AI infrastructure**.
  - **Broadcom is arranging about $60 billion** for AI projects.
  - **Paramount's new bonds fell 4% on their first day**; they help fund its **$52 billion Warner Bros. deal**.
- **Small-cap companies are down about 8.5% since August** as higher rates bite.

&nbsp;

### Oil: G7 reserve release vs. a closed Hormuz

- **G7 nations agreed to release 100 million barrels** of crude and diesel from emergency reserves, front-loaded over several months.
- **WTI (November) fell 1.5% to $91.44** on Friday. **Brent was about $102.70.**
- The **Strait of Hormuz remains closed** on **day 218 of the Iran war**.
- Reports say **Trump's cabinet met at Camp David on Friday** to weigh next steps in the war.
- **Baker Hughes US rig count fell 1 to 598.**

&nbsp;

### Movers

- **Nvidia hit a fresh record**; **Broadcom, AMD and Alphabet** also gained as investors rotated into AI stocks.
- **Tesla rose more than 5%** after **Q3 deliveries of 486,532**, above forecasts.
- **Ford stayed No. 3 in US sales** with **507,395 vehicles in Q3** (−6.6% year on year).
- **ON Semiconductor and Synaptics jumped** after revising their merger agreement.
- **Seagate fell** on news about Toshiba production.
- **Nike fell** after its weak quarterly sales (see 1 October).

&nbsp;

### Gold, crypto and policy

- **Gold fell 0.9% to about $4,140** on Friday.
- **Bitcoin jumped above $86,000** after the jobs report and **traded near $84,700 on Saturday**.
- The **IRS issued guidance on "351 exchange" ETF strategies**, warning against rapid turnover of contributed securities and pre-arranged deals.
- The **Treasury auto-enrolled more than 60 million children** in "Trump Accounts".

## Technology

### OpenAI: safety-report lead quits, calls culture "broken"

- **David Robinson** resigned in an **essay in The Atlantic** published on Saturday.
  - He led OpenAI's **launch safety reports for 3.5 years**, covering **system cards for 12 frontier models**.
  - He helped draft **version 2 of the Preparedness Framework** (April 2025).
- He says the company is **sprinting from launch to launch** without enough care.
  - He called for frontier labs to adopt **nuclear-plant-level safeguards**.
- His exit comes **days after OpenAI fired three safety researchers** (Jasmine Wang, Tomek Korbak, Mikita Balesni) for allegedly sharing confidential information.

&nbsp;

### White House forms AI "Super Intelligence Force"

- **Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton** will chair the new task force, acting as **Trump's de facto AI czar**.
- It will study **AI's risks and opportunities** and recommend **what role the federal government should play** in oversight.
- A **report is due within 120 days**.
- First reported by the **Wall Street Journal**.

&nbsp;

### Amazon answers data center backlash

- **AWS will no longer use NDAs** with government agencies on data center projects.
- New **"Built Together" program: $1 billion over five years** in data center communities.
  - Covers remaining college costs for **more than 300,000 students** in trades, IT, healthcare and manufacturing.
  - Plans **25 training centers** for up to **100,000 people a year by end of 2028**.
- Also promises **lower-emission backup generators**, **yearly energy and water reports**, and paying enough for power so **local bills do not rise**.
- Amazon warned that **local opposition threatens the US lead in AI**.

&nbsp;

### Flock camera search ruled unconstitutional

- **US District Judge Sara E. Hill** (Oklahoma) ruled a deputy's **warrantless search of Flock's license-plate network** violated the **Fourth Amendment**.
  - The deputy pulled **a month of a woman's travel history** after following her because of her California plate.
- The judge called it **"indiscriminate mass surveillance."**
- Not binding precedent, but **one of the first federal rulings** against a Flock search.

&nbsp;

### Elsewhere in tech

- **Apple confirmed a defect** cutting cellular service on some **iPhone 18 Pro Max units on AT&T**; affected phones get **free hardware replacement**.
- **Apple is limiting Mac disk access for AI agents** after complaints about **Meta's Muse**.
- **Meta launched Muse Gadgets**, an open-source **ESP32 firmware and Linux SDK** for building AI hardware.
- **Nvidia launched a 64GB DGX Spark at $4,999** amid GPU memory shortages.
- **Google's Gemini 4 Argon** (announced 30 September) is rolling out first to **cyber defenders in its Fairwind program**; pricing starts at **$2 / $10 per million input / output tokens**.
- **Musk confirmed talks with TSMC** on a **"Terafab"** chipmaking project, with **Intel** the only other partner.
- **ShinyHunters member "Rey"** was **detained in Jordan** and is cooperating with investigators.
- **YouTube changed Shorts recommendations** to favor **original content** over re-uploads.
