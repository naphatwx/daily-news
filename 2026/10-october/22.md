# Daily News — 22 October 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Thursday, 22 October 2026. **The Nasdaq fell 2.6%, its worst day since June.** **Meta fell about 7%** after its cash flow turned negative on AI spending. **Brent oil rose 4% to about $113**, its highest since May, on Iran and Houthi fears. The Dow stayed flat. After the close, **Western Digital and Bloom Energy fell** despite beating forecasts, and **RingCentral jumped about 14%**. In tech, **PHP shipped emergency fixes** for two critical flaws, and **Google DeepMind staff quit** over military AI work.

---

## Finance

### US stocks: AI selloff

- **Closing levels** (CNBC):
  - **Dow: +0.02% (+11.89) to 52,867.98.**
  - **S&P 500: −1.21% to 7,663.43.**
  - **Nasdaq: −2.6% to 26,008.86.** Worst session since June.
  - Other outlets show slightly different numbers. CNBC's are used here.
- **Why it fell:**
  - Doubts about AI spending came back after Meta and Tesla results.
  - Investors also worry about the cost of borrowing to build data centers (BNN Bloomberg / AP).
  - Higher oil prices added to the pressure.
- **AI stocks fell around the world**, not only in the US (AP).

&nbsp;

### Big movers during the day

- **Meta: down about 7%** (CNBC).
  - **Free cash flow was negative $2.1 billion** in Q3. Capex of $34.9 billion beat $32.8 billion of operating cash flow.
    - One source says this is Meta's first negative free cash flow since its 2012 IPO.
  - **Full-year capex guide raised to $135–145 billion.** The low end was $130 billion.
  - A $9.89 billion charge for teen mental health lawsuits by state attorneys general hit profit.
  - **Meta's 5-year credit default swap spreads hit record highs.** This shows rising credit worry.
  - Revenue was a record $64.6 billion, up 26%. Analysts still raised price targets.
- **Tesla: −3.08% to $374.58** (Yahoo Finance).
  - Q3 EPS beat, but operating income fell 56%.
  - 2026 capex guide cut to about $20 billion.
  - Robotaxi revenue pushed into 2027. No update on a SpaceX merger.
- **Bloom Energy: −8.7% to $217.77 in the day**, before its report.

&nbsp;

### Earnings after the close

- **Western Digital: down about 5–6% after hours** (CNBC, Investing.com).
  - Adjusted EPS $4.64 vs $4.09 expected. It still fell with the AI trade.
  - One source says shares rose 1.3%. Sources disagree.
- **Bloom Energy: down about 4–7% more after hours** (TradingKey, Investing.com).
  - Adjusted EPS $0.94. Revenue $1.24 billion, up 138%. Both beat forecasts.
- **RingCentral: up about 14–15% after hours.**
  - Adjusted EPS $1.33 beat forecasts.
  - Revenue about $676 million, up 5.9%. One source says $653 million.
- **Deckers and Comfort Systems** also moved after hours (CNBC). I found no reliable details.
- **148 companies reported on the day**, 70 after the close (Earnings Whispers).

&nbsp;

### Oil, crypto and rates

- **Brent rose 4% to $113.17 a barrel.** Highest close since 4 May (CNBC).
  - A New York Times report said US and Israeli planners prepared new strikes on Iran after the midterms.
  - Trump said the US is not negotiating with Iran.
  - More Houthi attacks hit Saudi Arabia. Saudi Aramco told staff at its Dhahran headquarters to work remotely.
  - **WTI was near $100.**
  - Kpler data show Persian Gulf oil exports are above prewar levels (The Guardian, via reports).
- **Trump invoked the Defense Production Act for oil**, 12 days before the midterms (investingLive).
- **Bitcoin: about $81,400–81,800**, down from about $84,000 at the open (Yahoo Finance, Fortune, CoinDesk).
- **10-year Treasury yield fell below 5%** (Benzinga). I found no exact close.
- **The Fed meets on 27–28 October.**

## Technology

### Security

- **PHP shipped emergency fixes for two critical flaws** ("PHP2Shell").
  - CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619. Both allow remote code execution.
  - Patches cover PHP 8.2, 8.3, 8.4 and 8.5. Reports say older versions back to PHP 5 are also affected.
  - Maintainers say there is no full workaround. **Updating is the only safe fix.**
  - The fix came early after details leaked in a deleted Hacker News post.
  - Some reports say AI tools helped find the flaws. This is not confirmed.

&nbsp;

### AI companies

- **Google DeepMind staff resigned over military AI work** (The Intercept, BBC).
  - Research engineer Sofia Liguori quit over Google's work with US and Israeli militaries.
  - Researcher Andreas Kirsch left earlier in October. He said oversight of classified deployments is too weak.
  - Google's Pentagon deal reportedly allows "any lawful government purpose", with some limits.
- **Anthropic opened a waitlist for Claude Duet**, an agent for US Pro and Max users (AI Weekly).
  - Reports say it runs on an unreleased Opus 5.5 model. Newsletter source only.
- **Meta is pushing its Muse AI agent.** Meta says it is too compute-heavy to launch outside the US yet.
- **Legora, a Swedish legal AI startup, reportedly raised $550 million at an $8.8 billion valuation.** Newsletter source only.

&nbsp;

### Policy

- **The US ban on DeepSeek in federal civilian agencies** is still in the news (ClearanceJobs, CNBC).
  - DHS made it public on 19 October. It covers apps built on DeepSeek too.
  - The Pentagon is not covered. Experts say the rule is hard to enforce because DeepSeek's model weights are open.
  - China said it will protect its companies.

&nbsp;

### Chips and data centers

- **Nvidia's DSX POD** news from GTC Berlin is still spreading. It is a container-based Vera Rubin data center that Nvidia says cuts build time from six months to 14 days.
- **Storage and power stocks tied to AI fell** after hours (Western Digital, Bloom Energy). Good results did not stop the AI selloff.
