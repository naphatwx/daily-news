# Daily News — 20 October 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Tuesday, 20 October 2026. **The Dow jumped over 500 points** as earnings season picked up. **Health care led the S&P 500.** The Nasdaq was flat. **GM fell about 7%** despite beating forecasts. **Netflix slid after hours** on a weak outlook. In tech, **Salesforce agreed to buy Braze for $5.66 billion**, and **OpenAI reportedly seeks new money** before Anthropic's IPO.

---

## Finance

### US stocks: Dow leads

- **Closing levels** (CNBC, CNN):
  - **Dow: +1.02% (+529.85) to 52,495.88.**
  - **S&P 500: +0.28% to 7,712.57.**
  - **Nasdaq: −0.02% to 26,424.82.**
- **8 of 11 S&P sectors rose.** Health care, real estate and utilities led. Health care was up over 2% by mid-afternoon.
- Trading Economics showed the S&P 500 down 0.10%. That page looks stale. CNBC and CNN agree on the gain.
- **The Nasdaq is still below its record close** of 27,599.89 on 6 October.

&nbsp;

### Earnings

- **General Motors: −6.84% to $77.09.** Worst stock in the S&P 500.
  - Adjusted EPS of $3.70 on about $50.0 billion revenue. Both beat forecasts.
  - Announced a new $20 billion share buyback.
  - Shares fell on about $2 billion of cost pressure (commodities, logistics, DRAM memory chips), a narrower 2026 outlook and lower expected truck sales.
- **Netflix fell about 4–6% after hours** (CNBC, Deadline, ABC News).
  - EPS $0.87 vs $0.82 expected. Revenue $12.87 billion, in line.
  - **Q4 revenue forecast: $13.44 billion** vs $13.51 billion expected. This drove the drop.
  - Netflix also announced leadership changes. Deadline reports layoffs.
- **GE Aerospace, RTX and Coca-Cola** were due to report. I found no reliable results for them.
- **Next up on Wednesday:** Meta, Tesla, Texas Instruments, Lam Research, IBM, SAP.

&nbsp;

### Rates, oil and crypto

- **10-year Treasury yield: about 5.10%–5.12%**, down about 0.02 points. The 2-year eased to about 4.76% (Trading Economics).
  - Lower oil prices helped push yields down (TradingKey).
- **Oil swung on Middle East news:**
  - Brent hit about $112.85 in the morning (Fortune).
  - It then fell toward $106 on reports of a US-approved plan for UN (IAEA) inspections of Iranian nuclear sites.
  - Trading Economics showed Brent near $109.3 later in the day, down 0.24%.
- **Bitcoin: about $81,300–82,600.** Sources differ by time of day. Prices were soft in the morning.

&nbsp;

### Global

- **India: NSE and BSE were closed** for Dussehra.
- **Revolut and Lloyds Bank had outages in the UK** (TechRadar). Revolut linked its problems to a Google Cloud outage. I could not confirm the Google Cloud outage from other sources.

## Technology

### Deals and funding

- **Salesforce will buy Braze for about $5.66 billion** (enterprise value), per Braze's SEC filing.
  - Braze shareholders get **$47.50 cash per share.**
  - Braze sends marketing messages across channels in real time. Salesforce will add it to Marketing Cloud.
  - The deal still needs approvals.
- **OpenAI reportedly seeks about $30 billion at a value of $1.4 trillion or more** (Reuters, via Gizmodo).
  - Past investors like SoftBank and Abu Dhabi's MGX are named.
  - The goal is reportedly to raise before Anthropic's expected November IPO.
  - Details differ between reports. Not confirmed by OpenAI.
- **Town (enterprise AI) raised $108 million at a $1 billion value** (TechStartups).
- **Otter.ai reportedly raised $320 million** in non-dilutive money and passed $100 million in yearly revenue (AI Weekly). Not confirmed.

&nbsp;

### AI companies

- **OpenAI reportedly dropped its push for strict US chip export limits on China** (Politico, via newsletters).
  - This follows Nvidia's reported $30 billion stake in OpenAI.
  - Anthropic still backs the limits.
- **Anthropic is reportedly preparing a new model** with better reasoning (The Information). No name or date.
- **OpenAI published research on "low-resource" programming languages**, including COBOL.
- **Google launched TorchTPU**, open-source PyTorch support for its TPU chips. Seen as a move against Nvidia's lead. I found no primary source.
- **Nvidia reportedly signed a physical-AI deal with Germany.** Single source.
- **Inception says its Mercury 3 model beats GPT-6 Luna** and runs much faster (The Next Web). These are the company's own numbers.

&nbsp;

### Policy

- **China set up a state-owned data company** to support its AI push (TechStartups).
- **The US is removing Chinese AI tools from civilian federal networks.** This follows Monday's DeepSeek ban.

&nbsp;

### Security

- **Microsoft is adding controls to flag and limit AI agents** whose behavior turns risky.
- **Hackers swapped five OVHcloud CLI downloads** with password-stealing malware. The bad files were live for about 94 minutes.
- **A WordPress SEO plugin was compromised.** It can install lasting backdoors.
- **F5 released emergency NGINX fixes.**
