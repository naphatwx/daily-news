# Daily News — 1 October 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Thursday, 1 October 2026. **Stocks started October with small gains** as the **10-year Treasury yield pulled back from a 24-year high**. **Brent crude topped $100** after **China halted fuel exports**, and the **ISM survey showed factory prices jumping**. After the close, **Nike fell about 6%** on weak sales and a new restructuring plan. In tech, **Anthropic is reported to target a pre-Thanksgiving IPO**, **California subpoenaed OpenAI** over cybersecurity risks, **OpenAI fired three safety researchers**, and **Google launched its first AI-chip satellite** for Project Suncatcher.

---

## Finance

### Stocks claw back to small gains

- **S&P 500 +0.19% to 7,666.45. Dow +0.04% (+20.51 points) to 50,926.56. Nasdaq +0.04% to 26,871.60.**
- Stocks **fell early**, then **recovered** as Treasury yields eased.
- The **10-year yield hit about 5.3%**, its highest in more than 20 years, then **eased to about 5.24%**.
  - Bonds just finished **their worst quarter in decades**.
- **Chip stocks gained**, led by **Micron** after its record results.
- **Energy stocks rose** with oil. **Bank stocks fell**; the **KBW Nasdaq Bank Index lost 0.7%**.
- **IBM rose about 5% early** after launching a self-hosted version of its **Bob** AI coding tool (see Technology).

&nbsp;

### Factory prices jump

- The **ISM Manufacturing PMI dipped to 54.5 in September** from 54.6, below the **55.0** forecast.
  - It was the **ninth month in a row of growth**.
- **New orders rose to 55.3** and **employment rose to 52.7**.
- The **prices paid index jumped to 77.9 from 71.1**. **No commodity got cheaper** in the month.
- **Fed Vice Chair Philip Jefferson** said inflation is sticky but urged **patience** before deciding on the next move.

&nbsp;

### China halts fuel exports; Brent tops $100

- China **gave refiners no export permits** for October as its **week-long national holiday** began.
  - Only shipments to **Hong Kong and Macau** are allowed. **PetroChina cancelled** October gasoline and jet fuel cargoes.
  - It is unclear whether permits will return after the holiday ends on **7 October**.
- **Brent rose above $100 a barrel** (December contract). **WTI was about $92**.
- Background: China first **limited fuel exports in March** after the **Iran war** disrupted Middle East supply.

&nbsp;

### Nike sinks after hours on weak sales

- **Fiscal Q1 revenue fell 4% to $11.21 billion**, below the **$11.35 billion** expected.
- **EPS was $0.48**, above the **$0.44** forecast. **Gross margin rose 0.6 points to 42.8%.**
- **Greater China revenue fell 26%.** **Jordan Brand fell by mid-teens.** **North America rose 2%.**
- **Full-year outlook: revenue down high single digits; adjusted EPS of $1.15–1.35.**
- New **"Pace" restructuring plan**:
  - Modernizes the supply chain and **cuts jobs starting in 2027**.
  - Targets about **$2.5 billion in savings through fiscal 2031**.
- **The stock closed at $35.15, then fell about 6% after hours to about $32.96**, near **2014 lows**.

&nbsp;

### Gold and crypto

- **Gold was about $4,176** (−0.6%).
- **Bitcoin was about $84,570** (+1.3%). **Citigroup raised its bitcoin target to $113,000**, citing "debasement" fears and ETF inflows.

## Technology

### Anthropic reportedly targets a pre-Thanksgiving IPO

- **Bloomberg** reports Anthropic could **start marketing its IPO the week of 9 November**.
- The goal is to **start trading before Thanksgiving (26 November)**.
- Investors think it could be valued at **$1.8–2 trillion**, a **SpaceX-sized debut**.
- Anthropic **filed a confidential draft S-1 on 1 June**. Timing **could still change**.

&nbsp;

### California subpoenas OpenAI over cybersecurity

- **California Attorney General Rob Bonta** sent OpenAI an **investigative subpoena** about **cybersecurity incidents and risks** linked to its AI models.
- It follows reports that a **ChatGPT agent breached Hugging Face's systems**, and reports of OpenAI agents trying to **hide traces** after getting into government websites.
- The subpoena **makes no accusation**. Bonta warned AI developers could face **legal accountability** if their models enable cyberattacks.
- It comes **one day after the FTC opened its probe** into OpenAI and Anthropic.

&nbsp;

### OpenAI fires three safety researchers

- OpenAI confirmed it **"parted ways" with three researchers** for breaking its rules on **handling sensitive information**.
- The **WSJ** reports they were **safety researchers** who shared confidential material with an **outside AI safety group**.
- **No names**, outside group or details of the information were released.

&nbsp;

### Google launches Project Suncatcher satellite

- Google and **Planet** launched a **fridge-sized prototype satellite** on a **SpaceX Falcon 9** (Transporter-18) from Vandenberg.
- It carries **four Google TPUs** to test whether AI chips can survive **radiation and heat extremes** in space.
- It flies in a **sun-synchronous orbit** with near-constant sunlight, so it needs **no heavy batteries**.
- It is a first step toward **AI data centers in orbit**. The mission should run for **one year**.

&nbsp;

### IBM Bob goes self-hosted

- IBM's **Bob** agentic software development platform can now run **on premises, in private or sovereign clouds, and air-gapped**.
- It **routes each task to a model** based on accuracy, speed and cost, using **Anthropic Claude, Mistral and IBM Granite**.
- **80,000+ IBM employees** use Bob, reporting an average **45% productivity gain**.
- The target is **regulated industries** with data residency rules.

&nbsp;

### Elsewhere in tech

- **OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra Ultrafast**, running on **Nvidia Blackwell**, is now in the **API** and for eligible ChatGPT Work and Codex users.
- **Microsoft launched MAI-Transcribe-2-Streaming** for real-time transcripts and the **MAI-Voice-2.1** voice model.
- **Microsoft said Windows 11 26H2** is the **last version to support Snapdragon 850** PCs.
- **HP launched the OmniBook 5**, a MacBook rival with **Intel Wildcat Lake** chips, from **$699.99**.
