# Daily News — 11 October 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Sunday, 11 October 2026. US markets were closed. **US stock futures edged higher** on Sunday night ahead of **big bank earnings on Tuesday**. **Oil rose above $105** in early trading after **new Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia**, then eased. **Trump's Russian diesel deal drew fire**, including from a Republican senator who said it may be illegal. In tech, **Nvidia is in talks to invest more in, or buy, Reflection AI**, per the FT.

---

## Finance

### Futures: small gains before a big week

- **S&P 500 futures rose about 0.1–0.3%** on Sunday night; **Nasdaq 100 futures rose about 0.2–0.5%**.
  - Sources differ on the size. CNBC had smaller gains than the Bloomberg wrap.
  - Futures **turned higher after crude oil gave up an early gain**.
- Last week's close (Friday, 9 October):
  - **S&P 500 at 7,811.54**, just short of its record close.
  - **Nasdaq at 27,366.17.**
  - All three major indexes gained for the week.
- **Asian shares were mixed** early Monday: **South Korea fell**, **Australia rose**.

&nbsp;

### Bank earnings kick off Q3 season

- **Tuesday, 13 October (before the open):** **JPMorgan Chase, Goldman Sachs, Citigroup and Wells Fargo.**
- **Wednesday, 14 October:** **Bank of America and Morgan Stanley.**
- What to expect:
  - **Banks are expected to grow earnings about 10%**, in line with history (FactSet).
  - **S&P 500 Q3 earnings growth is forecast near 29.6%** (FactSet's John Butters).
  - Options price a **JPMorgan move of about ±3.6%** after its report.
- Also this week: **September CPI on Wednesday** and **PPI on Thursday**.

&nbsp;

### Oil: Houthi attacks push Brent above $105

- **Brent rose above $105 a barrel** in early trading after **new Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia**.
  - Reports say **an attack on Riyadh's airport on 10 October killed 12 people**, followed by **another attack on 11 October**.
  - **Saudi Arabia is backing strikes on Houthi forces near the Bab el-Mandeb Strait**, a key shipping route.
- The gain **faded later on Sunday**, which helped stock futures.
- **The dollar edged higher** on safe-haven demand.
- Why it matters:
  - Higher oil adds to **inflation risk** and the chance of **another Fed rate hike**.
  - **The 10-year Treasury yield ended last week near 5.24%**, close to a 24-year high.

&nbsp;

### Trump's Russian diesel deal draws criticism

- On Friday, **Treasury issued a temporary license allowing Russian diesel into the US** (until about April 2027; some outlets say six months).
  - Trump said Russia will supply **300,000 tons now, 500,000 tons in November and 1,000,000 tons after that**.
  - **US diesel averages about $6.23–6.27 a gallon**, nearly double a year ago.
- On Sunday:
  - **Republican Senator Thom Tillis said on NBC's Meet the Press the deal may break US law.**
  - **Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent defended it** as needed relief from high diesel prices.
  - **Ukraine and European allies criticized the deal**; CNN said it **splits the Western alliance**.
- Analysts doubt it will **lower prices much**, since Russia's extra supply is small. **Russia had also paused its own diesel exports earlier this month.**

&nbsp;

### Other markets

- **Indian rupee hit a record low**, per Bloomberg, after trading near **96.9 per dollar** last week.
  - Drivers: **high oil prices, foreign stock selling and a strong dollar**.
  - **The RBI's FX reserves fell $51 billion** in the four weeks to 2 October as it defended the currency.
- **Bitcoin traded around $83,000–84,000** on Sunday, based on Robinhood prediction-market pricing (no news report of the exact price).

## Technology

### Nvidia weighs buying Reflection AI

- **The FT reported that Nvidia is in early talks to invest more in, or buy, Reflection AI**, an open-weight model startup.
  - **Nvidia has already invested $800 million** in the company.
  - Reflection was last reported raising at **a $25 billion pre-money valuation**.
  - One option is **an acqui-hire** (hire staff and license tech), which could **avoid a long antitrust review**.
- A deal **could come in the coming weeks**, but talks could still fail. Neither company confirmed.

&nbsp;

### AI funding and power: still in focus

- **OpenAI is in talks to raise about $30 billion** at **a $1.4 trillion pre-money valuation** (Bloomberg, earlier this week).
  - **UAE funds, including MGX, may put in up to $10 billion**; **BlackRock** is also in talks.
  - The round has **not closed**, and terms could change.
- **SpaceX is seeking about $40 billion in financing to buy Nvidia chips** (FT, 6 October).
  - About **$10 billion in bank loans** and **$30 billion in investment-grade debt**; **Apollo** may lead.
- **Google signed a 20-year deal with Constellation Energy** for **890 MW of new nuclear capacity** on the PJM grid (6 October).
  - Plus a separate **15-year deal for 2,700 MW** from existing plants.

&nbsp;

### Apple, Tesla and Microsoft

- **Apple holds a smart-home event on Tuesday, 13 October.**
  - Bloomberg's Mark Gurman expects **a home hub with a screen, a new HomePod mini and an updated Apple TV**, built around Siri AI.
  - This follows last week's report that **Apple cut iPhone 18 Pro component orders by 15–20%**.
- **Elon Musk said on Saturday that talks with TSMC on the Terafab chip complex are "just discussions."**
  - Tesla is also **renaming FSD to "Tesla Assisted Driving" in Europe**. **Tesla reports earnings on 21 October.**
- **Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella called for a human-controlled "emergency brake" on advanced AI**, with controls kept outside the model.

&nbsp;

### Other AI news

- **Nous Research raised $90 million (Series B) at a $1.5 billion valuation** to build an enterprise version of its open-source agent Hermes.
- **Microsoft began an October preview of Work IQ**, which links Copilot agents to live Dynamics 365 and Power Platform data.

&nbsp;

### Reported, not yet confirmed

- Some roundups say **OpenAI began rolling out GPT-6 in ChatGPT** with an "Intelligent UI" mode. Few sources confirm this; dates differ (7 vs 11 October).
