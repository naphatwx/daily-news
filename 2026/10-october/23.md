# Daily News — 23 October 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Friday, 23 October 2026. **The Dow fell 653 points (−1.2%)** as oil jumped again. **Brent topped $116**, its highest since March, and **WTI went above $100**. Stocks ended the week lower. **CrowdStrike rose about 8%** and **Crown Castle jumped 9–12%** on reports that SpaceX wants space on 80,000 US cell sites. Phone carriers fell. In tech, **PHP2Shell exploit attempts reached the millions**, and **SK Hynix dropped plans for a US IPO of Solidigm**.

---

## Finance

### US stocks: oil hits the Dow

- **Closing levels** (CNBC):
  - **Dow: −1.23% (−652.82) to 52,215.16.** First drop in four sessions (BigGo).
  - **S&P 500: −0.52% to 7,623.68.**
  - **Nasdaq: −0.2% to 25,955.34.** Small loss after Thursday's 2.6% drop.
  - One site (Ticker Daily) reported a record S&P 500 close at 5,847. This does not match any other source. It is ignored here.
- **Why it fell:**
  - Oil rose on fears that the US–Iran conflict will flare up again.
  - Higher oil feeds inflation fears. That makes a Fed rate cut less likely.
  - Consumer staples, utilities and healthcare fell (CNBC).
- **All three indexes fell for the week** (TheStreet).

&nbsp;

### Big movers

- **CrowdStrike: up about 8%** (CNBC, The Wall Street Republic).
  - Demand for security after the PHP2Shell flaws helped.
  - Reports also point to a new AI security deal with Nvidia.
- **Crown Castle: up about 9–12%** (Benzinga, Yahoo Finance, Motley Fool).
  - Bloomberg reported that SpaceX is in talks to lease space on about 80,000 US cell sites.
  - The goal would be a fourth national phone network built on Starlink.
  - Crown Castle's CEO did not confirm a deal. No lease is signed.
  - **American Tower also rose.**
  - **Verizon, AT&T and T-Mobile fell** on fears of new competition (Forbes).
- **Chevron: up about 2–3%** (CNBC, MarketBeat).
  - Exxon Mobil, Chevron and ConocoPhillips all closed at record highs on the oil jump (BigGo).
- **American Express: down about 3–3.7%** (BigGo, The Crypto Basic).
  - Q3 results beat forecasts.
  - The company plans to cut about 5% of its global staff.
- **SK Hynix (US shares): rebounded** after a sharp fall on Thursday. Details are in the Technology section.

&nbsp;

### Oil, crypto, gold and rates

- **Brent topped $116 a barrel**, its highest since March (Cointelegraph, Bitbase).
  - One outlet reported Brent as high as $117.42 on fears of strikes on Iran and more Houthi attacks.
  - **WTI went above $100** (CNBC).
- **Bitcoin fell below $81,000** early in the US session, then got back to about $81,600 (Cointelegraph).
  - About 3% lower over 24 hours. Higher oil makes rate cuts less likely, which hurts risky assets.
- **Gold: about $3,996**, down about 0.7% on the day (FinanceFeeds, FXStreet).
  - Gold lost more than 3.5% this week.
- **10-year Treasury yield: near 5%.** Sources disagree on the direction.
  - Kiplinger says yields rose. Other reports put the 10-year at about 4.95–5.00%.
- **Dollar index: about 101.6–101.7** (FXStreet).
- **The Fed meets on 27–28 October.** The decision is due Wednesday at 2 pm EDT.
  - Markets expect no change. The Fed raised rates by 0.25 points in September.
  - Some traders price a higher chance of another hike by December because of oil.

## Technology

### Security: PHP2Shell spreads

- **Exploit attempts against the two PHP flaws reached the millions** (Cloudflare CEO, via reports).
  - CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619 let attackers run code on web servers without logging in.
  - Fixed versions: **PHP 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36 and 8.2.35.**
  - Amazon says the flaws are now widely exploited. CISA says it has found no successful attacks so far.
- **Governments took websites offline** as a safety step (CNN, eBuilder Security).
  - Reports name the Netherlands, Finland, Germany, France and Belgium. Lists differ by source.
  - Australia's cybersecurity agency also took its site down.
  - Parts of Wikipedia and the Internet Archive were briefly down.
- **The US NSA told groups to take systems offline** if they cannot patch now. CERT-EU and the UK NCSC gave similar advice.
- **Cloudflare added temporary firewall rules** for all customers, including the free plan.
- **AI tools found the flaws**, CNN reports.
- **Separately, a researcher found one attacker using AI agents to break into 100+ company websites**, mostly online shops (The Neuron). Newsletter source only.

&nbsp;

### Telecom: SpaceX goes after phone carriers

- **SpaceX is in talks to lease space on at least 80,000 US cell sites** (Bloomberg, via Yahoo Finance).
  - The aim is a Starlink mobile network that competes with Verizon, AT&T and T-Mobile.
  - Bernstein called the step a possible "point of no return" for SpaceX.

&nbsp;

### Chips and data centers

- **SK Hynix stopped its review of a US IPO for Solidigm**, its NAND storage unit (CNBC, Seoul Economic Daily).
  - The listing could have raised up to $15 billion.
  - SK Hynix cited market conditions. Korean regulators and lawmakers had raised concerns about listing a parent and a subsidiary together.
  - **SK Hynix closed 4.42% higher in Seoul at 1.277 million won**, after early losses.
  - The company is still looking for money to build a new US fab.
- **Microsoft's exit from Nscale's UK data center** is still in the news (FTI Consulting, Bloomberg).
  - The Loughton, Essex site was planned at 50MW with about 23,000 Nvidia GB300 GPUs.
  - Grid officials reportedly said power may not reach the site until the 2030s.
  - Nscale is preparing for a possible IPO. Microsoft and Anthropic make up about 85% of its contracts.
- **Nvidia showed Minerva at GTC Berlin**, a platform to track GPU, switch, cable and job data in AI clusters (AI Weekly).

&nbsp;

### AI companies

- **OpenAI is close to a bridge funding round of up to $30 billion** (AI Weekly). Newsletter source only.
  - UAE investors and Coatue reportedly lead it.
  - Reported valuation: $1.25 trillion before the new money. That is below the $1.4 trillion it reportedly sought.
- **The UK will fund a £100 million AI Biosecurity Lab** (AI Weekly).
  - OpenAI and Anthropic each pledge up to £100 million in model credits and access.
- **Anthropic's Claude Duet** waitlist is still in the news. It is an agent that works in connected apps and asks before it deletes, pays or sends (AI Weekly).
- **Volkswagen Group rolled out Microsoft Copilot to more than 330,000 staff** (The Neuron).
- **A US congressional watchdog said Pentagon AI weapons projects do not always follow the Pentagon's own AI rules** (NPR).

&nbsp;

### Other

- **Microsoft shipped new Windows 11 Insider builds** to the Beta and Experimental channels. They add a speed test in Quick Settings.
