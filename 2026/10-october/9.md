# Daily News — 9 October 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Friday, 9 October 2026. **Stocks rallied to end a volatile week**, and **the S&P 500 closed near its record high**. **Chip stocks bounced** after **OpenAI told investors it expects annualized revenue of $70 billion or more by the end of 2026**. **Apple fell** on a report that it **cut iPhone 18 Pro component orders by 15–20%**. **Consumer sentiment dropped to 46.3**, close to its record low. **Oil and Treasury yields eased** but stayed high. Big bank earnings start next week.

---

## Finance

### Wall Street: all three indexes rise into earnings season

- **S&P 500 +0.59% (+46.18 points) to 7,811.54** — just short of the record close set on Tuesday.
- **Nasdaq +0.64% (+172.83 points) to 27,366.17.**
- **Dow +0.83% (+423.37 points) to 51,655.01.**
- **Russell 2000 +0.5% to about 2,808.6.**
- **All three major indexes gained for the week.**
  - Sources differ on the size: TheStreet puts the S&P 500 up about 0.95% for the week.
- **Real estate and healthcare** were the strongest S&P 500 sectors.
- Main drivers:
  - **A rebound in chip and AI stocks** after the new OpenAI revenue figures.
  - **Lower Treasury yields and oil prices**, which both eased from earlier in the week.
- **Q3 earnings season starts next week with the big banks**; Wall Street expects strong results.

&nbsp;

### OpenAI's $70 billion target lifts chip stocks

- On Thursday, **an FT report put OpenAI's annualized revenue near $50 billion**, below the **$68 billion** figure investors had used.
  - **The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell 3.4%** that day, and **the Nasdaq 100 had its worst day in seven weeks**.
- On Friday, **Bloomberg reported that OpenAI told investors it expects annualized revenue to reach or pass $70 billion by the end of 2026**.
  - The figures were shared as part of **a new fundraising effort**.
  - OpenAI also cited **77% run-rate growth in Q3** and **107% run-rate growth in its enterprise business**.
- People familiar with the matter said **the $68 billion figure included gross revenue from partners**.
  - Analysts said the gap is about **how revenue is counted**, not **weaker AI demand**.
- Market reaction:
  - **Nvidia and AMD rose about 1.7% early**; **Micron, Marvell and Intel each rose more than 1%** before the open.

&nbsp;

### Apple cuts iPhone 18 Pro orders, per Nikkei

- **Nikkei Asia reported that Apple told suppliers to cut October component orders for the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max by 15–20%** from earlier plans.
  - Reasons cited: **weaker-than-expected demand** and **higher prices from rising memory costs**.
  - Another factor: **the foldable iPhone Duo ships on 23 October** and may take some Pro sales.
- **Apple fell about 2–3% during the day** (about **$335** in the afternoon).
- **Skyworks fell sharply** — reports range from **about 4% to 6.8%** — because Apple is a major customer for its radio chips.
- Some analysts said **an order cut is not the same as weak sales**, since **fewer models are on sale** after the base iPhone 18 moved to 2027.

&nbsp;

### Bonds, oil, gold and crypto

- **10-year Treasury yield ended at about 5.24%**; **2-year at about 4.80%**.
  - The 10-year **peaked near 5.35% on Monday**, its highest in about 24 years, then eased after **a strong 10-year auction** and **lower oil prices**.
- **Oil fell on the day:**
  - **WTI settled near $90.35–90.39**, down about **0.9–1.2%**.
  - **Brent settled near $102.6–102.9**, down about **1.6%** (sources differ).
  - Iran tensions eased after **President Trump said on Thursday the U.S. will not attack Iran before the 3 November midterms**.
- **Gold traded around $4,120–4,160** per ounce, based on prediction-market levels; no clear official close was found.
- **Bitcoin recovered to the low $83,000s on Friday morning** after **dipping near $80,300 on Thursday**.

&nbsp;

### Economy and Fed

- **The University of Michigan consumer sentiment index fell to 46.3** (preliminary, October).
  - **Down 1.8 points from September** and the **lowest since May**, when it hit a record low.
  - Forecast was **47.6**. The survey director pointed to **frustration over the cost of living**.
- **Fed Governor Christopher Waller said more rate hikes are needed** to bring inflation down to 2%.
  - But he said **rates do not need to rise right away**.
  - The Fed **raised rates by 25 bp to 3.75–4.00% in September**, its first hike since 2023.
- **Samsung's Q3 guidance** (released 8 October): **operating profit of about 107.4 trillion won** on **sales of about 195 trillion won**.
  - That is **well above Q2's 89.49 trillion won**, driven by **the AI memory boom**.
  - Its **mobile business is expected to post a loss** due to higher memory costs.

## Technology

### AI and Big Tech

- **OpenAI's revenue story dominated tech news** (see Finance above).
- **Wall Street is selling a record $60 billion chip-financing package for Anthropic**, tied to Broadcom.
  - **A $42 billion senior tranche** is led by **Bank of America, Citigroup and Morgan Stanley**.
  - **An $18 billion junior tranche** is led by **Blackstone**, which commits **$9 billion**.
  - The money backs **Anthropic's lease of Google TPU chips** — the largest chip financing so far.
- **Goldman Sachs said investors will shift focus to whether AI spending turns into products that make money.**
- **Manus raised more than $500 million**, its first round since its **planned sale to Meta fell apart**.
- **A watchdog group said most of ChatGPT for Teens' special safeguards do not work** (per tech news roundups).
- **Surveillance company Flock cut staff** as **privacy backlash grows** (per tech news roundups).

&nbsp;

### Earlier this week, still in focus

- **Microsoft and Nvidia launched the Surface Laptop Ultra** (7 October), built on **Nvidia RTX Spark** for local AI work.
  - Prices start at **$2,600**; a stronger model starts at **$3,700**.
- **Amazon (AWS) said it will stop using NDAs with government agencies on data center projects** (2 October).
  - It also pledged **more than $1 billion over five years** to host communities ("Built Together").
- **OpenAI fired three safety researchers** (early October) for **mishandling sensitive information**, per the WSJ.
  - The researchers had been **outspoken about AI risk**; OpenAI says **no one was fired for raising safety concerns**.
- **Pwn2Own Ireland 2026 ended on 9 October** in Cork; the full results and **Master of Pwn** were not yet posted.
