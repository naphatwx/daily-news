# Daily News — 8 October 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Thursday, 8 October 2026. **The AI trade took a hit** after **the Financial Times reported that OpenAI's annualized revenue is about $20 billion lower than earlier estimates**. **The Nasdaq fell 1.25%** and **chip stocks dropped sharply**, while **the Dow edged up**. **Oil jumped** on Iran fears, then cut gains after **President Trump said the U.S. will not attack Iran before the midterms**. **Bitcoin fell to about $81,000.** In tech, **TSMC's September revenue rose 54.6%**, **Manus raised more than $500 million**, and **an Oracle Health breach may affect nearly 20 million people**.

---

## Finance

### Wall Street: tech leads a second day of losses

- **S&P 500 -0.47% to 7,765.36.**
- **Nasdaq -1.25% to 27,193.34.**
- **Dow +0.1% (+51.77 points) to 51,231.64** — the only major index to gain.
- **The S&P 500 and Nasdaq fell for a second day in a row**, after record closes on Tuesday.
- Main drivers:
  - **The OpenAI revenue report** (see below), which hit AI and chip stocks.
  - **Still-high Treasury yields** and **elevated oil prices**.

&nbsp;

### OpenAI revenue report shakes AI stocks

- **The FT reported that OpenAI's annualized revenue was near $50 billion at the end of September.**
  - Earlier reports put it at about **$68–70 billion**, so the gap is about **$20 billion**.
- Reason for the gap: **OpenAI and Anthropic count annualized revenue differently**.
  - **Anthropic counts sales made through its cloud partners**; **OpenAI does not**.
  - The higher estimate came from **comparing OpenAI with Anthropic's numbers**.
- OpenAI's investor deck also showed **77% run-rate growth in Q3** and **107% run-rate growth in its enterprise business** (per CNBC).
- Market reaction:
  - **The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell as much as 4%.**
  - **Arm, Intel and Marvell each fell more than 6%**; **Micron fell about 5%**.
  - **Broadcom fell about 4.6%**; **Nvidia fell about 3%**.
  - **Oracle and CoreWeave** also sank.

&nbsp;

### Other movers

- **Palantir rose about 3%** after **Goldman Sachs upgraded it to Buy** with a **$230 target**.
- **PepsiCo beat Q3 estimates** (revenue **$25.27 billion**, adjusted EPS **$2.34**).
  - But it **cut its core EPS growth outlook to 2.5–3%**.
- **Coinbase, Strategy and Robinhood each fell about 1%** as bitcoin slid.
- **Amazon confirmed it cut fewer than 1,000 jobs**, mostly in retail.

&nbsp;

### Bonds, oil, gold and crypto

- **10-year Treasury yield briefly touched 5.34%** — near its **highest since 2002** — then **eased to about 5.23–5.24%**.
  - **30-year yield was about 5.61%.**
- **Oil jumped**, with **WTI up as much as 5% to almost $93** on reports that **the Pentagon was preparing for possible strikes on Iran**.
  - Prices **cut gains** after **Trump said the U.S. will not attack Iran before the 3 November midterms**.
  - **Brent traded near $103–104**; **WTI near $90–91** later in the day.
  - **A tropical storm forced Gulf of Mexico producers to cut output.**
- **Gold was flat to up about 1%**, around **$4,115–4,200**.
- **Bitcoin fell about 2% to about $81,000**, its **lowest in about three weeks**.
  - **About $974 million in crypto positions were liquidated** in 24 hours.

&nbsp;

### Economy and Fed

- **Initial jobless claims fell by 2,000 to 197,000** (week ended 3 October).
  - **Continuing claims rose by 17,000 to 1.71 million.**
- **Minutes from the September FOMC meeting** (released 7 October) were still in focus.
  - In September the Fed **raised rates by 25 bp to 3.75–4.00%** — its **first hike since 2023**.
  - Futures pointed to **a hold at the late-October meeting** as the most likely outcome.
- **FactSet expects S&P 500 Q3 earnings growth of about 29.5%.**
- **Jeff Bezos said Blue Origin will likely go public in the coming years.**
  - It recently raised **$10 billion at a $140 billion valuation**.

## Technology

### Chips and AI hardware

- **TSMC's September revenue was NT$511.86 billion**, **up 54.6% from a year ago** and **down 0.6% from August**.
  - **Revenue for the first nine months was NT$3.90 trillion**, up **41.1%**.
  - TSMC reports full Q3 results on **15 October**.
- **GlobalFoundries signed a five-year deal to make silicon interposers for TSMC** at its **Malta, New York** plant.
  - Production should ramp in **the first half of 2028**.
- **Samsung was due to give Q3 guidance**, with forecasts calling for **another record quarterly profit** on the **AI memory boom**.
- **HPE showed its first ProLiant Gen 13 servers** with **AMD EPYC "Venice" chips**.
  - The **DL585a** supports **up to eight double-width GPUs**; available **March 2027**.
- **Nvidia expanded its Halos safety system to humanoid robots and robotaxis**; **Agility Robotics** built it into its **Digit** humanoid.

&nbsp;

### AI products and funding

- **OpenAI's GPT-6 with "Intelligent UI" reached Free and Go users in ChatGPT** on 8 October, one day after paid tiers.
- **Anthropic released Claude Haiku 5.5** on 7 October.
  - **$0.10 per million input tokens** and **$0.50 per million output tokens** (prompts under 100K tokens) — about **75% cheaper than Haiku 4.5**.
  - **1M-token context window**; Anthropic also **halved Sonnet 5.5 cache-read prices**.
- **Manus raised more than $500 million** after its **planned sale to Meta fell apart** (per The Information).
- **Tencent is weighing an offshore bond sale of up to $5 billion** (per Bloomberg).
- Other funding:
  - **Universal Quantum** — **$100 million Series A** (trapped-ion quantum computers).
  - **Mecka AI** — **$60 million Series B** led by **Sequoia**, with **Nvidia and M12** (human motion data for humanoid robots).
  - **Vesta** — **$30 million Series B** (mortgage software).

### Security

- **Oracle Health (Cerner) breach may have exposed data on nearly 20 million people.**
  - Attackers got into **legacy Cerner systems** around **February 2025**.
  - **Texas filings alone list nearly 3 million residents.**
- **Pwn2Own Ireland 2026**: researchers showed **45 new zero-days on day two**, earning **$232,500**.
  - **Samsung Galaxy S26** was hacked **three more times**; targets also included **Oracle Autonomous AI Database** and **Home Assistant Green**.

&nbsp;

### Policy and infrastructure

- **Finland ordered Google to halt preparatory work at data-center sites in Muhos and Kajaani** over **suspected illegal forest clearing**.
  - Tied to Google's **€13 billion Finland investment**; Google has until **14 October** to respond.
- **A drone attack hit Yandex's Sasovo data center in Russia**, causing a fire and **halting operations**.
  - It reportedly housed **two of Yandex's three Nvidia A100 supercomputers**; consumer services kept running.
- **Denmark proposed a law to protect people's faces and voices from AI deepfakes**, with exceptions for satire and parody.
  - Target start: **early 2027**.

### Science

- **The 2026 Nobel Prize in Chemistry went to Henri B. Kagan and Kenso Soai** for work on **chirality and autocatalysis**.
