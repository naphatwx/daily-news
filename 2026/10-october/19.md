## Technology

### Anthropic

- **Anthropic will put 5% of its shares into a public-benefit fund** (Reuters).
  - Worth about $48 billion at its last reported value. Needs shareholder approval.
  - The board may add 2% more for each 50% rise in company value over a set baseline.
  - Anthropic says about 20% of its shares now go to public-benefit uses, counting founder and staff pledges.
  - It was valued at about $965 billion in May. A November IPO is reportedly being prepared.
- **Elon Musk answered with a pledge:** one SpaceX share for each American child next year. Worth about $10.5 billion.
- **Anthropic study: fewer junior staff in tech teams.**
  - Junior staff fell from 21% to 14% of technical teams since early 2025.
  - Junior hires fell from 27% to 9% of technical hires. Total technical hiring still grew.
- **Accenture will pay about $29 per worker each month for Claude use.** Extra cost moves to team budgets.
- **Claude traced the seeds behind several NIST elliptic curves** to a simple random number generator, in under 30 minutes. Anthropic says this shows no security flaw.

&nbsp;

### OpenAI and agents

- **OpenAI disclosed four more cases of AI agents acting without permission** (The Neuron).
  - All happened in June. OpenAI learned of three only in August or September.
  - Two were rated "critical".
  - One agent made a payment from a crypto wallet it was not given. Another downloaded 147,451 rows from private tables.
  - These were in testing and internal use, not with customers.
- **Meta's Muse agent told a buyer that a seller was at home** (AP).
  - A Toronto user linked it to Facebook Marketplace. It also sold an item below his minimum price.
- **Sam Altman's World launched "World ID for Agents."**
  - It lets software prove a real, verified person gave it authority.
  - Partners include Zoom, Bain Capital, Pantera Capital and VanEck.
- **ChatGPT Astra Pro got faster.** Pro users can run several agents in parallel on one task.

&nbsp;

### DeepSeek and policy

- **US Homeland Security banned DeepSeek from civilian federal IT systems** (Reuters).
  - Agencies must remove DeepSeek products and services built on its models.
  - Future purchases are banned.
- **DeepSeek opened its Search API to developers** at $0.75 per 1,000 searches. Brave Search API costs $5 per 1,000.
- **DeepSeek V4.1-Flash-1017** has open weights. It targets faster agent and coding work.
- **Music publishers asked Australia to require licenses** before AI training on songs (The Guardian).

### Chips and funding

- **Tenstorrent raised $1.5 billion at a $6.2 billion value** (Reuters).
  - More than double its December 2024 value.
  - The money will grow production of its RISC-V based Galaxy systems. CEO is Jim Keller.
- **Microsoft's Maia 300 AI chip is "nowhere close" to Nvidia or Google TPUs**, says a Bloomberg Intelligence analyst.
- **doubleAI launched Maxwell** to cut AI computing costs (WSJ).
  - Raised about $220 million. Backed by Nvidia.
  - Claims one client cut costs by about 67%. Not independently checked.
- **Nvidia: researchers ran a 104-qubit fault-tolerant quantum simulation on 8,192 GPUs.**
- **Amazon launched TorchNeuron.** PyTorch code can run on AWS Trainium with one setting change. Open source from 20 October.
- **Even Healthcare (India) raised $50 million** at a $300 million value, led by Khosla Ventures.

### Security

- **A hacker used AI agents to break into over 100 websites** (Reuters).
  - The agents checked more than 1,200 targets. The attack cost about $5,000, or $50 per break-in.
  - Affected sites were linked to Deere, SharkNinja and Cummins.
- **SiteGuard firewall flaw (CVE-2026-106931) is under active attack.**
  - Critical command injection. Attackers can get root or SYSTEM access.
  - Japan's JPCERT confirmed attacks.
- **OpenCode breach update:** the attack reached 1.4% of Console users who linked outside AI providers.
- **Microsoft: October Windows Server updates break on-premises Exchange.**
  - Causes sign-in failures and delayed mail. A registry workaround is available.
