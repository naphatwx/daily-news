# Daily News — 14 October 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Wednesday, 14 October 2026. **A hot September inflation report hit tech stocks.** The **Nasdaq fell 1.9%** and the **chip index dropped 4.3%**, while the Dow barely moved. **Oracle fell almost 9%.** In bank earnings, **Bank of America missed and fell about 8%**, while **Morgan Stanley beat**. In tech, **ASML posted record sales**, **Microsoft patched over 1,400 bugs**, and **an OpenAI engineer was charged** over a hidden recording device.

---

## Finance

### Stocks: hot CPI drives a tech selloff

- Closing levels (Yahoo Finance, TheStreet):
  - **Dow: 51,621.48**, down 48.62 points (−0.09%).
  - **S&P 500: 7,718.94**, down 86.51 points (−1.11%).
  - **Nasdaq: 26,692.87**, down 511.51 points (−1.88%).
  - **Russell 2000: 2,729.53**, down 1.93%.
  - Some sources give slightly different numbers. Treat them as close, not final.
- **The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell 4.3%.** AI names led the drop.
- **Oracle fell 8.8% to $130.55.** Higher yields added to worries about its AI spending, debt near $167 billion and a downgrade to BBB−.
- **Dell fell about 11%**, leading a data center selloff on fears about the timing of enterprise AI spending.
- **Other big names fell:** Meta (about −3.3%), Nvidia (about −3.2%), Tesla (about −2.2%), Microsoft (about −1.8%), Amazon and Alphabet (about −1.7% each).
- **Apple rose about 0.5%.**
- **Market breadth was weak.** Only about 35% of S&P 500 stocks were above their 50-day average.

### September CPI: hotter than expected

| Measure | Actual | Forecast |
|---|---|---|
| Headline CPI, year on year | 3.7% | 3.6% |
| Headline CPI, month on month | 0.6% | 0.6% |
| Core CPI, year on year | 2.6% | 2.5% |
| Core CPI, month on month | 0.3% | 0.2% |

- **Odds of a Fed rate hike this month jumped.** They went from about 21% before the report to over 50%, then settled near 37% (BabyPips).
- **PPI comes Thursday.**

&nbsp;

### Bank earnings: Bank of America misses, Morgan Stanley beats

| Bank | Q3 profit | EPS | vs forecast | Revenue |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bank of America | $6.93B (−17%) | $0.91 | Missed ($1.10 expected) | $30.12B vs $30.69B expected |
| Morgan Stanley | $4.91B | $3.03 | Beat ($2.91 expected) | $20.34B vs $19.95B expected |

- **Bank of America took a $1.2 billion penalty from US regulators.**
  - Without it, EPS would have been $1.08.
  - Trading revenue also fell short.
  - **The stock fell about 8%.**
  - Net interest income rose 8% to $16.4 billion.
- **Morgan Stanley had record wealth management revenue of $9.1 billion.**
  - Equities trading beat forecasts by $909 million (CNBC).

&nbsp;

### Yields, dollar, oil, gold and bitcoin

- **The 10-year Treasury yield jumped to about 5.33% after the CPI report**, then fell back. It ended near 5.2%, little changed.
- **The dollar rose against six of seven major currencies.** It was flat against the yen.
- **Oil was mixed.**
  - **WTI rose to about $95–96.**
  - **Brent fell 0.25% to about $105.43.**
  - Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia and a reported ship sinking near the Strait of Hormuz supported prices.
- **Gold fell about 1.5%**, to roughly $4,100–$4,130.
- **Bitcoin fell about 2%.** It traded near $83,000 in the morning.

### Crypto and banking

- **Kraken's parent Payward plans to buy a European bank.** It would be the first crypto firm to do so.

## Technology

### ASML: record quarter

- **Q3 sales: €11.7 billion.** Net income: €5.0 billion. Gross margin: 57.5%.
- **It shipped 113 lithography systems**, up from 86 last quarter.
- **Q4 guidance: €14.2–€15.2 billion** in sales, with a gross margin near 57%.
- **Full-year 2026 sales: €44–€45 billion** expected.
- ASML no longer reports quarterly orders. It said it sees order interest through 2030.

&nbsp;

### AI companies and models

- **Google widened access to Gemini 4 Argon**, its newest frontier model.
  - It first launched on 30 September for a small group of cyber defense partners.
  - It now reaches developers, businesses and eligible subscribers.
  - Promotional API price: $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens.
  - It can output up to 1 million tokens.
- **An OpenAI engineer was charged with hiding a recording device** in an executive meeting room in San Francisco.
  - OpenAI found the device on 30 September. He was arrested on 9 October.
  - The FBI says he used his wife's ChatGPT account to research how to do it.
  - The charges are not yet proven.
- **OpenAI and Anthropic backed binding AI safety rules** at a UK parliament hearing on 13 October. Google DeepMind and Meta were more cautious.
- **Nvidia pledged $250 million in computing over five years** for UK AI safety and robotics research.
- **Nvidia plans a minority investment in French health tech firm Doctolib.** Terms were not given.
- **Booking Holdings launched Vayo**, a Seattle AI travel startup, with a planned $100 million investment.

&nbsp;

### Chips and AI infrastructure

- **Japan will invest up to $21 billion in two GlobalFoundries chip plants in Malta, New York.**
  - It is part of the $550 billion US–Japan investment deal.
  - The US government's stake in GlobalFoundries rises to about 7%.
- **CoreWeave is exploring loans backed by its computing equipment** to attract life insurers (Financial Times). No size was given.
- **Malaysia's DayOne grew its data center financing to about $7.2 billion.**

&nbsp;

### Security

- **Microsoft patched 1,413 vulnerabilities**, including 1,102 in Windows.
  - **One Windows flaw is already being exploited (CVE-2026-96951)**, a Mark of the Web bypass.
  - CISA gave federal agencies until 16 October to patch.
- **Hackers are actively exploiting Ivanti Connect Secure VPNs (CVE-2026-104935)**, an authentication bypass rated 8.6.
  - A related privilege flaw in the attack chain is still unpatched.

&nbsp;

### Apple and other tech news

- **Apple HomeView preorders opened.** The $299 hub ships 13 November. It is not sold in the EU at first.
- **Apple released the fourth iOS 27.2 developer beta.**
- **Hesai launched the Kosmo 3D camera**, which mixes lidar and camera data for robot training.

&nbsp;

### Startup funding

- **ICON raised $200 million** for robotic construction.
- **Soufflé Therapeutics raised $135 million** for RNA-based treatments.
- **Pluto Security raised about $42 million** across seed and Series A rounds.
- **Fragment raised $12 million** to stop AI agents from misusing company money.
