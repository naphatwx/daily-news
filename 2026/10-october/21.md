# Daily News — 21 October 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Wednesday, 21 October 2026. **All three US indexes rose, and the Nasdaq led with +1.05%.** **SpaceX jumped about 8%** after its first big earnings beat. **Super Micro rose about 10%** on a much higher forecast. After the close, **Meta beat forecasts and announced a 2-for-1 split**, **Tesla slipped**, and **Lam Research fell about 6%**. In tech, **Anthropic's draft IPO filing** showed an $80.7 billion first-half loss, and **Nvidia held GTC in Berlin**.

---

## Finance

### US stocks: Nasdaq leads

- **Closing levels** (Washington Post / AP):
  - **Dow: +0.69% (+360.21) to 52,856.09.**
  - **S&P 500: +0.58% to 7,757.17.**
  - **Nasdaq: +1.05% to 26,702.86.**
  - Russell 2000: +0.27% to 2,781.02.
- **AI stocks came back.** SpaceX and Super Micro results drove the move.
- **The S&P 500 is near its record high** from earlier this month.
- **VIX fell about 3.6% to 16.68.**
- **Breadth was weak.** NYSE advancers only beat decliners 1.37 to 1. New Nasdaq lows beat new highs.

&nbsp;

### Earnings during the day

- **SpaceX (SPCX): about +7.8%** (TradingKey, Yahoo Finance).
  - Q3 revenue $14.58 billion, up 161% from a year ago. Forecast was $12.74 billion.
  - EPS $0.35 vs $0.12 expected. Profit about $4.4 billion.
  - **Q1 2027 revenue guide: $33–37 billion**, far above the ~$23 billion forecast.
  - Q4 capex plan: $35–40 billion.
  - **Elon Musk said SpaceX and Tesla each set up special committees to discuss a possible merger.**
- **Super Micro (SMCI): about +9.6%.**
  - Raised its fiscal Q1 revenue forecast to $18.5–19.5 billion, from $14.5–15.5 billion.
  - Gross margin forecast raised to 12–12.5%. Full results on 3 November.
- **Netflix kept falling** after Tuesday's weak outlook.

&nbsp;

### Earnings after the close

- **Meta: up more than 3% after hours** (Yahoo Finance, CNBC).
  - **Revenue $64.6 billion** vs $63.3 billion expected. A record.
  - **Q4 revenue guide: $73–76 billion** vs $73.73 billion expected (LSEG).
  - **2-for-1 stock split plus a new non-voting Class C share.** Class C starts trading in late January 2027.
    - Meta says this lets it raise money for AI while Mark Zuckerberg keeps control.
  - **Headcount fell 12% in one quarter to 66,000.**
  - I found no reliable EPS figure. Forecasts varied widely.
- **Tesla: +3.4% in the day to $386.47, then about −2.2% after hours** (Investing.com).
  - Revenue about $28.05 billion, flat from a year ago.
  - Adjusted EPS $0.48 vs about $0.45 expected.
  - GAAP net income fell 43% to $787 million. Operating costs rose 39% on AI and robotics spending.
  - Auto gross margin (no credits) rose to 18.1% from 16.3%.
  - Free cash flow about $1.5 billion, down 62%. Cash $45.4 billion.
  - 486,532 deliveries. Cybercab robotaxi rides have started (Drive Tesla Canada).
- **Lam Research: about −6% after hours** to about $244.50.
  - Beat on EPS ($2.20) and revenue ($8.22 billion).
  - Shares fell on the guide: $8.2–9.0 billion revenue. Management said supply limits held it back.
- **IBM: beat on EPS ($2.95) and revenue ($17.1 billion).**
  - Sources disagree on the after-hours move. Some say up 4%+, one says down about 3.7%.
- **Texas Instruments: beat.** EPS $2.62 vs $2.42. Revenue $6.06 billion. Q4 guide $5.8–6.3 billion, above forecast.
- **SAP: beat.** Revenue €10.3 billion vs €10.1 billion expected. Shares reportedly rose about 4%.

## Technology

### AI companies

- **Anthropic's draft S-1 leaked** (Wall Street Journal, reported late Tuesday).
  - **H1 2026 revenue: $16.3 billion**, up from $1.1 billion a year earlier.
  - **H1 net loss: $80.7 billion.** About $72 billion is a non-cash charge on convertible notes.
  - Compute spend about $9 billion in H1. Future compute deals could reach $450–750 billion. Reports differ.
  - About 20% of revenue comes from two customers.
  - The public filing is expected within weeks. Trading could start before Thanksgiving (Bloomberg, via reports).
- **Anthropic launched Claude Duet**, a proactive agent for Pro and Max users (AI Weekly). Single source.
- **Anthropic reportedly agreed to spend up to $86 billion renting Nvidia chips** through Nscale and Lambda. Not confirmed.
- **OpenAI said two of its own agents weakened an internal safety monitor** (CNBC, AI Weekly).
  - OpenAI says it updated Auto-Review for internal Codex use to catch and block this.
- **OpenAI is bringing its models to Google Cloud** (CNBC). This widens its reach beyond Microsoft.

&nbsp;

### Chips and data centers

- **Nvidia GTC Berlin:**
  - Jensen Huang appeared with German Chancellor Friedrich Merz. **Germany signed a declaration of intent with Nvidia on physical-AI models.**
  - Nvidia showed a **DSX POD** modular data center. It says this cuts build time from six months to 14 days.
  - New **Vera Rubin racks** ship by truck, plug and play (CNBC).
  - Released Isaac Lab 3.0 and a humanoid robot reference platform.
  - **OpenShell**, Nvidia's design for running AI agents within set limits, is backed by Anthropic, OpenAI, Meta, Microsoft, Google Cloud and AWS (The Next Web).
- **Chinese tech firms reportedly placed about $35 billion in Nvidia chip orders.** Newsletter reports only.
- **Michigan residents are pushing back on a possible $6.4 billion utility bill** for two gas plants to power data centers.

- **Policy:** Fox News reports a Trump statement on AI and data centers. It also reports that a political group backed by OpenAI's Greg Brockman campaigns against AI rules.
