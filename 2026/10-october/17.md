# Daily News — 17 October 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Saturday, 17 October 2026. **US markets were closed.** The finance section starts with Friday's close and the week's results. **The Nasdaq lost about 4.5% this week**, its worst week in months, as money left AI stocks. **Oil rose above $100.** On Saturday, **AI was the main topic at the IMF–World Bank meetings in Bangkok**, **Anthropic signed its first Canadian data center lease**, and **DeepSeek released a new open model**.

### AI stocks under pressure

- **CoreWeave fell 12% on Friday**, its second big drop in three days, on debt worries.
  - It fell nearly 25% for the week.
- **Meta fell nearly 4%, Tesla 2% and Nvidia 1.5%** on Friday.

- **Jefferies warned that AI earnings growth will slow** (note shared on 17 October):

### IMF–World Bank meetings in Bangkok

- **The meetings run 12–18 October.** It is the first time Bangkok has hosted them since 1991.
- **More than 15,000 people from 191 countries attended**, per one report.
- **AI is the main theme.** IMF chief Kristalina Georgieva said AI investment is helping keep global growth near 3%.
- **Anthropic co-founder Jack Clark spoke on 17 October.**
  - He shared an internal scenario where AI lifts US output to about $44 trillion by 2030. That is about 32% above a no-AI path.
  - He asked for international rules, oversight that can be checked, and required "kill switches".

## Technology

### Anthropic

- **Anthropic signed a 20-year lease for its first Canadian data center**, with EverWind Fuels at Point Tupper, Nova Scotia.
  - Phase one: 95 MW, planned for late 2027.
  - Growing to 420 MW by 2028.
  - EverWind plans over C$10 billion of total investment.
- **Research: Claude redesigned quantum circuits for eight tasks**, mostly on its own.
  - One version uses 12% more qubits but 17 times fewer gates.
- **Researchers say Claude found where NIST's elliptic-curve "seeds" came from in under 30 minutes.** They trace them to a C library random number generator. This is a single-source report.
- **Bank of America reportedly hired 1,000 contractors** to fix flaws found by Anthropic's Mythos model (CNBC, via a roundup).

&nbsp;

### AI models and research

- **DeepSeek released V4.1-Flash-1017** on Hugging Face under the MIT license.
  - 552 billion total parameters. About 8 billion active on input and 16 billion on output.
  - Context window: 1 million tokens.
  - Reported scores: 77.0 on DeepSWE v1.1, 42.6 on HLE.
- **Google's Gemini 4 Argon is now available worldwide** through the Gemini API and AI Studio. It targets long-running agent tasks.
- **Apollo Research: Z.ai's open GLM-5.3 model reasoned about its outputs becoming training data** for future models.
  - This happened in 10 of 6,452 graded test runs.
- **TheoPhysEval study:** top AI models found checked counterexamples to a 1967 fusion physics conjecture. All 50 problems cost $116.28 in total.

&nbsp;

### AI industry and policy

- **Character.AI filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy**, with Disney reported as lead bidder for its assets at about $20 million (Reuters, via a roundup). Not yet confirmed by other sources.
- **Meta removed a clause from its Commercial Terms** that let it train AI on content from business-linked websites. The new terms still start on 30 October.
- **US court rules committee heard that AI deepfakes may become impossible to detect.** A March survey found 15 of 931 judges had faced a deepfake challenge.
- **OpenAI hired Graham Seamans**, a former xAI sales leader, for its cybersecurity team.
- **Zen Technologies will buy 51.98% of Cogknit Semantics** for ₹135.15 crore (about $14.2 million). Cogknit makes AI battlefield systems.

### Security

- **OpenCode had a breach.** Stolen API keys affected 1.4% of Console users.
  - It is working with Google, OpenAI and Anthropic.
  - It asks affected users to revoke their keys.
- **Reminder: CISA's deadline to patch the PAN-OS zero-day (CVE-2026-0322) was 17 October.**

### Hardware, robots and other tech

- **DeepX began mass production of its DX-M1 AI chip** on Samsung's 5nm process. It reports 77 orders from more than 10 countries.
- **Nvidia says the RTX 5090 is not discontinued** and will keep shipping.
- **Pudu Robotics showed the D7 semi-humanoid robot** (23 joints, 20 kg payload) and the D5-W wheel-leg robot.

- **Apple named Scott Tucker, from Meta, to lead its global litigation.**
