## Technology

### Anthropic

- **Anthropic gave US lawmakers a draft AI safety bill** (Reuters, citing Politico).
  - Officials could block a frontier model only for "imminent and significant" risks.
  - The bar: a likely risk of over $1 billion in damage, or death or serious injury to more than 50 people.
  - It covers bioweapons, cyberattacks and models escaping developer control.
  - It would set up independent evaluators registered with the Commerce Department.
  - It is a proposal only. It is not law.
- **Survey: "The AI Expectations Gap."**
  - Over half of 1,334 Anthropic staff expect AI to do at least 80% of their work by 2030.
  - About 5% of US workers said the same.
  - Average estimate: 75% for Anthropic staff, 28% for US workers.
- **Claude beat the best published methods on eight quantum circuit redesign tasks** (reported again this weekend).

&nbsp;

### Open models

- **Meta released Muse Spark Open** (15 October, still a top story this weekend).
  - 816 billion total parameters, 42 billion active. 1 million token context.
  - Open weights on Hugging Face. The license has some commercial limits.
  - **Built with Apple to run on two M5 Ultra Mac Studios** using Apple's MLX. About 25 tokens per second in one Apple test.
  - Meta calls it the most capable US open-weight model so far. One review says it trails rivals on coding.
- **StepFun released Step 5 Preview.**
  - 600 billion parameters, 27 billion active. 1 million token context.
  - Needs four B300 GPUs to run in FP8.
- **DeepSeek V4.1-Flash-1017** (released 17 October):
  - Beats the earlier V4.1-Flash by 2.0 to 11.1 points on 11 tests.
  - Output up to 384K tokens.
  - **DeepSeek also opened its Search and Fetch APIs to all developers.**

### Policy

- **China plans to limit or ban exports of 40 technologies** (AP, draft published 16 October).
  - 23 are AI items, from large models to agents.
  - Also chip design for some AI and memory chips, and solar tech.
  - Models with 2 trillion or more parameters could face limits.
  - It is a draft. Final list and dates are not set.
- **California: Governor Newsom reportedly signed an order** for independent checks on AI models and "kill switches" for rogue AI agents. He vetoed a similar bill in 2024. Single-source report (newsletter citing Al Jazeera).
- **Utah approved a pilot for an AI acne app that can write prescriptions.**
  - Nolla Health, $4.99 a month. Doctors review the first 100 prescriptions.

### AI agents and finance

- **Schwab and JPMorgan block unauthorized AI agents** from moving money or reading customer data.
  - Schwab says it blocks dozens of attempts each week.
  - A federal bill on AI agent liability is stuck in committee.
- **OpenAI reportedly disclosed four new incidents with its AI agents**, 19 in total. Single-source report.
- **Replit is reportedly switching its default model provider to OpenAI.**
