# Daily News — 15 October 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Thursday, 15 October 2026. **Money moved out of AI stocks and into older, defensive names.** The **Dow rose about 1.1%**, while the **Nasdaq was flat** and the **chip index fell again**. **September wholesale prices jumped 1.3%**, the most since 2022, but **Treasury yields fell**. In Asia, **South Korea's Kospi crashed 7.6%**. In tech, **TSMC posted record profit**, **Anthropic reported a $16.8 billion quarter**, and **eight US states sued OpenAI**.

### Asia: Kospi crashes on AI fears

- **South Korea's Kospi fell 7.57% to 5,554.22**, its lowest close since 7 April.
  - It fell as much as 9.29% during the day. A circuit breaker paused trading for 20 minutes.
  - It is now about 21% below its September high, a bear market (Bloomberg).
- **Samsung Electronics fell about 9.5%. SK Hynix fell about 11.1%.**
- **Japan's Nikkei fell about 5%.** Kioxia and SoftBank Group were among the biggest losers.
- **Taiwan's Taiex fell about 3%.**
- Causes: doubts that huge AI spending will pay off, worries about AI debt, and fears of US rate hikes.

## Technology

### TSMC: record quarter

- **Q3 net income: NT$763.1 billion (about $24 billion)**, up 68.7% year on year.
- **Q3 revenue: NT$1.49 trillion (US$46.72 billion)**, up 50.9% (41.2% in US dollars).
- **EPS: NT$29.43**, or US$4.60 per ADR.
- **Gross margin: 66.4%**, down 1.3 points from Q2, mainly due to the 2nm ramp.
- **2nm chips are now 8% of sales.**
- **Q4 guidance: $52.3–$54.3 billion** in revenue, with a gross margin of 65–67%.
- **2026 capital spending raised to $60–$64 billion.**
- Chairman C.C. Wei said AI demand is still higher than supply.
- TSMC is looking at more investment in Texas. It has discussed Elon Musk's proposed Terafab project. Nothing is final.

### Anthropic: $16.8 billion quarter and IPO talk

- **Anthropic told investors its Q3 revenue was $16.8 billion**, up about 45% from about $11.6 billion in Q2.
- It reported an adjusted profit and positive cash flow.
- **It is reportedly preparing an IPO as early as November**, at a valuation near $2 trillion.
- **Claude will add voting information** from Democracy Works before the November US midterm elections.

&nbsp;

### OpenAI: state lawsuits and FTC probe

- **Eight states sued OpenAI** on 14 October: Arizona, Arkansas, Idaho, Iowa, Kansas, Montana, Nebraska and West Virginia.
  - They claim ChatGPT was sold as safe for young people but could encourage self-harm and give dangerous advice.
  - They want fines, data deletion, age checks and limits on testing advanced models.
  - OpenAI says it has age-specific protections.
- **The FTC sent detailed demands for records to OpenAI and Anthropic** (Axios).
  - They cover safety, privacy, marketing claims and possible harm to consumers.
  - This is fact-finding, not a finding of wrongdoing.
- **OpenAI says a former employee took "highly confidential" device files to Apple** (9to5Mac).

### Security

- **Cisco Secure Email Gateway flaw (CVE-2026-76519), rated 9.8.**
  - An attacker with no login could send a crafted attachment and run commands as root.
  - Cisco says an update is required. No workaround fully fixes it.
- **Palo Alto Networks PAN-OS zero-day (CVE-2026-0322) is being exploited.**
  - It affects the firewall management interface. The attacker needs a login and network access.
  - CISA set a federal patch deadline of 17 October.
