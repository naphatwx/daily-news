# Daily News — 7 October 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Wednesday, 7 October 2026. **Stocks pulled back from records** as **a global bond selloff pushed Treasury yields to their highest levels since 2002**. **The Dow fell 341 points**, and **small caps dropped 1.3%**. **Oil climbed back toward $100**, while **gold and bitcoin fell**. In tech, **OpenAI rolled out GPT-6 and "Intelligent UI" in ChatGPT**, **Microsoft held its Windows and Surface event focused on local AI PCs**, and **SpaceX is seeking about $40 billion in debt to buy Nvidia chips**.

---

## Finance

### Wall Street: a pullback from records

- **S&P 500 -0.22% to 7,801.77.**
- **Nasdaq -0.22% to 27,538.69.**
- **Dow -0.66% (-341 points) to 51,179.87.**
- **Russell 2000 -1.31% to 2,793.36.**
- **The S&P 500 and the Dow snapped 5-day winning streaks.**
- Main driver: **rising bond yields**, plus **oil moving back toward $100 a barrel**.
  - Stocks fell harder early, then **cut losses as yields eased** after a **strong 10-year Treasury auction**.
- **FOMC minutes** from the September meeting came out at **2 p.m. ET**.
  - Investors looked for clues on **whether the Fed will raise rates again later this month**.

&nbsp;

### Bond selloff: highest yields since 2002

- **10-year Treasury yield hit as high as 5.36%**; **30-year hit 5.73%** — both the **highest since 2002**.
  - The 10-year **closed near 5.3%**, after easing back from about **5.31% to 5.27%** following the auction.
- The selloff was **global**:
  - **French and Italian bond yields rose sharply.**
  - **The UK 30-year gilt yield hit its highest level since 1998.**
- Reasons cited: **heavy government debt** and **huge borrowing needs tied to the AI boom**.

&nbsp;

### Movers

- **Caterpillar fell about 5.7%** — the worst Dow stock, as **higher yields hit industrial and cyclical names**.
- **Allegion fell about 6%**; **EMCOR fell 4.8%**, giving back much of Tuesday's gain.
- **Mohawk Industries fell 5.5%** after **RBC downgraded it to Underperform** (target cut to **$112** from **$130**).
- **Skydance (SKYD) fell about 7.7%** on its **second day of trading**.
- **Spotify rose about 5%**; **Skyworks rose about 3.4%** as its **$22 billion merger with Qorvo** moves toward closing.
- **Bank stocks were weak** in the U.S. and Europe (**BofA, Citi, JPMorgan, Goldman, Deutsche Bank, SocGen**) ahead of **bank earnings next week**.

&nbsp;

### Deals still in focus

- **Option Care Health** stayed in focus after **McKesson and CD&R agreed to take it private for about $5.8 billion**.
  - **$32.05 per share**, a **37.1% premium**; **CD&R gets about 51%**, **McKesson about 49%** (about **$1.4 billion**).
  - Expected to close in **the first half of 2027**.
- **Lambda is raising up to $4 billion** at a **$14.5 billion pre-money valuation**, led by **Blackstone and Coatue** (per WSJ).
  - Planned as its **final private round before a 2027 IPO**.
  - Backlog reportedly grew to **$50 billion**, much of it from a **$35 billion Anthropic commitment**.

&nbsp;

### Oil, gold and crypto

- **Brent rose about 1% to above $101** after **Iran stepped up tanker attacks in the Strait of Hormuz** and **Houthi strikes hit Saudi targets**.
  - **WTI traded around $90.**
  - **A G7 release of 100 million barrels of reserves** helped cap the move.
- **Gold fell about 0.7–2%**, to roughly **$4,100–4,160**, hurt by **high yields and a stronger dollar**.
- **Bitcoin fell about 1.6% to about $84,000.**

## Technology

### OpenAI rolls out GPT-6 and "Intelligent UI"

- **OpenAI began rolling out GPT-6 with "Intelligent UI" in ChatGPT.**
  - **Paid users (Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise) got it first**, running on **GPT-6 Sol**.
  - **Free and Go users get it from 8 October**, running on **GPT-6 Luna**.
- What Intelligent UI does:
  - ChatGPT can answer with **interactive interfaces** — charts, buttons, forms, maps, calculators and small games.
  - The UI **streams in while the model writes**, so users do not wait for the full answer.
- **GPT-6 Instant starts answering web-search questions 44% faster** than GPT-5.6 Instant, says OpenAI.
- The update covers **only the Chat tab**; **Work and Codex models do not change**.
- ChatGPT has **more than 1.2 billion weekly users**.

&nbsp;

### Microsoft Windows and Surface event

- **Microsoft held its Windows and Surface event in San Francisco**, focused on **local AI on the PC**.
  - Speakers included **Satya Nadella** and **Pavan Davuluri**; **Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang** was also expected.
- New hardware:
  - **Surface Laptop Ultra** — **15-inch touchscreen**, **up to 128 GB of unified memory**.
  - **Surface RTX Spark Dev Box.**
- **RTX Spark laptops from Lenovo, Asus, Dell and MSI** were also shown.
  - **Preorders opened on 7 October**; **shipping starts Friday, 16 October**.
- Also announced: **a major Copilot overhaul** and **new developer safeguards for running AI agents**.
- **No Windows 12** was announced.

&nbsp;

### AI infrastructure money

- **SpaceX is seeking about $40 billion in debt to buy Nvidia AI chips** (Bloomberg, FT).
  - About **$10 billion in bank loans** plus **$30 billion in investment-grade bonds**.
  - **Apollo** is expected to lead; **Pimco** is among possible investors.
  - Talks are **early** and could fall apart; financing is expected to close in **2027**.
- **Zuckerberg's Biohub** brought in **Google, Meta and the U.S. government** for a **$1.8 billion push to generate biology data to train AI**.
- **Morgan Stanley estimates AI infrastructure will need about $1.5 trillion of outside financing by 2028.**

&nbsp;

### Policy and other tech news

- **The U.S. Senate blocked a bill** that would have **shifted extra grid costs onto data centers**.
- **Finland is investigating forest clearing** tied to a **Google data-center project**.
- **California set fines for robotaxis that block emergency responders.**
- **Google paused part of an open-source bug bounty program** after **AI-generated reports overwhelmed human reviewers**.
- **SAP agreed to buy TechWolf**, a work-intelligence platform.
- **Lenovo announced its CES 2027 keynote will be held at the Sphere** in Las Vegas.
