# Daily News — 29 October 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Thursday, 29 October 2026. **Alphabet had its worst day ever, down about 12%.** **The Nasdaq fell 1.4%.** **September PCE inflation rose to 3.6%.** **The ECB held at 2.50%, and Lagarde said she will leave early.** **Amazon missed estimates.** **Samsung posted a record profit.** **Google launched Gemini 4 Carbon.**

## Finance

### Inflation and central banks

- **September PCE inflation rose** (Commerce Department, via Fox Business and Stock Titan):
  - Headline PCE: +0.5% in the month, 3.6% over the year (3.4% in August).
  - Core PCE: 3.1% over the year (3.0% in August).
  - Both came in slightly above expectations. Energy prices drove much of the rise.
  - The data keeps a December Fed hike in play. The Fed held at 3.75%–4.00% the day before.
- **The ECB kept rates unchanged** (CNBC, Euronews):
  - Deposit rate: 2.50%. Main refinancing rate: 2.65%. Marginal lending rate: 2.90%.
  - This is a pause after a 0.25-point hike in September.
  - Euro area inflation is about 3.8%. The ECB warned energy prices add risk to inflation and growth.
  - Markets price about a 67% chance of a December hike.
  - Next meeting: 17 December 2026.
- **Christine Lagarde will leave the ECB early.**
  - She steps down on 7 February 2027, about nine months before her term ends.

### Stocks

- **US stocks fell, led by Alphabet** (Yahoo Finance, TheStreet):
  - S&P 500: −0.30% (−23.59) to 7,720.39. This ended a three-day winning streak.
  - Nasdaq Composite: −1.38% to 26,184.72.
  - Dow: −0.25% (−132.54) to 52,342.56.
  - Most stocks still rose. The equal-weight S&P 500 ETF (RSP) gained 1.20%.
- **Alphabet fell about 12%**, its worst day ever.
  - Its Q3 results missed. Free cash flow was negative, about −$9.1 billion.
- **Microsoft rose about 3%** after an Azure-led beat. Some reports say it gave back part of the gain during the day.
- **Meta rose about 3%** to about $629 (24/7 Wall St.).
- **Amazon earnings (Q3, after the close):**
  - Revenue: $201.9 billion, +12%. Analysts expected about $202.2 billion.
  - EPS: $1.89. Analysts expected about $1.97.
  - AWS revenue: $46 billion, +39% (37% in Q2).
  - Q4 revenue forecast: $240–247 billion.
  - Investors focused on about $220 billion in planned capex.
  - Shares closed down 4.25% at $243.79 before the report, then fell further after hours (Motley Fool).
- **Oracle rose about 11%** after forecasting $225 billion of revenue in fiscal 2030 (daily digest report).
- **Fortinet fell about 25% after hours.** Revenue grew 20%, but product revenue growth slowed (daily digest report).
- **Samsung posted a record quarterly profit:**
  - Operating profit: about KRW 107.4 trillion (about $80.5 billion). Reports say it is the first tech firm to top KRW 100 trillion in a quarter.
  - Revenue: about KRW 195.3 trillion.
  - Memory chips made about 99% of the profit. The mobile business lost money.
  - Profit was slightly below forecasts. Shares fell about 6% in Seoul.

## Technology

### AI models and labs

- **Google launched Gemini 4 Carbon** (Google blog, Help Net Security).
  - Google calls it its strongest model, built for cyber defense and long coding tasks.
  - It can work on tasks for days. One report says it finds 88.2% of test software flaws.
  - First access goes only to trusted cyber defenders in Google's Fairwind program.
  - Price: $2 per million input tokens, $10 per million output tokens.
  - All benchmark numbers are Google's own. No outside tests yet.
- **Anthropic reportedly has an internal model that chains flaws into working exploits** (single digest report; not confirmed).
- **OpenAI reportedly set up an election monitoring center** before the US midterms (digest report).

### Security

- **Cisco SD-WAN flaw used to plant key-stealing malware** (Cisco Talos, The Hacker News).
  - Flaw: CVE-2026-20127, CVSS 10.0. First disclosed in February 2026.
  - Malware name: KeyDrift. It targets encryption keys.
  - The activity is tied to Volt Typhoon's "Silent Train" relay network.
- **Second Cisco firewall VPN zero-day since August** (SecurityWeek).
  - Flaw: CVE-2026-76418 in Secure Firewall ASA/FTD, CVSS 8.6.
  - A crafted VPN request can force the device to reload (denial of service).
  - No data theft or remote code execution reported.
- **CISA added a VMware ESXi logging flaw to its exploited list.**
  - Flaw: CVE-2026-41709. Low severity on paper.
  - CrowdStrike says the China-linked group Warp Panda used it as a zero-day against US groups, including law firms.
  - Federal agencies must patch within two weeks.
