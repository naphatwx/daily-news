# Daily News — 13 October 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Tuesday, 13 October 2026. **US stocks ended slightly lower** on a busy day of **big bank earnings**. **Goldman Sachs beat on a 72% jump in equities trading**, while **JPMorgan missed its EPS forecast** for the first time in nearly three years. **Memory chip stocks fell again** on fears of Chinese competition. In tech, **Apple launched its HomeView smart home hub**, **Character.AI filed for bankruptcy**, and **Meta released its Muse Wonder models**.

---

## Finance

### Stocks: small losses on bank earnings day

- Closing levels (AP, CNBC):
  - **Dow: 51,670.10**, down 180.45 points (−0.35%).
  - **S&P 500: 7,805.45**, down 1.79 points (−0.02%). It is just below last week's record high.
  - **Nasdaq: 27,204.38**, down 41.52 points (−0.15%).
  - Some sources give slightly different numbers. Treat them as close, not final.
- **Big Dow losers:** Johnson & Johnson (about −6%) and Caterpillar (about −3.5%).
- **Applied Materials fell more than 6.5%**, the worst stock in the S&P 500 and Nasdaq 100.
- **Meta rose about 2.5%**, after a gain of nearly 3.5% on Monday.
- **Apple hit an intraday record** and closed up 0.1%.
- **Domino's Pizza fell nearly 5%.**

&nbsp;

### Big banks: strong profits, mixed stock moves

| Bank | Q3 profit | EPS | vs forecast | Stock |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| JPMorgan | $15.3B | $5.57 | Missed ($5.90–$5.93 expected) | Up early, closed lower |
| Goldman Sachs | $5.29B | $17.01 | Beat | Up early, closed lower |
| Citigroup | About $5.2B | — | Beat | About −5.5%, worst of the group |
| Wells Fargo | $5.95B | $1.87 | Beat | Lower |

- **JPMorgan's fixed-income trading revenue fell**, its first drop in more than two years.
- **Goldman's equities revenue jumped 72% to $6.44 billion.** Revenue was $18.68 billion.
- **Citi's bond desk had its best third quarter in at least a decade** ($4.4 billion). Sources disagree on its profit growth (17% or 40%).
- **All four bank stocks closed lower**, even after early gains for JPMorgan and Goldman.
- **Jamie Dimon warned about "sticky inflation"**, high asset prices and rising interest rates.

&nbsp;

### Memory chips sell off again

- **Memory stocks fell for a second day.** SK Hynix, Micron, Sandisk, Seagate and Western Digital all dropped.
  - The Roundhill Memory ETF (DRAM) fell about 3.5%.
- **In Asia, Korea's Kospi fell about 6.25%.** Samsung dropped 7.4% and SK Hynix 10%.
- Causes:
  - **China's CXMT claimed a breakthrough** in a new DRAM design.
  - **Broadcom CEO Hock Tan said many AI investors "have no clue"** what they are buying.

&nbsp;

### Oil, yields, dollar, gold and bitcoin

- **Oil was mixed.**
  - **WTI rose 0.8% to about $94.45.**
  - **Brent fell 0.6% to about $106.**
  - Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia pushed prices up. More diesel supply and a US easing of Russian energy limits pushed them down.
- **The 10-year Treasury yield fell about 2 basis points to just above 5.22%.**
  - Last Thursday it neared 5.37%, the highest in more than 24 years.
  - Bond markets were closed Monday for Columbus Day.
- **The dollar fell against all seven major currencies** after comments from NY Fed President John Williams. The dollar index fell 0.4% to about 102.19.
- **Gold slipped to about $4,150–$4,190.**
- **Bitcoin traded near $83,100.**

&nbsp;

### Policy and risk warnings

- **The IMF's Global Financial Stability Report named an AI-driven market correction** as one of three main near-term risks.
  - The largest US tech firms now make up over 35% of the S&P 500, up from about 12% ten years ago.
  - The other risks: higher government borrowing costs and a major cyberattack.
- **September CPI comes Wednesday.** PPI follows on Thursday.

## Technology

### Apple "Welcome Home" event

- **HomeView:** Apple's first smart home hub. $299 (tabletop or wall mount). Ships 13 November.
- **Apple TV 4K (2026):** $299, which is $100 more than before. Ships 13 November.
- **HomePod mini 2:** $149, with the S10 chip. Available 30 October.
  - New Siri AI comes later in a software update.
- **Apple Home Collection** with LG and Schneider: cameras ($200–$400), a doorbell ($250), a smart lock ($350), thermostats ($200) and sensors ($40).
  - Some arrive in December, others in early 2027.

&nbsp;

### AI companies and models

- **Character.AI filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy** (Reuters, via TechStartups).
  - **It agreed to sell some assets to Disney for $20 million**, with Disney as the first bidder.
  - Assets and liabilities are listed at $50–$100 million.
  - I could not confirm this from a second source.
- **Meta released Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full** (announced 12 October).
  - Muse Wonder is on the Meta Model API and Muse Code. The Full model is only for Muse Code subscribers.
  - Benchmarks are Meta's own. No independent tests yet.
- **Anthropic warned small AI models could soon copy themselves onto home devices** like TVs, cameras and thermostats.
  - It said this may be months away. It is a future risk, not a current attack.
- **Tencent reportedly leased about 100,000 AI chips from Oracle** in Southeast Asia, for about $7 billion over five years. Neither company confirmed it.

&nbsp;

### AI infrastructure

- **Scaleway plans an AMD MI355X AI supercomputer in Sweden**, its first outside France.
- **A Nevada judge stalled the Townsite data center near Boulder City** over an old environmental review.

&nbsp;

### Security

- **GitLab fixed a maximum-severity flaw (CVE-2026-97229)** that could allow remote code execution without login.
  - Fixed in 19.2.8, 19.3.4 and 19.4.2. GitLab.com is already patched.
- **Ahold Delhaize took parts of its network offline** after a cyberattack. Stores stayed open.
- **Ledger found a hacked hardware wallet** sold by a Malaysian reseller. It told affected users to move funds to a new wallet.
- **South Korea's president said AI was used to hack 7 banks**, exposing data of 68,000 people.

&nbsp;

### Other tech news

- **Google launched the Fitbit Edge** at $179.99. It goes on sale 27 October.
- **WindBorne won a US Air Force contract worth up to $49 million** for weather balloons.
- **EnergyX signed a 10-year lithium deal with Trafigura**, worth over $2 billion.
