# Daily News — 31 October 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Saturday, 31 October 2026. US markets were closed, so the market numbers are from Friday, 30 October, as reported on 31 October. **Intel fell 29%, its worst day since 1974.** **The Nasdaq fell 2.9% and is near a correction.** **The $6.3 billion Binance hack is still shaking crypto.** **OpenAI cut its funding target by $10 billion.** **Bitcoin traded near $77,000.**

---

## Finance

### Stocks (Friday close, 30 October)

- **US stocks fell hard, led by tech** (Reuters via The Star, Yahoo Finance):
  - S&P 500: −1.82% to 7,580.07.
  - Nasdaq Composite: −2.86% to 25,437.00. Its biggest drop since June.
  - Dow: −1.09% (−572.25) to 51,770.31.
  - The Nasdaq is 7.8% below its closing high, after its worst month since March 2025.
  - The Nasdaq 100 fell 3.5%, close to a correction.
- **Weekly view** (Seeking Alpha):
  - The S&P 500 fell about 0.6% for the week. This is its third down week in a row.
  - It is still only 3.1% below its all-time high.
  - Money moved to small caps, REITs, utilities, value and oil stocks. Tech and communication stocks lagged.
- **Intel fell 29% to about $65** (CNBC):
  - Its worst day since 1974. The stock had been up about 150% this year.
  - Q3 EPS beat. Revenue was slightly below forecasts.
  - Data center and AI revenue: $7.3 billion, up nearly 80%.
  - Q4 revenue guidance was below forecasts. Reasons: weak PC demand from high memory prices, server supply problems, and a fab explosion in Israel earlier in October.
  - Bank of America, Roth and Seaport cut the stock to neutral. New Street raised it to buy.
  - The US government's stake (433 million shares) lost about $11 billion in value in one day.
- **Other big moves** (Reuters via The Star):
  - Reddit: −29%.
  - Sandisk: −13%.
  - Jefferies: −6%. Goldman Sachs: −4%.
  - Microsoft, Meta, Amazon and Alphabet: −1% to −2%.
- **S&P Global cut Meta's outlook to negative** from stable. It cited high capex and negative free cash flow (aggregator reports).

### Commodities

- **Oil rose about 2% on Friday** (FinanceFeeds, Reuters):
  - Brent January settled at $113.43. The expiring Brent December contract traded near $116.
  - WTI traded near $103.
  - Iran's Revolutionary Guards claimed three tankers hit mines near Hormuz. US CENTCOM rejected this.
  - Brent is on track for a fourth monthly gain in a row.
- **Precious metals fell** (Reuters via The Star):
  - Gold: −3.5%, about $3,899. Down about 10% for 2026.
  - Silver: −6%. Down about 31% for 2026.

### Crypto

- **Binance hack: $6.3 billion stolen, the largest crypto theft on record** (CNBC, NPR, Bloomberg):
  - TRM Labs and Chainalysis link it to North Korea. TRM names the group TraderTraitor.
  - Funds moved across 10 blockchains, mostly within about 20 minutes.
  - About $3 billion is frozen. Stablecoin issuers (Tether, Circle) can freeze their tokens. Bitcoin and Ether cannot be frozen.
  - Binance paused deposits and withdrawals. It says it will cover all losses.
  - Analysts say the key sign to watch is when withdrawals restart.
- **Bitcoin traded near $77,000–$77,600 on 31 October** (CryptoTicker, Coin Gabbar):
  - It slipped below $78,000 on Friday, down about 3% that day.
  - It is about 38% below its record high of $126,080.
- **Spot Bitcoin ETFs had large outflows on Friday.**
  - Reported figures differ: $409.5 million to $643.2 million.
  - Fidelity's FBTC alone lost about $201 million.
- **October review** (24/7 Wall St.):
  - The crypto market lost about $360 billion in October, to about $2.63 trillion.
  - Bitcoin fell about 7.6% in October. Monero did best among the top 10 coins.
- **Next Technology Holding approved a plan to sell or pledge about 5,833 BTC**, citing quantum computing risk. It has not sold yet.
- **The Bitcoin white paper turned 18** on 31 October.

### Deals and funding

- **OpenAI cut its funding target by $10 billion** (Bloomberg, citing the FT):
  - New target: about $20 billion at a $1.25 trillion valuation.
  - Last month's plan: $30 billion at $1.4 trillion.
  - Some investors cut their commitments after the AI stock selloff.
  - Expected investors include MGX (UAE), SoftBank, Sequoia, Coatue, Founders Fund and Andreessen Horowitz.
  - Reports say Anthropic is preparing an IPO next month at a valuation of at least $1.5 trillion.
- **&Truth came out of stealth with a $70 million seed round** to formally verify AI-written code (aggregator report).
