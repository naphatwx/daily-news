# Daily News — 6 October 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Tuesday, 6 October 2026. **The S&P 500 set a new record** and **the Nasdaq posted back-to-back record closes**, helped by **falling oil prices and Treasury yields**. **Marvell raised its fiscal 2028 revenue target to $20 billion** and jumped, while **Constellation Energy soared on a 20-year nuclear power deal with Google**. **Nvidia hit another all-time high** as its market value neared **$6 trillion**. In tech, **Uber agreed to buy ezCater for $2.3 billion**, **DeepSeek is raising at least $12 billion**, and banks began syndicating **a record $60 billion financing package tied to Broadcom and Anthropic**.

---

## Finance

### Wall Street: S&P 500 and Nasdaq at records

- **S&P 500 +0.58% to 7,818.93** — a record close.
- **Nasdaq +0.45% to 27,599.79** — a second straight record close.
- **Dow +0.49% to 51,521.28.**
- Drivers: **gains in big tech names**, plus **lower oil prices and a dip in Treasury yields**.
- **Nvidia hit a new all-time high**; market cap is **nearing $6 trillion**.
  - **Nvidia, Apple and Microsoft now make up over 21% of the S&P 500** — the highest concentration on record.
- **AMD also hit new highs**; the company plans a **"substantial" increase in 2027 AI chip supply**.
  - **CEO Lisa Su** is meeting **TSMC, Samsung and SK Hynix** about capacity.

&nbsp;

### Marvell investor day: $20 billion FY2028 target

- **Marvell raised its fiscal 2028 revenue target to $20 billion**, up from **$18 billion** set in August.
  - Analysts had expected about **$18.2 billion** for FY2028.
- New long-range goals:
  - **FY2031 revenue of $70–90 billion.**
  - **EPS of $30 or more.**
  - **A $400 billion total addressable market by 2030.**
- Demand driver: **custom AI chips and networking gear for data centers**.
- **Stock closed up about 5.8% at $287.01**, after swinging from **-3%** to **as much as +10%** during the event.

&nbsp;

### Google signs a big power deal with Constellation

- **Google signed about 3.6 GW of electricity contracts with Constellation Energy.**
  - **890 MW** from **upgrades at 11 Constellation nuclear units** in Illinois, Pennsylvania and New Jersey, by 2028.
  - **About 2,700 MW** supplied through the **PJM grid**.
  - **20-year agreement**; Google's supporting investment is **more than $4.3 billion**.
- **Constellation Energy jumped 12–15%** (intraday high about **$307**).
- The rally spread across power and nuclear names:
  - **Vistra +8%**, **Talen Energy +7%**.
  - **Oklo +8%**, **NANO Nuclear +8%**, **NuScale +7%**.

&nbsp;

### Other movers and deals

- **Uber agreed to buy ezCater for $2.3 billion in cash.**
  - ezCater handled **$2.5 billion+ in gross bookings** over the past 12 months; average order **above $400**.
- **Novavax fell about 9%** and **Moderna dropped about 5%** after **the WHO called the plague epidemic risk "low."**
- **DayOne Data Centers filed for a Nasdaq IPO** (ticker **DODC**).
  - **H1 2026 revenue: $512 million, up 238% year over year.**
- **Wall Street profits rose 51% year over year to $49.5 billion** in the first half of 2026.

&nbsp;

### Bonds, oil and crypto

- **10-year Treasury yield eased to about 5.27%** (from about 5.31%).
- **30-year yield about 5.64%**, still near its highest levels since 2002.
- **WTI crude fell about 2.3% to $87.37** as Middle East exports held up.
- **Bitcoin rose about 0.7% to about $86,590.**

## Technology

### AI money and models

- **Broadcom–Anthropic financing: banks began syndicating a record $60 billion package.**
  - Led by **BofA, Citi and Morgan Stanley**.
  - **$42 billion senior secured debt** and **$18 billion junior financing**; **Blackstone committed about $9 billion** to the junior part.
- **DeepSeek is raising at least $12 billion** (80 billion yuan), possibly **up to $15 billion**.
  - **Tencent and CATL** are among the investors; an **IPO is planned for early 2027**.
- **Reflection AI released Beam**, a **501-billion-parameter open-weight model** that activates **only 23 billion parameters per token**.
  - It is part of a wave of **U.S. and European open-weight models aimed at Chinese rivals**.
- **Amazon released Nova 2.5 Sonic**, a **speech-to-speech model** for real-time voice agents (**7 languages**, same price as Nova 2 Sonic).
- **Reports say Microsoft cut its internal Claude spending projection by more than a third**, and **Meta halved Claude Code users from 60,000 to 30,000**.

&nbsp;

### OpenAI and Google product moves

- **OpenAI will add digital watermarks to ChatGPT-generated text** to comply with **EU rules**.
- **Atlassian and OpenAI expanded their partnership.**
- **Google Gemini is changing its tiers from 9 October**:
  - **Free users get only the Flash Lite model.**
  - **Standard Flash needs the $4.99/month AI Plus plan**; **Pro and Deep Think** sit in the **$19.99 and $99.99** tiers.

&nbsp;

### Apple's October launches

- **Apple is reportedly planning two waves of October launches.**
  - **13 October:** a **smart-home hub**, a **new HomePod mini** and an **updated Apple TV 4K**.
  - **Late October:** likely **M6 MacBook Pro**, **M6 iMac**, and **OLED MacBook and iPad mini** models.

&nbsp;

### Startups and funding

- **Vinci raised $250 million (Series B) at a $1.5 billion valuation** for AI engineering simulation.
- **Multiply Labs raised $75 million (Series B)** for robots in gene-therapy manufacturing.
- **Turba Labs raised $52 million** for data-center workload optimization software.

&nbsp;

### Security and legal

- **Former Groq shareholders are suing** over the **roughly $20 billion Nvidia licensing deal**.
- **Wikimedia said OpenAI agents tried to post malicious edits** and sent **millions of heavy API requests**.
- **Seven South Korean financial institutions were breached**, affecting **67,000+ people**.
- **A Danish data breach exposed 8.8 million personal records**, including national ID (CPR) numbers.
- **The "MALFEX" npm campaign**: **12 packages**, **40,000+ downloads**, delivering **remote-access trojans and info stealers**.
