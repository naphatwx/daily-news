# Daily News — 10 November 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Tuesday, 10 November 2026. **US October CPI came in hot: +0.4% for the month, core +0.3%.** **US stocks fell: the S&P 500 lost 0.38% and the Nasdaq lost 0.74%.** **The 10-year Treasury yield touched 5%.** **Nvidia fell about 5.5% as the CEO probe went on.** **Anthropic set its IPO price range at $70 to $78 and plans to start trading on 19 November.** **CoreWeave beat on revenue and raised its forecast.**

---

## Finance

### Markets

- **US indexes closed lower** (GV Wire/Reuters, StockTitan):
  - Dow: −0.30% (−157.86 points) to 52,231.89.
  - S&P 500: −0.38% to 7,520.93.
  - Nasdaq Composite: −0.74% to 25,065.87.
- **The drop ended a two-day recovery** for the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq.
- **Tech was the worst of the 11 S&P 500 sectors.** Nvidia was the main drag.

&nbsp;

### Inflation and the Fed

- **October CPI** (BLS, CNBC):
  - Headline: +0.4% month over month. Forecast was +0.3%.
  - Core: +0.3%. Forecast was +0.2%.
  - Energy: +0.7%, after +5.2% in September (CBS News).
  - Food and rent costs rose sharply (Yahoo Finance).
- **No year-over-year rate.** Last year's government shutdown left the BLS without October 2025 data.
- **Fed talk:**
  - Dallas Fed President Lorie Logan sees a need for at least two more quarter-point hikes.
  - Governor Waller had said a half-point hike should be considered if core CPI came in near 0.3% or higher.
- **Rate bets** (CME FedWatch, via CNBC): the chance of a half-point hike in December rose to about 14% from near zero. A quarter-point hike is about 86%.
- **Treasury yields:** the 10-year rose about 11 basis points to near 5% and crossed 5% during the day. The 2-year jumped 17 basis points to 4.74%.

&nbsp;

### Big movers

- **Losers:**
  - Nvidia: about −5.5% (Motley Fool, Morningstar).
  - Skydance: −7.8%.
  - GE Vernova: −7.1%.
  - First Solar also fell (TheStreet).
- **Gainers:**
  - Charter Communications: +10.6%.
  - Sea Limited: +8.1%.
  - Take-Two: about +6% to +8%, depending on the source.
  - Meta: more than +5%. JPMorgan added it to its Focus List.
  - AMD rose. A Motley Fool headline ties it to a plan to invest $2.5 billion in Anthropic.
- **After hours:** CoreWeave rose after its Q3 report (see Technology).

&nbsp;

### Oil, crypto and others

- **Oil fell again** on hopes of lower tension with Iran (CNBC):
  - WTI: −0.93% to $86.04.
  - Brent: about −1% to near $91.70 (Trading Economics). It dipped below $90 for the first time since August.
- **Bitcoin fell** to about $73,300, down roughly 2.6%. One report says it slipped below $73,000 for the first time since August.

&nbsp;

### Asia and Europe

- **Asia rallied:**
  - Kospi: +4.4% to 5,239.13. Chip stocks led. A buy-side sidecar (a short halt on program trading) was triggered in Seoul.
  - Nikkei 225: about +2.9% to 60,459.58. Trading Economics says +3.34% to 60,741.
- **Europe was mostly flat:**
  - Stoxx 600: +0.27% to 658.32. Oil and gas stocks fell for an eighth day.
  - FTSE 100: −0.29% to 10,751.59.

## Technology

### Anthropic IPO

- **Price range: $70 to $78 per share** (CNBC, citing sources and an updated SEC filing).
- **Size:** 825 million shares. That is about $64.35 billion at the top of the range.
- **Value:** up to about $1.4 trillion, fully diluted. Some outlets had talked about $2 trillion weeks ago (Fortune).
- **Dates:** pricing on 18 November. Trading on Nasdaq as "ANT" on 19 November.
- **Private placement:** seven strategic partners agreed to buy $26 billion of non-voting stock at the IPO price (Fortune).
  - Named buyers include Google, Nvidia, AMD and TSMC (Invezz).
  - FourWeekMBA says Nvidia and Google each take $10 billion.
  - TechStartups says the IPO and placement together could raise up to $90.4 billion.

&nbsp;

### Nvidia

- **Nvidia fell about 5.5%**, its second drop in a row.
- **The board hired law firm Wachtell Lipton** to lead the probe into CEO Jensen Huang (CNBC).
- **Next dates:** Q3 earnings on 17 November. A closed House briefing was requested for 18 November.

&nbsp;

### CoreWeave Q3

- **Revenue:** $3.66 billion, up 168% from a year ago. Analysts expected $3.55 billion.
- **Net loss:** $709 million. Adjusted net loss: $529 million.
- **2026 revenue forecast raised** to $13.0–$13.3 billion, from $12.4–$13.2 billion.
- **Backlog:** $132.5 billion.
- **Debt:** almost $47 billion. The CFO said the company does not plan more parent-level unsecured debt at current yields.
- **Stock:** up after hours. It had lost over 40% in the past month.

&nbsp;

### Other AI and tech news

- **Google** made its Gemini 4 Carbon model widely available. It was first limited to cybersecurity firms (Engadget).
- **Google's November Pixel Drop** added Rambler dictation and other AI features to Pixel 10 phones.
- **Apple** launched its first paid Apple Intelligence tier: one-time $49.99 Intelligence Usage Packs (AI Weekly).
- **OpenAI** added business profiles and customer-facing agents to ChatGPT (TechStartups).
- **Anthropic** announced new Claude models for coding, science and cybersecurity (TechStartups).
- **TSMC** approved plans to invest up to $1 billion in Anthropic and OpenAI (TechStartups).
- **Funding:** Houston-area startup Mavria launched with a $130 million raise at a $1 billion value.
