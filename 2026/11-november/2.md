### Commodities

- **Oil fell about 7%** (Quartz, Gulf News, OilPrice, JKN):

### Deals and IPOs

- **Marvell to buy Lumentum** (TechCrunch, CNBC, Yahoo Finance, SEC 8-K):
  - All-stock deal. Enterprise value: $115 billion. Equity value: about $80 billion.
  - It is one of the largest tech deals ever.
  - Lumentum holders get 4.5 Marvell shares per share.
  - Marvell holders will own about 67% of the combined company.
  - Expected close: first half of 2028, after approvals.
  - The deal is a bet on optical links for AI data centers.
- **Anthropic amended its IPO filing** (Fortune, Northeast Times, OfficeChai):
  - Q3 revenue: $16.96 billion, up about 47% from Q2 ($11.56 billion).
  - Q3 operating loss: $567 million. Q2 had a $974 million operating profit.
  - Main cause: $5.0 billion of short-term compute, which costs more than long-term deals.
  - Compute commitments: about $895 billion (some sources say $807 billion through 2036).
  - First nine months of 2026: revenue $33.2 billion, operating loss $1.26 billion.

### Chips and trade

- **Marvell–Lumentum** (see Deals above) is the biggest chip story of the day.
- **China rare earth deadline** (Startup Fortune, Technology.org):
  - The pause on China's wider rare earth export rules ends on 10 November, by the published text.
  - Beijing says the US and China agreed to extend the wider deal to 10 January 2027.
  - The paused rules include a "0.1% rule" that would hit foreign chipmakers.
