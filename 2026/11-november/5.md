# Daily News — 5 November 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Thursday, 5 November 2026. **A sharp AI and chip selloff erased the post-midterm rally.** **The Nasdaq fell 5.5% and entered a correction.** **The Bank of England raised rates for the first time since 2023.** **Gold rose to a two-week high. Bitcoin slipped below $80,000.**

---

## Finance

### Stocks

- **US close, Thursday 5 November** (Fox Business, StockTitan, Yahoo Finance):
  - Dow Jones: −3.44% (about −1,833 points) to about 51,523.
  - S&P 500: −4.36% to about 7,354.
  - Nasdaq Composite: −5.52% to about 24,315.
- **Worst day since April 2025** for all three indexes (StockTitan, citing CNBC).
- **Nasdaq is in correction:** it closed at least 10% below its 6 October record high.
- **Wednesday's post-election gains are gone.**
- **More than $2 trillion in tech market value was lost** (NPR).
- **Why stocks fell:**
  - Investors fear the new Democratic Congress will slow the AI data center build-out.
  - ING expects tighter data center rules: new taxes, fewer tax breaks, and environmental standards.
- **Chips led the drop:**
  - Philadelphia Semiconductor Index: down about 12%.
  - Nvidia: down about 9.3% (Reuters). AP reported −6.3%, likely at a different time of day.
- **Asia, next morning** (CNBC, ABC):
  - Japan's Nikkei 225 entered a bear market.
  - Australia's ASX fell sharply.

&nbsp;

### Central banks

- **Bank of England raised rates by 0.25 points to 4.00%** (Yahoo Finance UK, Babypips):
  - First hike since 2023. The vote was 8–1; one member wanted a bigger hike.
  - Governor Andrew Bailey said this is not the start of a long run of hikes.
  - Next moves depend on the Iran war, which pushed oil and inflation up.
- **FTSE 100 rose 0.4%**, unlike US stocks.

&nbsp;

### Earnings (after the close)

- **Winners** (Closing Look):
  - Cloudflare: up about 17% after an earnings beat.
  - Airbnb: up about 7% after an earnings beat.
- **Losers** (Closing Look):
  - DraftKings: down about 18%. Missed and cut its full-year revenue guide.
  - Atlassian: down about 16% despite a beat. Cautious cloud outlook.
- **Other reports after the close** (Seeking Alpha): Lumentum, IonQ, IREN, Main Street Capital.
- **AppLovin rose about 31% in Thursday trading**, after Wednesday's beat (Closing Look).

&nbsp;

### Commodities

- **Gold rose to a more-than-two-week high** (Yahoo Finance, Trading Economics, ABC):
  - About $4,119, up about 1.5% (Trading Economics).
  - December futures hit $4,134 in the morning (Yahoo Finance).
  - Goldman Sachs cut its end-2026 gold forecast to $4,150 from $4,650.
- **Oil** (Fortune, Nexo, Bitcoin Magazine):
  - Brent: $99.52 at 6 a.m. ET, about $6 lower than Wednesday morning (Fortune).
  - Nexo put Brent near $96 later in the day.
  - Bitcoin Magazine reported a rebound in oil during the day.

&nbsp;

### Crypto

- **Bitcoin traded between about $76,000 and $80,000** (Coinbase, Bitcoin Magazine, Nexo, ABC):
  - Near $79,600 in the morning.
  - Dipped below $78,000 after lower-than-expected US jobless claims (Nexo).
