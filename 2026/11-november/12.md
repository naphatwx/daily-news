# Daily News — 12 November 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Thursday, 12 November 2026. **Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia's CEO, president and board member. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO.** **Nvidia closed down about 4.3% after falling about 14% at the open.** **AMD and Broadcom each rose more than 5%.** **US stocks slipped: the S&P 500 lost about 0.35% and the Nasdaq lost 0.39%.** **The 10-year Treasury yield closed near 5.10%.** **Brent oil rose about 3.3% after attacks on Saudi energy sites.** **Cisco and Applied Materials beat estimates after the close.**

---

## Finance

### Markets

- **US indexes closed lower** (Capp Notes, StockTitan, Motley Fool):
  - Dow: −0.36% (−189.40 points) to 52,297.09.
  - S&P 500: −0.35% to 7,592.40.
  - Nasdaq Composite: −0.39% to 25,366.09.
  - Russell 2000: −1.66% to 2,799.06. It was the weakest major index.
- **Why:** Nvidia's CEO exit, higher Treasury yields and new inflation worry.
- **Stocks were higher at midday,** then faded into the close (Motley Fool).

&nbsp;

### Bonds and the Fed

- **Yields rose** as the bond market reopened after Veterans Day:
  - 10-year: near 5.10%, back above 5%.
  - 2-year: about +15 basis points to 4.88%.
- **Cause:** the hot October CPI from Tuesday. Headline CPI rose 0.4% in the month (forecast 0.3%). Core CPI rose 0.3% for a third month in a row.
- **Rate bets** (CME FedWatch, as reported after the CPI data):
  - About 85% to 89% chance of a 0.25-point hike in December.
  - About 11% to 15% chance of a 0.50-point hike.
- **Next Fed decision:** 9 December.

## Technology

### Nvidia

- **Jensen Huang resigned** as CEO, president and board member. It took effect at once (Nvidia press release, CNBC).
- **Interim CEO:** Colette Kress, the longtime CFO.
- **New board chair:** Harvey C. Jones. He is the company's first chair.
- **The board will search for a permanent CEO.**
- **Background:**
  - Huang admitted to an affair with a woman reported to be a suspected Chinese spy.
  - CNBC reported two affairs. One was with an employee.
  - The board opened an independent probe on 8 November. It hired the law firm Wachtell Lipton.
  - A person familiar with the matter told CNBC that the board decided on Wednesday night to remove him.
- **Stock:** about −14% at the open, about −4.3% at the close.
- **Next:**
  - Nvidia still reports Q3 results on 17 November.
  - Three House committee chairs asked Huang to attend a closed-door briefing on 18 November.

&nbsp;

### Chip rivals

- **AMD and Broadcom rose** as investors bet they can take share from Nvidia.
- **Google** showed two special AI chips. One is the TPU 8tx, designed with AMD (newsletter report).

&nbsp;

### Earnings after the close

- **Cisco:**
  - Revenue: +24% to a record $18.52 billion (estimate $18.12 billion).
  - Adjusted EPS: $1.40 (estimate $1.29).
  - It raised its fiscal 2027 outlook. It expects $9 billion of AI infrastructure revenue from hyperscalers in fiscal 2027.
- **Applied Materials:**
  - Fiscal Q4 revenue: +53% to a record $10.41 billion.
  - Adjusted EPS: $4.16.
  - Q1 revenue guidance: $10.6 billion to $11.6 billion.
- **Cerebras:** Q3 revenue beat estimates and guidance was strong.

&nbsp;

### Anthropic IPO

- **The order book is said to be several times oversubscribed** ahead of next week's pricing (newsletter report).
- **TSMC's board approved up to $1 billion in AI investments:** $500 million in Anthropic shares at the IPO price and $500 million in OpenAI preferred stock.

### Other AI and tech news

- **Google:**
  - Gemini Decisions API: answers up to 1,000 yes-or-no or multiple-choice questions in about a quarter of a second.
  - DeepMind released Gemini Robotics 2.5 and AlphaGenome 2.
- **OpenAI** launched "Businesses in ChatGPT" (newsletter report).
- **ByteDance:** co-founder Zhang Yiming reportedly said it will not catch OpenAI and Anthropic at the frontier before 2029 or 2030.
- **United Airlines** is blocking unauthorized AI agents from flight bookings.
