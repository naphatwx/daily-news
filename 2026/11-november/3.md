# Daily News — 3 November 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Tuesday, 3 November 2026 (US midterm Election Day). **Chip stocks rebounded and lifted the Nasdaq 0.8%.** **Apple fell after a weak margin outlook and pulled the Dow down.** **AMD beat estimates and jumped about 8% after hours.** **Oil fell for a second day on hopes of US–Iran talks.**

---

## Finance

### Stocks

- **US close, Tuesday 3 November** (StockTitan, Motley Fool, EconCurrents):
  - S&P 500: +0.36% to 7,568.92. Ended a three-day losing streak.
  - Nasdaq Composite: +0.83% to 25,372.01.
  - Dow Jones: −0.38% (−197.26 points) to 51,786.72.
- **Chip stocks led the gains** (24/7 Wall St.):
  - Nvidia: up about 3%. AMD: up about 5% before its earnings. Broadcom: up about 3%.
- **Apple weighed on the Dow** after its earnings (see Earnings below).
- **Market breadth was weak:** decliners beat gainers about 1.7 to 1 (EconCurrents).
- **10-year Treasury yield rose** about 6 basis points to near 4.95% (Investrade). Sources range from 4.91% to 4.97%.
- **Still ahead this week:**
  - Midterm election results.
  - EIA oil inventory report on Wednesday, 4 November.
  - October jobs report on Friday, 6 November.

&nbsp;

### Earnings

- **AMD Q3 beat estimates** (CNBC, Yahoo Finance, Investing.com, SEC 8-K):
  - Revenue: about $13.38 billion, up 45% from a year ago. Estimate: about $13.06 billion.
  - Adjusted EPS: $2.09. Estimate: about $1.94.
  - Data center revenue: $8.55 billion, nearly double. It is 64% of total revenue.
  - Q4 guidance: about $17 billion, plus or minus 3%. Above the consensus.
  - Management expects 2027 revenue to grow more than 80%.
  - Stock: closed at $483.90 (+3.65%), then rose about 7–8% after hours.
- **Apple fiscal Q4 beat, but the stock fell** (CNBC, MacRumors, KuCoin):
  - Results came out after the close on Monday, 2 November.
  - Revenue: about $114.5 billion, up 12%. EPS: $2.10. Both above estimates.
  - iPhone revenue: about $57.6 billion, up 17% (one source says $54.3 billion).
  - December-quarter gross margin guide: 45–46%, below estimates.
  - Causes: higher memory costs and Siri AI spending.
  - CEO John Ternus called memory prices "unprecedented".
  - December-quarter revenue guide: up 7–10%, above estimates.

&nbsp;

### Commodities

- **Oil fell for a second day** (CNBC, Rigzone, Investrade):
  - Brent: −$1.17 to $103.60.
  - WTI: −$0.37 to $96.09.
  - Brent swung in a range of more than $6 during the day.
  - Prices rose early after reports of a US strike on Iran's Qeshm Island, near the Strait of Hormuz.
  - Prices then fell on hopes of a deal to end the war.
  - Iran's Foreign Ministry said it will not accept diplomacy "based on coercion".
- **Bank forecasts** (CNBC):
  - Goldman Sachs raised its December Brent forecast to $105.
  - Goldman warns of $140 if Gulf output stays 3.5 million barrels a day below 2025 levels.
  - TD Securities sees fair value for Brent at $90–$100.
- **Gold stayed below $4,000** (Yahoo Finance, Investrade, Rio Times):
  - December gold futures: −$29.20 (−0.74%) to $3,940.40.
  - Spot gold traded between about $3,922 and $3,948.
  - Higher Treasury yields pushed gold down.
  - Gold is about 29% below its 28 January high of $5,513.

&nbsp;

### Crypto

- **Bitcoin slipped toward $76,000** (Fortune, CoinGecko via Admiral Markets, KuCoin):
  - Fortune: $76,995 at 6:45 a.m. ET, down $755 from the day before.
  - CoinGecko: $76,466 at 08:56 GMT, down about 2.2% in 24 hours.
  - Bitcoin is about $29,600 lower than one year ago.

## Technology

### AI

- **AI firms prepare for the midterms** (The Neuron):
  - OpenAI and Anthropic set up teams to watch for election misinformation.
- **Goldman Sachs pulled advanced AI coding tools** from many staff who had been testing them (The Neuron).
- **OpenAI** (The Neuron):
  - Signed deals to bring AI into Abu Dhabi's government and major industries.
  - Plans to push for limits on the most powerful AI models when Congress returns on 9 November.
- **Apple Intelligence in China** (9to5Mac, The Neuron):
  - Testing with Alibaba's Qwen model starts Wednesday, 4 November.

&nbsp;

### Chips

- **Nvidia and China** (24/7 Wall St., Financial Times report):
  - The FT reported that Nvidia told cloud clients to sell at least 51% of their services to non-Chinese companies.
  - An AI startup CEO warned about rising Chinese chip competition. Nvidia stock still rose about 3%.
  - Nvidia reports earnings on 17 November.
- **AMD** (see Earnings above): data center sales nearly doubled on AI demand.
