# Daily News — 13 November 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Friday, 13 November 2026. **Tech stocks fell again as the Nvidia turmoil went on.** **The Nasdaq lost 0.91% and the S&P 500 lost 0.40%. The Dow rose 0.10%.** **All three still gained more than 2% for the week.** **Nvidia fell about 2% and AMD fell almost 9%.** **October producer prices rose 6.5% in a year, the fastest pace since November 2022.** **The 10-year Treasury yield closed near 5.15%.** **Bitcoin fell below $70,000.** **The FAA ordered a nationwide ground stop, and officials are checking if a new AI system played a part.**

### Inflation, bonds and the Fed

- **October PPI** (BLS, CNN, Reuters):

  - Food prices: +1.3% in the month, the fastest gain since July 2022.
- **Yields rose:**

- **Rate bets for the 9 December Fed meeting:**

  - Kalshi: 91.5% chance of a 0.25-point hike.
- **Fed speakers:** Mary Daly and Susan Collins both said more tightening is likely.

## Technology

### Nvidia

- **Stock:** about −2% on Friday and about −12% for the week. Market value closed below $4 trillion.
- **Successor search:** the Financial Times reported that the board approached Arm CEO Rene Haas.
- **AI financing plan in doubt** (New York Times and Bloomberg, via Seoul Economic Daily):
  - In August, Jensen Huang announced a plan to supply more than $500 billion for AI infrastructure with six firms: Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs and KKR.
  - No loans have been made yet.
  - At least one firm discussed leaving the plan on 12 November.
  - A first deal may not come before January 2027.
- **Backstops:** Nvidia had $36 billion in off-balance-sheet AI cloud agreements, per its July filing (CNBC).
- **Analysts:**
  - HSBC cut Nvidia to Hold. Its price target fell to $170 from $365.
  - Truist cut it to Hold.
  - Morgan Stanley cut its price target to $267.
- **Politics:** Democratic leaders on two congressional committees are opening a probe into approvals of Nvidia AI chip exports to China-linked companies (search summary).
- **Hugging Face:** Nvidia's planned $12.9 billion purchase is still expected to close in 2027 (newsletter report).
- **Next:** Q3 results on 17 November.

&nbsp;

### FAA ground stop

- **The FAA ordered a nationwide ground stop** for all departing flights (Click2Houston).
- **Cause given:** a telecommunications problem at air traffic control centers.
- **AI link under review:** Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin said agencies are checking if the FAA's new AI system, SMART, played a part.
  - SMART adjusts flight routes with real-time data. Its rollout began in September.
  - Officials have not said the AI system caused the problem.
  - Mullin said there is no threat to the public.
