# Daily News — 16 November 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Monday, 16 November 2026. **US stocks fell hard: the Nasdaq lost 2.5%, the S&P 500 1.9% and the Dow 1.4%.** **Chip stocks led the fall. Nvidia closed at about $156, its lowest since July 2025.** **CNBC reported that Nvidia's board is not discussing a return of Jensen Huang.** **The US issued a new rule that limits remote access to advanced US chips.** **Brent oil fell about 3.5% to $92.44.** **Bitcoin held near $65,000.** **Next: Nvidia reports results on 17 November and Anthropic prices its IPO on 18 November.**

## Finance

### Markets

- **US indexes closed lower for a third day** (AP, CNBC):
  - Nasdaq Composite: −626.52 points (−2.49%) to 24,509.76.
  - S&P 500: −145.08 points (−1.92%) to 7,416.59.
  - Dow: −739.38 points (−1.41%) to 51,608.17.
- **Fear rose:** the VIX jumped as much as 25% to a high of 24.05 (CNBC).
- **Sectors:** consumer staples was the only S&P 500 sector to rise (+0.3%). Information technology fell 2.7%. Communication services fell 2.6%.
- **Chip stocks led the fall:**
  - iShares Semiconductor ETF: −5.4%.
  - Micron: −7.6%. Intel: about −7%. Sandisk: about −8%.
  - AMD: −5.6%. Oracle: about −5%. Broadcom: −3.9%.
  - Dell: about −7%. CoreWeave: −6.1%.
- **Banks fell too:** Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs and JPMorgan each lost more than 2%.
- **Other movers:**
  - Tyson Foods: +8.7% after profit beat forecasts.
  - Summit Therapeutics: −16.5%. The FDA indicated it cannot approve its lung cancer drug application.
  - L3Harris: −3.9%. Officials suggested its work for the FAA may have played a part in Friday's nationwide ground stop.
  - Baidu: −5.4% after online marketing revenue fell 22%.
  - XPeng: +3.2% on record revenue.
- **Asia:** Taiwan's Taiex fell 6.0%, the Kospi 3.6% and the Nikkei 2.4%. TSMC fell 5.7% (The Close newsletter).
  - Reason given: SemiAnalysis cut its forecasts for AI chip spending at the weekend.

### Economy and the Fed

- **New York factory index:** 7.8 in November, down from 25.5 in October. Forecasts were about 17 to 18.
  - New orders fell to 3.0.
  - The employment index rose to 18.1, its highest since June 2022.
- **Fed Chair Kevin Warsh** spoke in San Francisco. He said AI could raise productivity but could also disrupt the job market. He gave no signal on rates.
- **Fed Governor Michelle Bowman** warned about company debt levels. She said stocks risk a "large correction" if AI does not deliver (Motley Fool).
- **Rate bets:** markets price a rate rise in December as almost certain. CME FedWatch showed above 93% for a 0.25-point rise (The Close). CoinDesk cited 100%.
- **Bonds:** the 10-year Treasury yield ended near 5.13%, down from 5.15% on Friday (AP).
- **Dollar:** the dollar index rose to about 103.5, its highest since April 2025.
- **China:** October industrial output, retail sales and investment all missed forecasts (CNBC).
- **Europe:** UniCredit got EU antitrust approval to take over Commerzbank.

### Oil and metals

- **Oil fell sharply:**
  - Brent: −3.5% to $92.44 a barrel (AP).
  - WTI: about −3.2% to −3.3%. Sources give $84.38 (CoinDesk) and $85.72 (The Close).
  - Reason given: the IEA reported a global supply surplus. October stocks rose by 52 million barrels.
- **Earlier in the day** oil was higher after the tanker mine reports. CNBC showed Brent at $96.13 at that time.
- **US diesel** fell below $6 a gallon for the first time since Labor Day (AAA, via CoinDesk).
- **Gold:** −0.9% to about $3,852. **Copper:** −1.5%, below $6 a pound for the first time since June.

## Technology

### Nvidia

- **No return for Huang, for now:** CNBC reported that the board is not discussing a return of Jensen Huang as CEO. This goes against Sunday's Financial Times report.
- **Shares:** −4.1% to about $156.10, the lowest close since July 2025. AP says Nvidia has lost $735 billion in market value since the scandal began.
- **Background:** Huang resigned last week. AP and CNBC link this to reports about his personal life, including alleged ties to an alleged Chinese spy. The board is running an investigation, led by law firm Wachtell Lipton (CNBC).
- **Politics:** Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent called for a "dramatic shakeup" and an outside CEO. President Trump said he would not mind if Huang came back.
- **Analysts:** Mizuho cut its price target to $220 from $250. UBS cut its target to $240 from $300.
- **Michael Burry** kept his bets against Nvidia before the results.
- **Results on Tuesday, 17 November**, after the close. Forecasts differ by source: about $2.47 to $2.52 a share on revenue of about $109 billion to $111 billion.

&nbsp;

### Chip export rule

- **The US Commerce Department issued an interim final rule** on Monday. It limits remote access to advanced US chips and servers.
- **Aim:** close a gap that let Chinese firms rent Nvidia computing power in third countries (CNBC).
- **Effect:** the Motley Fool linked AMD's fall to new limits on AI chip exports to Southeast Asian countries.
