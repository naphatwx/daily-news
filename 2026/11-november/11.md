# Daily News — 11 November 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Wednesday, 11 November 2026. **US stocks rallied: the S&P 500 gained 1.31% and the Nasdaq gained 1.60%.** **CoreWeave jumped 20% to 25% after its Q3 beat and led an AI rebound.** **Apple hit a record high.** **After the close, the WSJ reported that Nvidia's board is preparing to remove CEO Jensen Huang. Nvidia fell about 8% after hours.** **The US bond market was closed for Veterans Day.** **Oil rose after Iran denied talks with the US.**

---

## Finance

### Markets

- **US indexes closed higher** (Capp Notes, StockTitan, Motley Fool):
  - Dow: +0.49% (+254.60 points) to 52,486.49.
  - S&P 500: +1.31% (+98.45 points) to 7,619.38.
  - Nasdaq Composite: +1.60% (+400.31 points) to 25,466.19.
  - Nasdaq-100: +2.28% to 28,374.25.
- **Tech led the rally.** CoreWeave's earnings beat brought buyers back to AI stocks.
- **Every stock in the Philadelphia semiconductor index rose.**
- **The S&P 500 is within about 2.6% of its record high.**
- **Gains were broad.** More stocks rose than fell on both the NYSE and the Nasdaq.

&nbsp;

### Bonds and the Fed

- **The US bond market was closed for Veterans Day.** There were no new closing yields.
- **Tuesday's closes still stand** (Saxo): 2-year 4.73%, 10-year 4.98%, 30-year 5.35%.
- **Rate bets:** traders fully price in a hike in December and two more by next June (Saxo).
- **Next Fed decision:** 9 December.

&nbsp;

### Big movers

- **Gainers:**
  - CoreWeave: about +20% to +25%, depending on the source (CNBC, Motley Fool).
  - Azenta: +22% after an earnings beat and strong guidance (CNBC).
  - Sandisk: +13.7%.
  - Micron: +11.9%.
  - Seagate: +10.0%.
  - Oracle: about +8.8%.
  - Western Digital: +8.3%.
  - Apple: new record high above $355.42. Market value about $5.16 trillion (TheStreet).
  - Nvidia: rose in the regular session, ending a two-day drop, despite an analyst downgrade.
- **After hours:**
  - Nvidia: about −7% to −8.6%, to near $160 (see Technology).
- **Coming Thursday:** Cisco and Applied Materials report after the close (CNBC).

&nbsp;

### Oil, crypto and others

- **Oil rose** after Iran denied it is in talks with the US (MLQ):
  - Brent: +1.39% (+$1.27) to $92.76.
  - WTI: +$0.49 to $86.53.
- **Oil is still near ten-week lows.** Middle East shipments are almost back to pre-war levels (Trading Economics).
- **EIA outlook:** Brent near $100 for the rest of the year.
- **Bitcoin** traded between about $73,800 and $74,600. One source shows +1.23% over 24 hours to $74,597 (Nexo).
- **Spot Bitcoin ETFs** had a third day of outflows in a row.
- **Gold slipped** (Eurasia Business News).

&nbsp;

### Asia and Europe

- **Asia rose again:**
  - Nikkei 225: +1.31% to about 61,249.
  - Kospi: +2.6% to about 5,375.
- **Europe closed at a record** (MLQ):
  - Stoxx 600: +0.72% to 663.05.
  - Euro Stoxx 50: about +1%.
  - Siemens Energy: +10.8% after it raised its margin outlook.

## Technology

### Nvidia

- **The WSJ reported that Nvidia's board is preparing to remove CEO Jensen Huang** (CNBC, CBS News, Korea Times).
  - The report cites people familiar with the matter.
  - It says the reason is two extramarital affairs. One involves a suspected Chinese spy.
  - The board could meet as soon as Wednesday.
- **A New York Times story came minutes earlier.** It alleges a relationship between Huang and an Nvidia executive who reports to him.
- **Stock after hours:**
  - CBS News: more than −8%, to $159.51.
  - Yahoo Finance: −7.28%, to $161.08.
  - StockTitan: −8.61%.
- **Value lost this week:** about $600 billion, including the after-hours drop.
- **Not confirmed.** Nvidia did not comment at once. Reuters could not verify the report. Huang has not been removed.
- **Also open:** probes by the FBI and three House committees (newsletter reports).

&nbsp;

### CoreWeave

- **The stock rose about 20% to 25%** after Tuesday's Q3 report.
- **Why:** revenue beat estimates and the company raised its full-year forecast. That eased worry about its debt.
- **It lifted the whole AI group:** chip, memory and storage stocks rose with it.

&nbsp;

### Anthropic IPO

- **The roadshow started** with the $70 to $78 range. Trading is still set for 19 November on Nasdaq as "ANT".
- **Amended S-1:** the $34.9 billion compute deal with Lambda can now be ended by either side (newsletter report).
- **TSMC:** up to $500 million in Anthropic shares at the IPO price, and up to $500 million in OpenAI preferred stock.
- **Rumor:** one newsletter says bankers may push the value to $1.5 trillion. This is not confirmed.

&nbsp;

### Other AI and tech news

- **OpenAI:**
  - Leaked documents put quarterly revenue near $10 billion and adjusted operating margin at −109%. OpenAI has not confirmed this.
  - It is said to seek a funding round at a $1.25 trillion value.
  - Advanced and Standard Voice will be retired on 11 December 2026.
- **Apple:** Chinese regulators approved Apple Intelligence on iPads and Macs.
- **Google** opened Gemini 4 Carbon to paying developers ahead of a December price rise.
- **Nvidia's open-source agent runtime** moved to CNCF Sandbox governance.
