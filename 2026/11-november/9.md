# Daily News — 9 November 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Monday, 9 November 2026. **US stocks had their best day since April: the S&P 500 rose 2.35% and the Nasdaq rose 2.74%.** **Chip stocks led. AMD jumped 13% after an Oppenheimer upgrade.** **Nvidia fell 4.3% after its board opened a probe into CEO Jensen Huang.** **Oil dropped almost 6%.** **The WSJ says Anthropic could list on Nasdaq as soon as the week of 16 November.**

## Finance

### Markets

- **US indexes closed sharply higher** (CNBC, StockTitan):
  - Dow: +2.15% (+1,104 points) to 52,389.75.
  - S&P 500: +2.35% to 7,549.94. Best day since 8 April.
  - Nasdaq Composite: +2.74% to 25,253.14.
  - Russell 2000: +3.1% (AP, via TechStartups).
- **The rally came after last week's sell-off.** Last week was the S&P 500's worst week since April 2025.
- **Sectors** (CNBC, afternoon): industrials +4.2%, consumer discretionary +3.7%, tech +3%. Energy was the only sector down.
- **Citi cut US, global and European tech to Neutral** from Overweight. It cited crowded positioning and sentiment (CNBC).

## Technology

### Nvidia CEO probe

- **Nvidia closed down 4.27% at $178.67** (StockTitan). That is about $192 billion in lost market value at the close. Some outlets say over $200 billion, likely an intraday number.
- **What happened:** on Sunday the board opened an independent probe into reports that Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort. Huang told staff he had an affair with a businesswoman whom intelligence agencies link to China (CNBC).
- **Huang stays CEO.** The board says it has no reason to think company secrets or national security were harmed.
- **China rejected the spy claims** (TechStartups).
- **Next dates:** Q3 earnings on 17 November. House committee leaders asked Huang for a closed briefing on 18 November.

&nbsp;

### AI

- **Anthropic IPO** (WSJ, via TechStartups):
  - It may list on Nasdaq as "ANT" as soon as the week of 16 November.
  - Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley are leading the deal.
  - Earlier talk was a value near $1.5 trillion. Some investors now want lower. No price is set.
- **Anthropic lawsuit:** a judge let parts of a class action over Claude Max advertising go ahead (Reuters).
- **Google announced new TPU chips** (Google Cloud blog): TPU 8tx for confidential computing, and TPU 8i Max for large-memory jobs. AMD says its EPYC CPUs power TPU 8tx (Motley Fool).
- **Adobe and Disney** signed a multiyear deal for custom AI models trained on Disney content.
- **Norway's grid operator Statnett paused new grid bookings for data centers.** The pause ends no later than 1 August 2027.

&nbsp;

### Other tech news

- **Tesla:** Elon Musk said Cybertruck production ends in about eight months to make room for Cybercab. Some buyers will get refunds.
- **Waymo:** US regulator NHTSA opened a recall query on 2,895 vehicles over passing stopped school buses (Reuters).
- **Meta** expanded AI age checks to Instagram worldwide and to Facebook.
- **Security:** a flaw called "SSHole" (CVE-2026-123158) affects OpenSSH with the musl C library in some setups.
- **Funding:** Onodrim raised $300 million at a $1.3 billion value. Ardian launched a €1 billion chip investment platform.
