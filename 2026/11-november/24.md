# Daily News — 24 November 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Tuesday, 24 November 2026. **Monday's AI rally faded. Chip stocks fell hard, and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index dropped about 7.9%.** **Nvidia fell about 5.9%. The Nasdaq fell 1.5% and the S&P 500 fell 0.5%.** **The Dow rose 0.85% and closed above 50,000.** **Anthropic fell below its $83 IPO price for the first time.** **Gold and bitcoin fell. Oil rose a little.**

---

## Finance

### Markets

- **US indexes were mixed** (CappNotes, 24/7 Wall St, journalarta):
  - Dow: +423.39 points (+0.85%) to 50,018.70. It closed above 50,000.
  - S&P 500: -36.08 points (-0.50%) to 7,201.09. This is its eighth loss in nine sessions.
  - Nasdaq Composite: -356.03 points (-1.48%) to 23,733.54.
  - Nasdaq-100: down about 2%.
- **Why:**
  - Investors sold chip and AI stocks after Monday's Nvidia-led jump. Doubts about AI spending and high prices returned (Motley Fool, Yahoo Finance).
  - Money moved into value and defensive stocks, such as health care and consumer names. This helped the Dow.
  - Traders fully priced a 0.25% Fed rate hike in December, per CME FedWatch (PiQ Markets).
- **Chip movers:**
  - Philadelphia Semiconductor Index: -7.88% to 8,832.50 (Chip Dispatch).
  - Nvidia: -5.86% to $155.38. It lost about $240 billion in market value (Motley Fool). It was down about 9% at midday.
  - AMD: down about 9.6%. Arm: down about 8%. Broadcom, TSMC and ASML: each down about 3% to 4%. These figures come from a snapshot near the close and may not match final closes.

## Technology

### Anthropic falls below its IPO price

- **Close:** down about 6.5% to $81.91. This is about 1% below the $83 IPO price (Bloomberg, CNBC).
- **Intraday:** the stock fell as much as about 13%, to near $76, in the morning (Yahoo Finance).
- **Speed:** it took only four trading days to fall below the IPO price. The stock opened at $111.50 on its first day, 19 November.
- **Possible reasons:**
  - The wider tech sell-off, and the early IPO premium wearing off.
  - Forbes linked the drop to an Axios report. It said staff at Anthropic and OpenAI worry more about the power of their internal models. Other outlets said this link is not proven.
- **Other details:**
  - Filings show CEO Dario Amodei and President Daniela Amodei each sold about $280 million in shares in the IPO (Yahoo Finance).
  - Options trading in the stock jumped (CNBC).
  - Canaccord Genuity started coverage with a buy rating and a $135 price target.

### AI and other

Most items below come from AI news digests. The Epochal item comes from Bloomberg. We could not confirm the others with major outlets.

- **Epochal:** three founding members of xAI started a new company called Epochal. It builds a tool that lets AI agents browse old versions of the internet (Bloomberg).
- **Anthropic:** launched Claude Ship. It bundles Code Review with two new agents in beta, for Team and Enterprise plans on GitHub.
- **Anthropic and Nscale:** Nscale is reportedly close to a second compute deal with Anthropic, worth about $45 billion.
- **Amazon and Decart:** one digest said Amazon agreed to buy Decart's technology and team for about $7.5 billion. Earlier reports said talks were at about $7 billion. We found no confirmed deal.
- **Google:** AI Mode in Search can now read Google Wallet.
- **OpenAI and Cisco:** reportedly open-sourced a project called Shirudo.
- **Cursor:** added support for Google's Gemini 4 Neon.
