# Daily News — 25 November 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Wednesday, 25 November 2026. **Stocks had a wild day before Thanksgiving. The Dow was down more than 1,100 points, then closed higher.** **Three things hit markets: hot PCE inflation, new Trump tariffs on 14 economies, and more AI selling.** **Nvidia fell about 3.9%. Anthropic swung hard but stayed near its $83 IPO price.** **US markets were closed on Thursday for Thanksgiving. Friday is a half day.**

## Finance

### Markets

- **US closes** (Motley Fool, GSM Global News, Swingfolio):
  - Dow: +0.33% to about 50,183. It stayed above 50,000.
  - S&P 500: -0.25% to about 7,183.
  - Nasdaq Composite: -0.62% to about 23,586.
  - Some outlets report slightly different closes.
- **Lows of the day:**
  - Dow: down more than 1,100 points, to about 48,914.
  - S&P 500: down more than 3%, to about 6,970.
  - Nasdaq: down about 4.7% at its worst point.
- **VIX (fear gauge):** closed at about 28.
- **Why it fell:** hot inflation data, new tariff plans, and doubts about AI spending (CNBC, FXStreet).
- **Why it came back:** buyers stepped in late. Money also moved from tech into "old economy" stocks. Most S&P 500 stocks rose, even though AI leaders fell (24/7 Wall St).
- **Holiday hours:** closed Thursday, 26 November. Stocks close early, at 1:00 p.m. ET, on Friday.

### Inflation (PCE)

- **October PCE price index:** +3.8% from a year ago. This was in line with forecasts. September was 3.7% (Morningstar).
- **Core PCE** (no food or energy): +3.3%. Forecast was 3.2%. September was 3.1%.
- **Monthly:** prices rose 0.3%.
- **Fed view:** the Fed range is 3.75% to 4.00%. This report adds to the case for a rate hike in December (Quartz, abc27).

&nbsp;

### New Trump tariffs

- **What:** the US proposed new tariffs on China, the EU and 12 other economies. It says their policies create too much factory output (Yahoo Finance, Axios).
- **Rates:**
  - China: 20% on most imports, on top of the 2018 Section 301 tariffs.
  - EU, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan and Switzerland: 15%, including normal import duties.
  - India and Vietnam are also on the list.
- **Left out for now:** Mexico and Norway. Their reviews are still open.
- **Timing:** public comments and hearings come first. The tariffs may not start until January 2027.
- **Reaction:** world stocks fell about 0.5% (MSCI, via Reuters). Other countries pushed back (Al Jazeera).

## Technology

### Nvidia keeps falling after Huang's return

- **Stock:** -3.9% to about $149.30. It was down as much as 8% early (GSM Global News, CNBC).
- **Background:**
  - Nvidia's board pushed out CEO Jensen Huang on 11–12 November. Reports linked this to his private conduct, with claims about a possible security risk. These are claims, not proven facts.
  - The board brought him back as CEO on 23 November. Nvidia said there is no national security risk. Reports say President Trump pushed for the move.
  - The stock jumped about 31% on Monday on the news, but it has fallen since.
- **Staff meeting:** Huang apologized to staff. He said Nvidia does not need a succession plan (CNBC).

&nbsp;

### Anthropic swings around its IPO price

- **Intraday:** fell almost 10% at one point (CNN).
- **Close:** reports differ. CNN said it ended up just over 1%. CNBC said it struggled to get back above the $83 IPO price.
- **Context:** Tuesday's close was about $81.91, the first close below the IPO price.
