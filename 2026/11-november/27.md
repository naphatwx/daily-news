# Daily News — 27 November 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Friday, 27 November 2026 (Black Friday). **US stocks rallied in a half-day session. The S&P 500 rose about 1.6%.** **It was the best week for US stocks since April 2025, but November may still be the worst since 2008.** **Stocks in Asia and Europe also rose, led by chipmakers.** **Gold jumped over 2%. Bitcoin held near $61,500.** **The FCC gave SpaceX conditional approval for up to a million AI satellites.**

---

## Finance

### Markets

- **US** (closed at 1:00 p.m. ET; reports differ slightly on levels):
  - S&P 500: about +1.6% to near 7,297.
  - Dow: about +1.2% to near 50,780.
  - Nasdaq: about +2.3% to near 24,130.
  - More than 80% of S&P 500 stocks rose (TheStreet).
- **Why it rose:** investors came back to AI and tech stocks after Thanksgiving. Nvidia led the gains. SanDisk rose more than 6%. TSMC's US shares rose nearly 3% (BigGo Finance).
- **The week:** the S&P 500 gained about 5.6%. This is its best week since April 2025. It came after a sharp drop the week before (TheStreet).
- **The month:** the S&P 500 and Nasdaq are still on track for their worst November since 2008 (World Today Journal).
- **Asia closes** (AP; other reports show smaller gains):
  - Japan Nikkei 225: +2.1% to 58,711.11.
  - South Korea Kospi: +4.3% to 4,915.68.
- **Europe closes** (MLQ; Newsquawk shows different numbers):
  - Germany DAX: +0.9% to 25,441.24.
  - STOXX 600: +1.1% to 643.84.
  - Chipmakers led: ASML +3.2%, Infineon +4.0%, STMicro +4.3%.

### Anthropic shares

- **Background:** Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 a share on 18 November. It trades on Nasdaq as ANT (Fortune, TechCrunch).
- **Report:** one AI briefing says ANT shares have fallen below the $83 IPO price.
- **Note:** this claim comes from one source. It was not checked against market data.

&nbsp;

### Black Friday shopping

- **Forecast:** Adobe expected US online sales of $12.9 billion on Black Friday, up 9.2% from last year (Adobe).
- **Season:** Adobe expects a record $275.1 billion online from 1 November to 31 December, up 6.7%.
- **Next:** Cyber Monday (30 November) is expected to be the biggest day, at $15.1 billion.
- **Note:** actual Black Friday results were not out yet when this was written.

&nbsp;

### Oil, gold and crypto

Prices below are snapshots. Reports differ.

- **Gold:** about $3,644 to $3,662, up 2.2% to 2.7%. Earlier in the week it fell to about $3,427 after a hot inflation (PCE) report (Trading Economics, KuCoin).
- **Bitcoin:** about $61,500, up about 1.4% to 1.6% in 24 hours. It is still about 29% below its September high (Rio Times).
- **Oil:** US crude near $85. One market report put Brent near $97 after a strike on a Saudi refinery on Thursday (LiteFinance, Ted Pillows).
- **Note:** the Saudi refinery strike on Thursday was not confirmed by a major news outlet. Houthi attacks on Saudi oil sites have happened several times this autumn.

## Technology

### SpaceX AI satellites

- **What:** the FCC gave SpaceX conditional approval to launch up to 1 million satellites for AI computing in orbit (Rio Rundown, The Neuron).
- **Canada:** also grew its AI computing fund to C$1 billion.

&nbsp;

### OpenAI fixes a billing error

- **What:** a billing error on GPT-6.2 made usage limits run out faster than they should.
- **Fix:** OpenAI fixed it. It says it will not charge users for the extra usage in the past (AI news roundups).

&nbsp;

### Google

- **Math proof:** Google Research reportedly posted a proof about integer factoring written by an AI agent. A machine-checked (Lean) version is to follow (The Century Report).
- **Workspace:** from 1 February 2027, Workspace agents must use Google's own connectors and MCP instead of direct REST API calls.

&nbsp;

### AI news in brief

These come from AI news roundups. They were not checked against company blogs.

- **Apple:** removed MiniMax's Talkie chatbot app worldwide. Reports said its AI characters sent sexual messages to minors.
- **Nvidia:** reportedly stopped shipping AI chips to China.
- **Z.ai:** released GLM-5.4. Reuters reported that GLM-5.5 may slip to early 2027 or later.
- **Anthropic:** reportedly moved its Messages API to Rust. It cut CPU use by 70% and made the first token 20% faster.
- **Unconfirmed:** two outlets claim an AI proof of the Riemann hypothesis was checked by two groups. Treat this as unconfirmed.
