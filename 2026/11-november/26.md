# Daily News — 26 November 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Thursday, 26 November 2026. **US markets were closed for Thanksgiving.** **Stocks in Asia and Europe rose as tech shares bounced back.** **Taiwan jumped 2.5%. Korea steadied after a 6.2% drop the day before.** **The Bank of England warned about AI debt. SpaceX got approval for up to a million AI satellites.** **US stocks reopen on Friday for a half day, closing at 1:00 p.m. ET.**

---

## Finance

### Markets

- **US:** closed for Thanksgiving. On Wednesday, the S&P 500 ended down 0.3%. It had been down as much as 3.2% during the day (AP, via ABC News).
- **Asia closes** (AP, BNN Bloomberg):
  - Japan Nikkei 225: +0.9% to 57,499.16.
  - South Korea Kospi: +0.3% to 4,711.21. It fell 6.2% on Wednesday.
  - Taiwan Taiex: +2.5%.
  - Hong Kong Hang Seng: about +1.2%.
- **Europe** (early and midday trading; no confirmed closes found):
  - Germany DAX: about +1.8% to +1.9%.
  - France CAC 40: about +1.6% to +1.8%.
  - UK FTSE 100: about +1.3% to +1.6%.
- **Why it rose:** AI and tech stocks bounced back. Late buying in the US on Wednesday pulled the Nasdaq 100 back from the edge of a bear market. Investors mostly shrugged off the new US tariffs on 14 economies (AP, Yahoo Finance).
- **A warning:** an analyst at Panmure Liberum told clients to sell all stocks now. This is one analyst's view (Fortune).

&nbsp;

### Bank of England warns about AI debt

- **What:** the Bank of England put big cloud companies' possible AI exposure at just over $4 trillion. It called AI financing complex and hard to see into (FourWeekMBA).
- **Banks:** Citi and JPMorgan were reported to be selling parts of their AI loan books to other investors.
- **UK regulators:** also warned that AI agents may fall under financial rules.
- **Note:** these details come from AI news roundups. They were not checked against the Bank's own report.

&nbsp;

### Oil, gold and crypto

Prices below are snapshots taken during the day. Reports differ.

- **Oil:** Brent about $91 (+0.7%). WTI about $84 (Centrino Capital, KuCoin).
- **Gold:** about $3,565 to $3,580. Down 0.2% to 0.6% (Trading Economics, Centrino Capital).
- **Silver:** +0.85%.
- **Bitcoin:** about $60,600 to $60,900, up about 3% in 24 hours (FinFly, Coincu).
- **ETF outflows:** US spot bitcoin ETFs lost about $4.94 billion in November through the 24th. This is the biggest monthly outflow since they launched in 2024 (KuCoin).

## Technology

### SpaceX gets approval for AI satellites

- **What:** the FCC approved SpaceX's plan for up to 1 million satellites with AI chips. The project is called "Starmind" (The Neuron).
- **How:** the satellites would run on solar power. They would send data to Earth by laser through Starlink.
- **Note:** the project name and some details come from one outlet.

&nbsp;

### OpenAI changes

- **ChatGPT limits:** OpenAI is putting Chat and Work under one usage meter. Heavy users may hit limits sooner (The Neuron, Sam on AI).
- **Private Inference:** OpenAI is testing an early version for business customers. It lets them send prompts that OpenAI cannot read. Sam Altman announced it on X.
- **OpenAI and Cisco:** released an agent tool that blocks attacks on known software flaws.

&nbsp;

### AMD and Meta

- **Investment:** AMD said it will put $1 billion into Meta's share sale (creati.ai, AItoolly).
- **Hardware:** AMD's Helios racks, each with 72 Instinct MI455X GPUs, are now installed and in testing at Meta.

&nbsp;

### AI news in brief

These come from AI news roundups. They were not checked against company blogs.

- **Anthropic:** reportedly rewrote the API that routes Claude chats from Python to Rust. It cut CPU use per request by about 70% in one region.
- **New York:** Governor Kathy Hochul signed a five-year ban on toys with built-in AI chatbots. New York is the second state after California to do this.
- **Google:** released a model in China.
