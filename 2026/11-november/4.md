# Daily News — 4 November 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Wednesday, 4 November 2026 (the day after the US midterm elections). **The Dow jumped 3%, its best day since April 2025.** **Democrats won control of both chambers of Congress.** **Oil fell sharply on Iran peace-deal hopes.** **Gold went back above $4,000 and Bitcoin back above $80,000.**

---

## Finance

### Stocks

- **US close, Wednesday 4 November** (CNBC, Motley Fool, StockTitan):
  - Dow Jones: +3.03% (+1,569.40 points) to 53,356.12.
  - S&P 500: +1.58% to 7,688.62.
  - Nasdaq Composite: +1.44% to 25,736.51.
- **Why stocks rose:**
  - Democrats won both the House and the Senate. This ends unified Republican government.
  - Oil prices fell sharply.
  - Motley Fool called it the biggest post-midterm rally in 44 years.
- **Market breadth was strong:** gainers beat decliners by about 3 to 1.
- **Still ahead this week:** October jobs report on Friday, 6 November.

&nbsp;

### Earnings (after the close)

- **AppLovin: up more than 20%** (CNBC):
  - Q3 revenue: $2.18 billion. Estimate: $2.07 billion.
- **Qualcomm: down almost 6%** (CNBC, SEC 8-K):
  - Fiscal Q4 non-GAAP EPS: $2.17, down 28% from $3.00 a year ago. Consensus: about $2.02.
  - Revenue came in at the high end of its $9.7–$10.5 billion guidance.
  - Full-year fiscal 2026 revenue: $43.1 billion.
  - Kept its fiscal 2027 data center revenue target of $5 billion.
  - Expects non-handset revenue to grow more than 60%.
- **Coherent: up 7%.** Adjusted EPS $2.09 vs. $1.99 estimate (CNBC).
- **Losers after hours** (CNBC):
  - Zillow: down more than 16%. Missed key Q3 metrics.
  - Cava: down more than 11%. Missed Q3 estimates.
  - HubSpot: down more than 8%. Weak Q4 guidance.
  - MercadoLibre: down about 7%. Q3 earnings missed.
  - Figma: down almost 7%, even after a strong Q3 beat.

&nbsp;

### Commodities

- **Oil fell sharply** (Trading Economics, Fortune, Forbes):
  - WTI: down about 8% to near $88.30 (Trading Economics).
  - Brent: near $99 by the end of the day.
  - Cause: hopes of a US–Iran peace deal.
  - Some reports say the two sides are still far from a deal.
- **Gold rose back above $4,000** (Trading Economics, Yahoo Finance):
  - Spot gold: about $4,047, up about 2.9%.
  - A weaker dollar and the election result helped gold.

&nbsp;

### Crypto

- **Bitcoin rose nearly 4% to back above $80,000** (Yahoo Finance).
  - Through October, Bitcoin and gold were the two worst major assets of 2026.

## Technology

### AI and data centers

- **Voters push back on data centers** (AI Weekly):
  - Ohio voters passed data-center bans in 14 cities.
  - Texas Governor-Elect Hinojosa called for a data center moratorium.
  - CoreWeave bonds hit new lows after the call.
- **Meta bought Conductor**, a startup for parallel AI coding agents (AI Weekly).
- **Apple** (AI Weekly):
  - Cut engineers' daily AI spend cap from $300 to $200.
  - Its 10-K warns that AI agents may act without permission.
- **Moonshot released Kimi K3.1 Pro weights** (AI Weekly):
  - A 2.8 trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 1 million tokens of context.

&nbsp;

### Chips

- **Qualcomm** (see Earnings above): stock fell despite an EPS beat.
- **Arm** (AI Weekly): reported record quarterly revenue of $1.44 billion. Data center royalties doubled.
