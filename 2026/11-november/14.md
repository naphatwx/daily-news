# Daily News — 14 November 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Saturday, 14 November 2026. **US stock markets were closed for the weekend.** **Bitcoin kept falling and traded near $63,500 late in the day.** **Nvidia's board said it has not yet picked a search firm or formally approached any CEO candidate.** **OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet, which lets its AI agents buy things with user approval.** **Iran said three more tankers hit mines.** **Next week: Nvidia reports results on 17 November and Anthropic prices its IPO on 18 November.**

---

## Finance

### Markets

- **US and most world stock markets were closed** on Saturday.
- **Friday's close, for reference** (see the 13 November file):
  - S&P 500: −0.40% on the day, +2.5% for the week.
  - Nasdaq Composite: −0.91%.
  - Dow: +0.10%.
  - Nvidia: about −13% for the week (Market Mechanics Report).
- **Investor mood is weak** (AAII survey, via Market Mechanics Report):
  - Bullish: 30.8%, down from 34.1%.
  - Bearish: 51.4%. The long-run average is 31.5%.

&nbsp;

### Crypto

- **Bitcoin fell further** (Cryptoticker, CoinGecko data):
  - Price: $63,528 at 23:03 UTC on 14 November.
  - Reasons given: bets on a Fed rate hike, ETF outflows and about $1 billion in liquidations.
- **Ether fell** about 9% (Cryptoticker headline).
- **Sources differ:** a Yahoo Finance history row shows a Bitcoin close of $67,016.88 for 14 November.

&nbsp;

### Bonds and the Fed

- **Treasuries had their biggest sell-off in 18 months** (Financial Times, via Debt Serious):
  - The 10-year yield rose 28 basis points in the week to about 5.14%.
- **Rate bets:** markets expect a 0.25-point hike at the 8–9 December Fed meeting. The target range now is 3.75% to 4.00%.
- **October inflation, reported earlier in the week:**
  - CPI: +0.4% in the month (forecast +0.3%). Annual inflation reached 4%.
  - Core CPI: +0.3% in the month.

&nbsp;

### Credit markets (week in review)

From the Debt Serious weekend letter. These items are from 8–13 November.

- **AI debt:**
  - CoreWeave is in talks for a $4 billion loan backed by GPUs and a Meta contract. Yield: 7% to 7.5% (Bloomberg).
  - CoreWeave credit default swaps trade above 1,000 basis points (CNBC, via Market News).
  - Two firms plan ETFs tied to credit default swaps on AI debt (Bloomberg).
  - Sixth Street's CEO warned about fast growth in AI credit (Bloomberg).
- **Private credit:**
  - The New York and Dallas Feds will study lending standards in private credit (Bloomberg).
  - BDC loan books shrank 4% in a year. Loans marked below 80 cents rose to 5% of portfolios, from 3% (Bloomberg).
- **Deals:**
  - Google raised about $3.8 billion in its first Hong Kong dollar and Singapore dollar bonds (Quartz).
  - Pizza Hut plans $1 billion of bonds to fund its buyout by LongRange Capital (Bloomberg).
  - Banks led by JPMorgan will sell $2 billion of the Qualtrics loan at 85 cents (Bloomberg).
- **Housing:** US existing-home sales fell to 3.75 million, the lowest since 2010 (Bloomberg).
- **China:** up to 100 million consumers have defaulted on personal debt (Bloomberg).

&nbsp;

### Oil

- **Iran said three more tankers hit mines** on Saturday. The claim is early and not confirmed (AND Magazine).
- **Friday's close:** Brent $95.84 and WTI $88.56. Brent fell about 2.5% for the week.
- **IEA:** world oil stocks rose 52 million barrels in October, the first rise since June.

## Technology

### Nvidia

- **CEO search** (CNBC, Reuters via The Star):
  - The board said it is still choosing a search firm.
  - It said it has not formally approached any candidate.
  - It called talk about named candidates too early.
  - This followed the Financial Times report that the board approached Arm CEO Rene Haas.
  - Analysts also name two inside candidates: interim CEO Colette Kress and engineer Ian Buck.
- **Congress:** three House committees called Jensen Huang to a closed-door interview on 1 December (CNN, via AND Magazine).
- **Startup funding:** Nvidia joined 105 funding rounds this year and was often the largest investor (TechCrunch, via Market News).
- **Next:** Q3 results on Tuesday, 17 November.

&nbsp;

### Anthropic IPO

- **Price range:** $70 to $78 a share.
- **Demand:** the book is reported to be about 6 times covered (20VC podcast, via Market News).
- **Valuation:** the Wall Street Journal reported that Anthropic may have to accept less than the $1.5 trillion it wanted (search summary).
- **Timing:** pricing on 18 November. Trading starts on 19 November on the Nasdaq under the ticker "ANT".
- **Products:** Anthropic launched Claude Meetings, Whiteboards and Sheets for work, and opened self-hosted Claude Managed Agents (newsletter reports).

&nbsp;

### AI agents

- **OpenAI** launched ChatGPT Wallet:
  - Its "dots" agents can buy things, with user approval for each purchase.
  - It is first open to ChatGPT Pro users in the US.
  - Spending limits and buying without approval are planned for later.
- **CrowdStrike:**
  - Added an emergency stop for AI agents that go wrong.
  - Bought two AI agent security startups for $495 million.
- **Cloudflare** open-sourced Kitesurf, a browser engine built for AI agents.
- **LangChain** raised $300 million at a $3 billion valuation.
- **AMD and Meta** deepened their partnership on gigawatt-scale AI infrastructure.
- **Google's** Gemini 4 Hydrogen model is rolling out in Search.

&nbsp;

### Robots and funding

From the Market News weekend letter. Most items are from 12–13 November.

- **Agile Robots** raised $500 million at an $8.5 billion valuation. SoftBank led the round. Nvidia also joined (Tech Times).
- **Mirendil** raised $1 billion at a reported $5 billion valuation. Kleiner Perkins and Clearlake led the round.
- **GXO** signed a letter of intent for up to 1,000 Apptronik Apollo robots over four years, from late 2027.
- **Norwest** closed a $4 billion fund, its largest ever.
- **Sequoia** is closing its London office and raising about $10 billion (20VC podcast).

&nbsp;

### Space

- **SpaceX:** Starship Flight 15 reached orbit with 26 Starlink V3 satellites (Market News).
- **ULA:** the Vulcan return-to-flight launch with 40 Amazon Leo satellites slipped to Saturday. The result is not known.

&nbsp;

### Other tech news

- **Microsoft:** the Outlook, Teams and Copilot outage on 12–13 November hit more than 70 countries. A faulty server update caused it.
- **FAA:** Friday's nationwide ground stop lasted about five hours. Officials said there was no sign of a cyberattack and no link to the Microsoft outage (CBS, via AND Magazine).
- **Apple** shipped the HomeView smart display with Siri AI at $299. It is not sold in the EU at launch.
- **Policy:** Democrats are drafting AI bills for the new Congress in January.
