# Daily News — 6 November 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Friday, 6 November 2026. **Chip stocks bounced back after Thursday's AI selloff.** **The Nasdaq rose 1.1%, but the Dow slipped.** **A hot October jobs report (+165,000) raised bets on a December Fed rate hike.** **Bitcoin fell to a seven-week low near $75,000.**

---

## Finance

### Stocks

- **US close, Friday 6 November** (StockTitan, Yahoo Finance, TheStreet):
  - Dow Jones: −0.46% (about −237 points) to 51,285.73.
  - S&P 500: +0.31% to 7,376.86.
  - Nasdaq Composite: +1.09% to 24,579.42.
- **Chips led the rebound:**
  - Nvidia closed up 2.78%, after a drop of about 9% on Thursday.
  - Big chip stocks won back over a third of Thursday's losses, on average (StockTitan).
- **The week still ended with losses.**
  - The S&P 500 had its worst week since June (Yahoo Finance).
  - Fear of tighter AI and data center rules from the new Democratic Congress is still the main worry.

&nbsp;

### Jobs report and the Fed

- **October payrolls rose 165,000** (BLS, Benzinga, Morningstar):
  - Forecast: about 65,000–70,000.
  - August and September were revised up by a combined 84,000.
- **Unemployment fell to 4.1%** from 4.2%.
- **Wages rose 0.4% in the month and 3.1% in the year.** Both beat forecasts.
- **Weak spots** (CNBC):
  - U-6 underemployment rose to 7.9% from 7.6%.
  - Long-term unemployed: 27.6% of all jobless workers. Highest share since late 2021.
- **Rate outlook:**
  - Treasury yields rose after the report (Yahoo Finance).
  - Markets see a December rate hike as likely. Sources put the odds between about 66% and 73%.

&nbsp;

### Earnings reactions

- **Winners** (StockTitan, Closing Look, CNBC):
  - Airbnb: +11.67%. Beat on earnings and revenue.
  - Cloudflare: +9.91%. Q3 revenue $782.5M beat. Q4 profit guide above forecasts.
  - JFrog: about +35% after it raised its full-year guide.
  - IonQ: about +17% after it raised its revenue guide.
- **Losers:**
  - DraftKings: −21.30%. Wider loss and a revenue miss.
  - Atlassian: about −16%. Data center revenue growth outlook slowed sharply.

&nbsp;

### Commodities

- **Gold fell about 1% to about $4,048** (Trading Economics). It gave back part of Thursday's jump.
- **Brent oil was flat at about $98** (Trading Economics).

&nbsp;

### Crypto

- **Bitcoin fell to a seven-week low** (Fortune, Crypto Briefing, CryptoBase):
  - Traded around $75,000–$76,000. Down about 5% in 24 hours.
  - About $1.26 billion in crypto positions were liquidated.
  - Causes cited: strong jobs data, ETF outflows, and a US Treasury action against the MEXC exchange.

## Technology

### AI

- **OpenAI released GPT-6 Cyber for approved security teams** (Dataconomy):
  - OpenAI rated it "Critical" under its Preparedness Framework.
  - It scored 48.5% on OpenAI's ExploitGym test, up from 32.1% for GPT-5.6 Cyber.
- **OpenAI released Images 2.6** (AI Weekly, TechRepublic):
  - It turns prompts, photos, or sketches into looping GIFs.
  - Images are made about 50% faster.
- **Google made Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash generally available.** Video clips can now be up to 15 seconds (AI Weekly).
- **Chrome gets Gemini autofill that uses Gmail data**, such as flight and order numbers. US rollout is gradual (TechRepublic).
- **AI firms back one opt-out signal for publishers** (AI Weekly):
  - OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, and Anthropic back it.
  - Anthropic already honors it. OpenAI plans to add it in the coming months.

&nbsp;

### Deals and infrastructure

- **Thinking Machines Lab is closing a $5 billion round** at a $40 billion pre-money value, led by Accel. Nvidia may invest about $2.5 billion (Business Insider, via AI Weekly).
- **Microsoft is ending a 2023 AI cloud deal with Oracle** over aging Nvidia chips (AI Weekly).
- **Voters backed data center limits** (TechRepublic):
  - 41 data center ballot measures were on midterm ballots.
  - Voters chose more limits in all but one.

&nbsp;

### Security

- **Attackers use stolen cloud credentials to resell enterprise AI access** through "LLM Mixers." Researchers saw over $100,000 of misuse in one day (TechRepublic).
