# Daily News — 18 November 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Wednesday, 18 November 2026. **Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 a share, above its $70 to $78 range. It raised about $81.3 billion, the largest IPO on record. Trading starts on Nasdaq on 19 November under "ANT".** **The Dow fell 561 points (−1.11%). The S&P 500 fell for a fifth day (−0.23%). The Nasdaq rose 0.42%.** **Nvidia fell about 2.9% after its strong results.** **Fed minutes showed almost all officials expect more rate hikes.** **Gold dropped below $3,800. Bitcoin stayed near $62,000 to $64,500.**

## Finance

### Markets

- **US indexes were mixed** (Motley Fool, Closing Look, Vista Global):
  - Dow: −561.25 points (−1.11%) to 50,198.17. UnitedHealth was the biggest drag.
  - S&P 500: about −0.23% to 7,323.63. This was its fifth loss in a row. It closed below its 200-day average.
  - Nasdaq Composite: about +0.42% to about 24,430. Chip stocks helped.
  - Russell 2000: about +0.5%.
- **Movers:**
  - Nvidia: about −2.9%. The after-hours gain from Tuesday did not hold. CoreWeave and Iren also fell (CNBC).
  - Target: about +7% in the afternoon. It beat forecasts, had its best sales growth in more than five years, and raised its full-year guidance (CNBC).
    - Adjusted earnings per share: $2.65. Without a tariff refund, $2.15. Forecast: $2.07.
  - Lowe's: beat forecasts (adjusted EPS $3.10 vs about $2.88, with a $0.34 tariff refund benefit). It raised its outlook, but the stock moved little. Sources disagree on the direction.

### Economy and the Fed

- **Fed minutes** (meeting of 27 and 28 October):
  - Almost all officials think more rate hikes will likely be needed (CNBC, Bloomberg).
  - At that meeting the Fed held rates at 3.75% to 4%. The vote was 10 to 2. Cook and Hammack wanted a quarter-point hike.
  - Officials also raised worries about AI's effect on inflation, AI debt and links to private credit (Benzinga).
- **Market bets:** futures show about a 95% chance of a quarter-point hike on 9 December, and about a 5% chance of a half-point hike.
- **Waller:** Fed Governor Waller said the Fed should be more aggressive: a half-point hike in December, or a faster pace of hikes in 2027.
- **Bonds:** the 10-year Treasury yield eased to about 5.06%. The 2-year was near 4.80%. Sources differ on small moves.

## Technology

### Anthropic IPO

- **Price:** $83 a share, above the $70 to $78 range (CNBC, Axios, TechCrunch).
- **Size:** about 980 million shares. Gross proceeds about $81.3 billion. This beats SpaceX's June IPO as the largest ever.
  - The company sells about 956.4 million shares (about $79.4 billion before fees). Existing holders sell the rest.
  - Underwriters can buy 147 million more shares. That could lift the total to about $93.5 billion.
- **Private placement:** AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC Partners will buy $26 billion of non-voting shares alongside the IPO.
- **Value:** about $1.5 trillion fully diluted. Axios gives about $1.08 trillion on a different basis.
- **Trading:** starts on Nasdaq on Thursday, 19 November, ticker "ANT".

&nbsp;

### Nvidia after results

- **Shares fell about 2.9%** despite strong results ($115.8 billion revenue, $130 billion guidance).
- **Reasons given:** worry about leadership after Jensen Huang left. The chair said on the call that Huang will not return as CEO (Semafor, CNBC).
- **China:** the outlook includes no China data center revenue. A House committee asked the Commerce Secretary to discuss chip export rules.
- The stock is down more than 13% over the past week.

### AI and other

- **OpenAI:** news digests report new models, GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol, in ChatGPT and Codex for paid users. This is not confirmed by a primary source in our search.
- **Microsoft and ByteDance:** ByteDance is reportedly in talks to spend about $500 million more on Microsoft cloud, mainly to use OpenAI models on Azure. OpenAI is reportedly unhappy (PYMNTS).
- **Microsoft Ignite:** coverage continued of the seven new MAI models launched on 17 November.
- **Google:** "Tasks in Gemini" is now free in six countries (digest report).
