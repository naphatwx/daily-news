# Daily News — 1 November 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Sunday, 1 November 2026. US markets were closed. Market numbers are from Friday's close or Sunday evening futures. **Binance reopened Bitcoin withdrawals after the $6.3 billion hack.** **The FBI blamed North Korea's TraderTraitor group.** **US stock futures rose slightly after a hard week.** **Anthropic's IPO is the big AI story heading into November.**

---

## Finance

### Stocks

- **Recap of Friday, 30 October** (CNBC, Substack market briefings):
  - S&P 500: −1.82%. Nasdaq Composite: −2.86%. It was the worst session of the week.
  - Intel fell almost 29% on weak guidance.
  - Alphabet fell after it signaled much higher capex for 2027.
- **Weekly view** (The Prof Investor):
  - The Russell 2000 rose 1.3% for the week. It was the only major US index up.
- **Fed** (market briefings):
  - The Fed held rates at 3.75%–4% on Wednesday, 28 October.
  - Two voters dissented. They wanted a quarter-point hike.
- **Sunday evening futures** (CNBC):
  - S&P 500 futures: +0.3%.
  - Nasdaq 100 futures: +0.4%.
- **Asia, early Monday** (TradingKey):
  - Japan's Nikkei and Korea's KOSPI opened lower. KOSPI fell more than 1%.
  - Kioxia fell about 12% on a warning about flash memory prices.
  - Samsung Electronics and SoftBank fell about 3%.
- **UK weekly review** (Share Talk):
  - The FTSE 100 closed Friday up 0.1% at 10,729.78.
- **Week ahead:**
  - More earnings.
  - US midterm elections on Tuesday, 3 November.
  - October jobs report on Friday, 6 November.

&nbsp;

### Commodities

- **Oil stays high on Hormuz risk** (FinanceFeeds, Kitco, The National):
  - Late Friday, Brent was near $116–$117 and WTI near $104.
  - Brent was on track for a fourth monthly gain in a row.
  - Kitco says the 10-year Treasury yield was near 5.0% on 30 October. High oil makes the Fed's inflation job harder.
- **OPEC+ was due to meet on Sunday, 1 November** (CNBC, Enerdata):
  - At its 4 October meeting, it kept November output targets steady.
  - No result from the 1 November meeting was found at the time of writing.
- **Gold fell below $4,000 on Friday** (Kitco).

&nbsp;

### Crypto

- **Binance reopened withdrawals in phases** (CoinDesk, CBS News, crypto.news):
  - Phase one: Bitcoin and assets on 15 other blockchains.
  - More chains will reopen after security checks. There is no fixed date.
  - Binance says it has covered about 95% of the stolen amount, up from 90%. It will absorb the losses.
- **The FBI blamed North Korea's TraderTraitor group** for the hack (CoinDesk).
- **Binance had $5.8 billion in net outflows** in the 24 hours after reopening (Cointelegraph).
  - Binance says activity is now stabilizing.
- **The hacker is still moving funds** (crypto.news):
  - Lookonchain says hacker wallets are swapping about 970,522 stolen SOL (about $96 million) into Bitcoin.
  - About $46 million of SOL went through the Relay protocol.
- **Bitcoin held near $77,800–$78,400** (CoinDesk, Yahoo Finance).
  - Bitfinex analysts see $75,000 as key support.
  - A real recovery needs Bitcoin back above $80,000.

&nbsp;

### Deals and IPOs

- **Anthropic IPO** (Built In, IBTimes, Crypto Briefing):
  - The S-1 became public on 26 October. Ticker: ANT, on Nasdaq.
  - The listing could come as early as November. No date is confirmed.
  - The S-1 leaves price and valuation blank. Press reports say $1.5 trillion to $2 trillion.
  - Last private valuation: $965 billion (May 2026).
  - H1 2026 revenue: about $16.3 billion, up from about $1.2 billion in H1 2025.
  - 22 underwriters, led by Goldman Sachs.
- **Saudi Aramco closed its deal for 49% of HUMAIN**, the Saudi AI company (AI Weekly).
  - The price is about $2.6 billion in cash and other assets. PIF keeps 51%.
