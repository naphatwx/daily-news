# Daily News — 20 November 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Friday, 20 November 2026. **US stocks had their worst day in months as the AI trade broke down. The Nasdaq fell 5.3% and wiped out its 2026 gain. The S&P 500 fell 3.7% for a seventh day in a row and closed in a correction. The Dow lost about 950 points and is also about 10% below its record.** **Nvidia and AMD each fell more than 13%.** **Anthropic fell 8.5% on its second trading day to $88.91, close to its $83 IPO price.** **Gold hit its lowest level since September 2025. Bitcoin fell toward $58,000 to $62,000.**

---

## Finance

### Markets

- **US indexes fell sharply** (AP via Yakima Herald, CNBC):
  - S&P 500: −265.44 points (−3.7%) to 6,910.39. Seventh loss in a row. It is now in a correction (more than 10% below its record).
  - Dow: −949.73 points (−1.9%) to 48,147.33. Also about 10% below its record.
  - Nasdaq Composite: −5.3% to 22,617.73. It is now down for 2026.
- **The week:** the S&P 500 lost about 8.6%, its worst week since April 2025. The Nasdaq lost about 10%.
- **Why stocks fell:**
  - AI sell-off. Investors fear heavy data-center spending will not pay off. Some say Anthropic's IPO pulled money away from other AI stocks (Admiral Markets).
  - Nvidia news. Reuters reported that SEC enforcement staff are looking at Nvidia's accounting. Nvidia was already down about 7% before that report.
  - Fed fears. A hot October inflation report and comments from Fed Governor Lisa Cook raised bets on a December rate hike (Nacionale).
- **Movers:**
  - Nvidia: down more than 13%. One source gives a close of $125.53 (−14%). Other figures differ.
  - AMD: down more than 13%. A short seller's report linked its firmware to a blacklisted Chinese server maker.
  - Broadcom and Micron: each down more than 11%.

&nbsp;

### Economy and bonds

- **10-year Treasury yield:** fell to about 4.92%, down from above 5% on Thursday (CappNotes). Lower yields did not stop the selling.
- **Fed bets:** traders still lean toward a December rate hike.

&nbsp;

### Oil and metals

- **Gold** fell to its lowest level since September 2025:
  - Spot gold traded between about $3,506 and $3,586 per ounce. Nexo gave a low of $3,513.54. Figures differ by source and time.
- **Oil** was about flat:
  - Brent: about $91 to $92.83. WTI: about $82 to $83.62 (Nexo, Fortune via Biztoc, Trading Economics).

&nbsp;

### Crypto

- **Bitcoin:** fell. Nexo put it near $62,000, about 4% lower on the week. GoldPrice.com showed $58,588 (−5.6%) late in the day.
- **ETF flows:** no source in our search reported Friday's spot ETF flows.

## Technology

### Anthropic's second trading day

- **Close:** $88.91, down 8.5% (Nasdaq data, StockAnalysis).
- **Range:** about $83.05 to $92.56. The low came within cents of the $83 IPO price (Robinhood, Benzinga).
- **Why it fell:** first-day excitement faded and AI stocks sold off broadly (Motley Fool, CNBC).
- **Data center deal:** Riot Platforms confirmed that its $28 billion, 15-year data center lease in Corsicana, Texas is with Anthropic (digest).
