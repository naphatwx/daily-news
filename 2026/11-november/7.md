&nbsp;

### Berkshire Hathaway Q3 results

- **Operating profit fell 5.8% to $12.7 billion**, from $13.5 billion a year ago (CNBC, NewsBytes).
- **Insurance was the main drag** (The Insurer, Forbes):
  - Insurance underwriting profit fell 42.9% to $1.35 billion.
  - GEICO's combined ratio got worse: 91.4%, up from 84.3%.
- **Other parts grew.** Railroad, energy, and manufacturing profits rose from a year ago (Forbes).
- **Net profit fell 28% to $22.1 billion.** Smaller gains on the stock portfolio caused most of the drop.
- **Buybacks jumped to about $9.6 billion**, more than double Q2 (CNBC, BigGo Finance):
  - It is CEO Greg Abel's first big buyback.
  - Analysts read it as a sign that Abel thinks the shares are cheap.
- **Cash fell after the Taylor Morrison deal** (StockTitan). Reported totals differ by source.

&nbsp;

### AI funding and IPOs

- **OpenAI raised $11 billion at a $1.25 trillion value**, below its first target (RUDI, AI Toolly).
- **Anthropic IPO talk:** investors now discuss a value of about $1.2–1.3 trillion, down from earlier talk (AI Toolly).
- **Ramp data: top AI spenders slowed down** (SaaStr):
  - The top 1% of Ramp firms spent less per worker in September than in July.
  - Anthropic's added spend per quarter fell from $6.8B in Q2 to $5.4B in Q3.
  - Ramp's economist says lower model prices explain most of it, not lower use.

&nbsp;

### Economy

- **Bank of England: AI adds only about 0.5% to UK productivity over three years** (AI Weekly):
  - Firms are hiring fewer people instead of firing staff.

## Technology

### AI

- **Google DeepMind's agents found weak spots in old 1024-bit RSA** (AI Weekly, AI Agents Directory):
  - Modern 2048-bit RSA keys are not affected.
  - DeepMind says it shows AI agents can speed up code-breaking research from years to weeks.
- **Perplexity launched "Teammates"** (AI Agents Directory):
  - Always-on AI agents for Max subscribers.
- **Meta rolled out new Muse memory and focus models** to all Muse and Meta AI users (AI Weekly).
- **Meta added parental controls** to its Meta AI app (AI Weekly).
- **AWS made its Kiro coding agents generally available** (AI Weekly).
- **OpenAI GPT-6 Cyber follow-up** (Gadgetbridge):
  - More coverage of the "Critical"-rated security model released on Friday.
  - It is only open to approved security teams. There is no public API.
