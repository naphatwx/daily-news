# Daily News — 23 November 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Monday, 23 November 2026. **US stocks had their best day since April 2025. Nvidia's board brought back Jensen Huang as CEO before the open, and Nvidia jumped about 31.5%.** **The Nasdaq rose 6.5%, the S&P 500 rose 4.7% and the Dow gained about 1,448 points.** **Anthropic did not join the rally. It fell about 1.4% to near $87.64.** **Gold bounced about 2% to 3%. Oil fell to its lowest level since mid-August. Bitcoin rose to about $61,000 to $63,000.**

---

## Finance

### Markets

- **US indexes rose sharply** (CNBC, 24/7 Wall St, CappNotes):
  - S&P 500: +326.78 points (+4.73%) to 7,237.17. Best day since April 2025. This ends a seven-day losing run.
  - Dow: +1,447.98 points (+3.01%) to 49,595.31.
  - Nasdaq Composite: +1,471.83 points (+6.51%) to 24,089.56.
  - One source gives slightly different figures (S&P 500 at 7,243.94, +4.83%). This may be a different time snapshot.
- **Why stocks rose:**
  - Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO (see Technology below). Chip and AI stocks bounced after their worst week since 2001 (Yahoo Finance).
  - Buyers returned after Friday's sell-off pushed the S&P 500 and Dow into a correction.
- **Movers:**
  - Nvidia: up about 31.5%.
  - AMD and Broadcom: both rose with chip stocks. AMD also announced an AI deal with Saudi Arabia's HUMAIN (see below). We did not find confirmed closing figures.
  - Anthropic: fell (see Technology below).

### Economy and bonds

- **10-year Treasury yield:** edged lower to about 4.92% (Babypips). Some outlets said yields bounced, so the direction is not clear.
- **2-year Treasury yield:** rose more than 7 basis points. The stock rebound made a December Fed rate hike look more likely (Babypips).
- **US dollar:** rose against most major currencies. CoinDesk said the dollar index hit its highest level since March 2025.
- **Fed debate:** Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent warned the Fed against raising rates too much while tech stocks are weak (AI digest).

&nbsp;

### Oil and metals

- **Gold** bounced after a drop of about 9% last week:
  - Up about 2% to 2.7%, to about $3,600 to $3,635 per ounce (Babypips, TalkMarkets).
- **Oil** fell:
  - WTI: down about 3.5% to 4%, to about $80 to $81. This is its weakest level since mid-August (CoinDesk).
  - Gulf shipping attacks and risk near the Strait of Hormuz still keep some premium in prices (NordFX).

&nbsp;

### Crypto

- **Bitcoin:** bottomed near $55,000 over the weekend, then rose.
  - Babypips: up about 3.9% to near $61,167. It briefly spiked toward $64,000 and then faded.
  - CoinDesk: near $63,000. It said bitcoin rose only a little compared with the Nasdaq.

## Technology

### Jensen Huang returns to Nvidia

- **What happened:** Nvidia's board voted unanimously to bring back Jensen Huang as president, CEO and board director, effective immediately (ABC News, PBS).
- **Background:**
  - Huang resigned less than two weeks earlier. Reports linked him to a suspected Chinese intelligence effort (ABC News).
  - At last Tuesday's earnings call, the board said it did not plan to bring him back.
  - An independent board committee is still reviewing the matter.
- **Why the board changed its mind:**
  - The board said it was satisfied with Huang's candor and his ability to lead.
  - CNBC reported that a personal promise from President Trump on Sunday sealed the deal. The board had asked for assurance that Huang was not a national security risk.
  - Other reported reasons: the falling stock price, and doubts from some investors and staff about interim CEO Colette Kress (The Hill).

### Anthropic's third trading day

- **Close:** down about 1.4%, near $87.64 (StockTitan, Bloomberg). This is about 5.6% above the $83 IPO price.
- **Trading:** shares rose before the open, then gave up early gains. The chip rally passed the new listing by (Bloomberg, Yahoo Finance).
- **Concerns:** debut fatigue and its high valuation. The stock is about 11% below its first-day close of $97.17 (24/7 Wall St).

### AI and other

Most items below come from AI news digests. The UN item comes from UN News. We could not confirm the others with major outlets.

- **UN:** Secretary-General António Guterres asked countries to start talks on a global AI safety framework. He warned of a historic shift in power (UN News).
- **US and China:** Trump and Xi reportedly discussed keeping humans in control of military AI decisions.
- **AMD:** AMD and Saudi Arabia's HUMAIN announced a partnership worth up to $10 billion. It will deploy 500 megawatts of AMD-based AI computing in Saudi Arabia and the US over five years.
- **OpenAI:** launched ChatGPT for Sales. Its chief scientist proposed industry-wide limits on AI development until safety monitoring catches up.
- **Perplexity:** launched an always-on Monitor API for AI agents.
- **Mistral AI:** Deutsche Telekom committed €100 million.
- **US states:** governors asked Congress for a federal AI baseline law.
- **South Korea:** the education minister resigned after AI cheating disrupted the national university entrance exam.
- **Security:** Dragos sent about 2 million test prompts to AI services from July to November. It said it found prompt theft at three AI providers.
