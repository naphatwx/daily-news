## Technology

### Nvidia CEO probe

- **Nvidia's board opened an independent investigation** (Axios, Fox Business, Newsweek):
  - It follows reports that Jensen Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort.
  - A committee of independent directors with outside lawyers will run it.
  - The board says it has no reason to think national security or Nvidia secrets were harmed.
  - The board says it still has confidence in Huang. He stays CEO.
- **Huang admitted an affair in a letter to staff** (Axios):
  - He apologized and said he will fully cooperate with the probe.
  - Reports name the woman as a Megaspeed executive. The spy claims are allegations.
- **Congress wants answers.** House committee leaders asked Huang to brief them on 18 November (Newsweek).
- **Market reaction:** an Nvidia price tracker on Hyperliquid fell about 2% over the weekend (CNBC).

### AI infrastructure

- **Anthropic bought $3.4 billion of gas generator orders from OnePWR** (Financial Times, via The Morning Brief and AI Weekly):
  - The orders total 1.7 gigawatts of power.
  - The deal was agreed in late September and reported on 8 November.
  - Reuters could not confirm the report. These are orders, not running power plants.
- **Anthropic's IPO filing names natural gas supply as a risk** (Wired, via AI Weekly):
  - Some planned data centers will use on-site gas turbines.
  - Gas supply or turbine delays could hold up or cancel a site.
