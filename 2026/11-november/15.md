# Daily News — 15 November 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Sunday, 15 November 2026. **US stock markets were closed for the weekend.** **US stock futures opened a little higher on Sunday evening.** **Bitcoin fell to about $62,100, its lowest since July, then rose back near $65,000.** **Iran said a supertanker with more than 2 million barrels of crude sank after it hit a mine.** **The Financial Times reported that Jensen Huang is discussing a possible return to Nvidia.** **This week: Nvidia reports results on 17 November and Anthropic prices its IPO on 18 November.**

---

## Finance

### Markets

- **US and most world stock markets were closed** on Sunday.
- **US futures opened higher on Sunday evening** (CNBC):
  - Early: Nasdaq-100 +0.6%, S&P 500 +0.3%, Dow +71 points (+0.1%).
  - Later: Nasdaq-100 +0.4%, S&P 500 +0.1%, Dow about flat.
- **Last week, for reference:**
  - The Dow, S&P 500 and Nasdaq each rose more than 2%. This was their biggest weekly gain since August (CNBC).
  - Nvidia fell about 13% in the week. It is down about 20% so far in November.
- **Asia, early Monday:** shares fell before the Nvidia results (Reuters, via Yahoo Finance). The Nikkei opened lower after Japan's economy shrank by surprise (MLQ).

&nbsp;

### The week ahead

- **Tuesday, 17 November:**
  - Nvidia third-quarter results, after the close. This is the first report since Jensen Huang left.
  - Forecast: about $2.47 a share. Revenue forecasts range from about $108.7 billion to $108.85 billion.
  - Interim CEO Colette Kress is expected to lead the call.
  - US retail sales.
- **Wednesday, 18 November:**
  - Minutes of the Fed's 27–28 October meeting.
  - Anthropic prices its IPO.
- **Thursday, 19 November:**
  - Anthropic starts trading on the Nasdaq under the ticker "ANT".
  - Walmart results.
- **Also reporting this week:** Palo Alto Networks, Disney, Home Depot and Target (Stocktwits).

&nbsp;

### The Fed

- **Officials sound ready to raise rates:**
  - Boston Fed President Susan Collins said tighter policy is coming.
  - San Francisco Fed President Mary Daly said she leans toward raising rates.
- **Kevin Warsh** is due to give a speech this week (Stocktwits).

&nbsp;

### Crypto

- **Bitcoin hit its lowest level since July, then recovered** (Cryptoticker, Aktualita):
  - Low: $62,086.
  - Later on Sunday: about $64,700 to $65,400.
  - Reasons given: outflows from US spot Bitcoin ETFs, liquidations of leveraged positions, and doubt about US interest rates.
- **Sources differ:** a Yahoo Finance history row shows a close of $65,448.30 and a low of $62,093.52 for 15 November.

&nbsp;

### Oil

- **Iran's Revolutionary Guards made new claims** about tankers in the Strait of Hormuz. None is confirmed by an independent source.
  - A supertanker with more than 2 million barrels of crude sank in the southern Gulf after it hit a mine (Anadolu, via A News).
  - A second tanker also hit a mine.
  - Three tankers caught fire after they hit mines on 14 November (bne IntelliNews).
  - Iranian state TV said five tankers hit mines in 48 hours.
  - The Guards said the ships had turned off their navigation systems and tried to cross unseen.
- **Oil fell anyway:**
  - Brent for January: −0.4% to $95.45 a barrel at 6:21 p.m. ET on Sunday.
  - WTI: about $87 (InvestingLive).
  - Reason given: the IEA expects supply to exceed demand in the fourth quarter.

## Technology

### Nvidia

- **Huang may come back:** the Financial Times reported that Jensen Huang is discussing a possible return (via Bloomberg's market wrap).
- **Background:** Huang resigned as president, CEO and director on 12 November. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO.
- **Investor view:** investors expect Kress to be "much more conservative" (newsletter report).
- **Anthropic stake:** Nvidia agreed to buy about $10 billion of Anthropic shares in a $26 billion private placement beside the IPO. Some analysts doubt the deal after the CEO change. Others expect it to go ahead (Bloomberg, Fortune).

&nbsp;

### Anthropic IPO

- **Size:** 825 million shares at $70 to $78 each, from the amended S-1 filing.
- **Timing:** pricing on 18 November. Trading starts on 19 November.
- **Models:** newsletters report that Claude Fable 5.2 is open to all users, with a stronger model, Mythos 5.2, for approved users.

&nbsp;

### AI

- **OpenAI:** ChatGPT Wallet lets agents pay with tokenized Visa, Mastercard and Amex cards (newsletter report).
- **Google DeepMind** reported AI-written security proofs for compiled Signal protocol code.
- **AWS** released SGLang-Neuron for its Trainium chips.
- **Malaysia** plans laws to track data center power use and to stop smuggling of AI hardware.
- **Courts:** a database now lists more than 2,280 cases of AI hallucinations in court filings worldwide.
- **Microsoft:** the Microsoft 365 outage reached 74 countries and cut ChatGPT and Claude off from Outlook (newsletter report).

&nbsp;

### Funding

- **Fractile** (UK AI chips) raised $720 million in a Series C round (TechCrunch, 11 November):
  - Valuation: about $7 billion.
  - Leads: Thrive Capital, Founders Fund, Lightspeed and Redpoint.
  - First chips are planned for 2027.
- **Operand** (AI due diligence for private equity) raised $31 million. Khosla led the round at a reported valuation of about $220 million.

&nbsp;

### Space

- **SpaceX:** the Market News letter leads with Starship Flight 15. It says the ship was caught after a return from orbit for the first time.
