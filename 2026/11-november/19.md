# Daily News — 19 November 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Thursday, 19 November 2026. **Anthropic started trading on Nasdaq as "ANT". It opened at $111.50 and closed at $97.17, up about 17% from its $83 IPO price. Its value at the close was about $1.76 trillion.** **The wider market fell hard. The Dow lost about 1,100 points (−2.2%). The S&P 500 fell 2% for a sixth day in a row, its longest losing streak since April 2024. The Nasdaq fell 2.2%.** **Fed rate hike fears, a 10-year yield above 5%, and an AI and chip sell-off drove the drop.** **Gold fell to a 14-month low. Bitcoin fell toward $61,600.**

---

## Finance

### Markets

- **US indexes fell sharply** (Motley Fool, Washington Post, Yahoo Finance):
  - Dow: about −1,101 points (−2.19%) to about 49,097. 29 of 30 Dow stocks fell. One intraday snapshot gave −1,084 points.
  - S&P 500: −2.00% to about 7,177. Sixth loss in a row.
  - Nasdaq Composite: −2.23% to about 23,886. It is about 13.5% below its early-October peak.
  - Nasdaq 100: −2.2%, about 15% below its October record (Bloomberg).
- **Why stocks fell:**
  - Fed fears. Minutes showed some officials open to hikes larger than a quarter point. Chicago Fed President Goolsbee said the Fed must stop the AI boom from overheating the economy.
  - Tech profit-taking. Chip stocks gave up early gains soon after Anthropic began trading.
  - Weak data. The Philadelphia Fed factory index fell to 6.3 from 20.9. Its prices-paid gauge rose to 58.2.
- **Movers:**
  - AMD: about −7.7% after a Hunterbrook report tied it to a blacklisted Chinese server maker. Hunterbrook Capital holds a short position in AMD.
  - Nvidia: fell again as investors weighed the CEO change despite strong results (Motley Fool).
  - UnitedHealth and Cigna: fell hard again on Medicare Advantage audit worries. UnitedHealth led the Dow lower.
  - Walmart: slipped even though revenue beat forecasts and it raised its sales outlook.

### Economy and bonds

- **10-year Treasury yield:** touched 5.11%, then eased to about 5.05%.
- **Fed bets:** traders see a December rate hike as almost certain.
- **Jobless claims:** no source in our search reported the weekly figure.

### Oil and metals

- **Gold** hit its lowest level in about 14 months on a strong dollar and Fed hike bets:
  - Spot gold traded between about $3,690 and $3,745. One recap gave a low near $3,660. Figures differ by source, time and contract (spot vs futures).
- **Oil** was mixed. Strait of Hormuz tanker incidents pushed prices up. Rising US stocks pushed them down.
  - Brent: near $92.53. WTI: near $84.77 (Investorideas, morning figures).

&nbsp;

### Crypto

- **Bitcoin** fell about 2% to about $61,635 in the evening, close to $60,000 (Motley Fool). Earlier reports put it between $62,000 and $64,000.
  - US spot bitcoin ETFs lost more than $300 million on Wednesday. Outflows for 2026 now pass $2.5 billion.
  - The Fear and Greed Index was 10, "Extreme Fear" (Cryptonomist).
- **Ether:** below $1,800 (KuCoin).
- **Total crypto market:** about $2.14 to $2.2 trillion.

## Technology

### Anthropic's first trading day

- **Open:** $111.50 at about 11:48 a.m. ET, 34% above the $83 IPO price (CNN, Yahoo Finance).
- **High:** about $115, which briefly valued the company at more than $2 trillion. Sources give $114.99 or $115.10.
- **Close:** $97.17, up 17.07% (Forbes, Bloomberg).
- **Value at close:** about $1.76 trillion (CNN).
- **Offering recap:** 980 million Class A shares at $83, raising $81.3 billion, the largest IPO on record.
- **Index entry:** Anthropic will join at least six big indexes, including the Nasdaq 100, within 5 to 15 days of listing (Reuters, via Khaleej Times).
