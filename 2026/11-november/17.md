# Daily News — 17 November 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Tuesday, 17 November 2026. **US stocks fell for a fourth day: the Dow lost 848.75 points (−1.64%), the S&P 500 1.0% and the Nasdaq 0.7%.** **Software stocks led the fall. Salesforce dropped about 9%.** **After the close, Nvidia beat forecasts: revenue was $115.8 billion and it guided about $130 billion for next quarter. Shares rose after hours.** **October retail sales rose 0.2%, in line with forecasts.** **Bitcoin stayed near $64,000.** **Next: Anthropic prices its IPO on 18 November and starts trading on 19 November.**

---

## Finance

### Markets

- **US indexes closed lower for a fourth day** (AP via WTOP, TradingKey):
  - Dow: −848.75 points (−1.64%) to 50,759.42. It fell more than 1,000 points at the low.
  - S&P 500: −76.06 points (−1.03%) to 7,340.53, its lowest close since late July.
  - Nasdaq Composite: −181.37 points (−0.74%) to 24,328.38.
- **Fear rose again:** the VIX rose for a fourth day. Its intraday high was 27.73. Closing figures differ by source (about 23 to 26).
- **Software stocks fell hard:**
  - Salesforce: about −9.5%, its worst day since May 2024 (Kiplinger, via Options Oracle).
  - Intuit and ServiceNow: each more than −6%.
  - Reasons given: security worries and fear that AI will replace software tools.
- **Other movers:**
  - Home Depot: about −2.5%. It guided full-year sales growth to the top of its range but margin to the low end. Richard McPhail becomes CEO on 23 November. Ted Decker stays as chair.
  - UnitedHealth: about −5.4% after new anti-fraud proposals for Medicare and Medicaid.
  - Bill Holdings: about +13% (24/7 Wall St).
  - Nvidia: closed at $155.46 (−0.44%) before its results (Motley Fool).
- **After hours:** Nvidia, CoreWeave and Nebius rose after Nvidia's results (CNBC).

### Economy and the Fed

- **October retail sales:** +0.2%, as forecast. September was +0.8% (Commerce Department, via PBS).
  - Excluding autos: +0.6%.
  - Auto dealers: −1.6%. Gas stations: +1.5%.
  - After inflation, sales volume fell about 0.1%.
- **Fed:** the next meeting is on 8 and 9 December. Markets still expect a rate rise.
- **Bonds:** the 10-year Treasury yield was about flat, near 5.10% to 5.13%. The 2-year was near 4.82%.
- **Dollar:** the dollar index was near 103.5, close to a 19-month high.

&nbsp;

### Oil and metals

- **Oil rose a little after big swings** (Bloomberg via Rigzone):
  - Brent: +0.8% to $93.14 a barrel.
  - WTI: +0.3% to $86.01.
  - Reason given: attacks in the Gulf continue. US–Iran talks have not ended the war.
- **Gold:** about flat, near $3,850 to $3,880. Sources disagree on the direction.
- **Silver:** about +1.5% to $47.72 (Centrino Capital).

&nbsp;

### Crypto

- **Bitcoin** traded near $63,500 to $65,000 during the day (CoinDesk, Fortune). It was near $64,000 in the evening (Motley Fool).
  - It is still below its 200-week average of about $68,000.
  - A weekend sell-off had pushed it as low as $62,000.
- **Ether:** below $1,800.
- **ETF flows:** US spot bitcoin ETFs took in a net $7.9 million on Monday. This ended seven days of outflows (Farside, via CoinDesk).

## Technology

### Nvidia results

- **Third quarter of fiscal 2027** (quarter ended 25 October), reported after the close:
  - Revenue: $115.8 billion, up 103% from a year ago. Forecast: about $109.1 billion.
  - Data center revenue: $107.6 billion.
  - Adjusted earnings per share: $2.68. Forecast: $2.48 (LSEG).
  - Adjusted gross margin: 74.9%.
- **Outlook:** about $130 billion for the next quarter, plus or minus 3%. Forecasts were about $125 billion. The outlook leaves out China data center sales.
  - TradingKey reports gross margin may fall to 71% to 72% next quarter.
- **Vera Rubin:** reports describe demand for the new chip as very strong (Benzinga).
- **Shares** rose about 2% to 4% after hours.
- **Leadership:** this is the first report since Jensen Huang resigned. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO.

&nbsp;

### Anthropic IPO

- **Timing:** pricing on Wednesday, 18 November. Trading on Nasdaq starts on Thursday, 19 November, under the ticker "ANT".
- **Price range:** $70 to $78 a share, for up to 825 million shares (CBS News). CNBC sources say the price may land above this range.
- **Owners:** Amazon and Google will hold about 28% together after the IPO (Bloomberg).
- **Nvidia** reportedly plans to buy about $10 billion of stock after the IPO (AP).
- **Analysts:** New Street Research started coverage with a buy rating and a $105 target (CNBC).

&nbsp;

### AI and other

- **Microsoft Ignite:** Microsoft AI launched seven new MAI models for code, security, images, voice and transcription. It pitches them as cheaper than OpenAI and Anthropic models (Axios). The speed and quality claims come from Microsoft's own tests.
