## Technology

### AI models

- **OpenAI** released **GPT-6 Astra**, its most powerful frontier model, rated "Critical" on cybersecurity capability — it can find unknown vulnerabilities and exploit hardened systems with limited human oversight. Extra safety controls were added.
- **Microsoft** launched **MAI-Transcribe-2** at **$0.10 per audio hour**, a 72% price cut, covering 60 languages.
- **HUMAIN** (Saudi Arabia) unveiled a **428-billion-parameter Arabic model** built on Chinese weights.

### Chips and infrastructure

- **Nvidia** agreed to buy **Hugging Face for $12.93 billion** — a large bet on open AI models.
- **Broadcom** posted **$16.7 billion** in quarterly AI semiconductor revenue; custom accelerators were about 73% of that.
- **Crusoe** raised **$3 billion at a $30 billion valuation**, and signed a ~$13 billion five-year GPU deal with Jane Street.
- **Figure** committed **$3.5 billion** to Nscale for GPUs — up to 100,000 Nvidia processors for humanoid robot training.
- Inference chip startups took ~**$3.62B across eight deals**, roughly 67% of all disclosed AI chip funding.
- **Microsoft Project Zenith**: high-memory PCs (64GB+) that run large models locally.

### Autonomous vehicles

- **NHTSA** opened a safety probe into nearly **1,000 Tesla Cybercabs** just after their Austin rollout — the cars have no steering wheel or pedals.

### Policy

- Senators **Bernie Sanders** and **Greg Casar** proposed a permanent ban on superintelligent AI plus a temporary pause on advanced systems until safety rules exist.
- **EU** defense officials pushed back on cutting reliance on U.S. cloud providers, citing NATO interoperability.
- **China** removed **5.61 million** pieces of AI-generated content.

### Other

- **ISRO** launched the **EOS-05** Earth-observation satellite after a seven-month gap.
- **Quantum Motion** expanded its Series C for silicon-based fault-tolerant quantum computers.
- **Qualcomm** joined **Ultrahuman's $70 million** round, betting on smart rings as an AI platform.
- **IFA 2026** centered on embodied AI — robots, smart devices, wearables.
- **Trezor** breach disclosure widened to about **67,000 customers** via logistics provider ShipMonk; phishing risk.
- **Adobe**: Anil Chakravarthy becomes CEO in December.
