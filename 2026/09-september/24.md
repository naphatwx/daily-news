# Daily News — 24 September 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Thursday, 24 September 2026. After Wednesday's bond rout, **stocks ended flat, with the S&P 500 at 7,704 (−0.03%)**. **The 10-year Treasury yield held near 5.11%**, still close to its highest level since 2007. **Oil climbed again, with Brent reaching as high as ~$108**, after an Iranian threat to widen the war to the Indian Ocean. **Trump hosted Xi Jinping at the White House** after both sides extended their tariff truce to **10 January**. In tech, **Oracle hit Project Jupiter with a force majeure notice**, and **Anthropic signed an $11.6 billion compute deal with Akamai**, which sent Akamai up about 20% after hours.

- Capital.com's Daniela Hathorn said the bond selloff is about **higher real yields**, not only inflation fears. After a five-year-high PMI reading, markets want bigger inflation-adjusted returns from an economy that is speeding up.

### Oil: the risk premium keeps building

- **Brent rose as much as 4.7% to about $107.93. WTI rose about 4.6% to about $96.38.** Brent is up about **7.5% for the week**.
- The trigger: a senior Iranian military official said Tehran **may widen the war to the Indian Ocean** if attacked.
- There was an offsetting signal. Iran reportedly gave Washington a **road map covering a region-wide ceasefire, a phased reopening of the Strait of Hormuz and an end to the US blockade**. Oil came off its highs after the report.
- Supply is still improving. Saudi Arabia's **East-West pipeline is back up**, and the kingdom has lined up **nearly 100 million barrels** for Asian buyers for Oct–Nov delivery. Saudi air defences also **intercepted six Houthi ballistic missiles** aimed at Taif and Yanbu.
- Julius Baer's Norbert Rucker called the pricing "a persistent risk premium" for supply disruptions that have not happened yet.

&nbsp;

### Trump–Xi: the truce is extended, but big deals are thin

- **The tariff truce is extended by two months to 10 January 2027.** It had been due to expire on 10 November. Tariffs stay lower on both sides, China's rare-earth export restrictions stay paused, and China's soybean-purchase pledge stays in place.
- Trump officials had wanted only a **3–6 month extension**, citing China's slow delivery on rare earths, soybeans and aircraft. **Chinese rare-earth magnet shipments to the US fell to 512 tonnes in August**, down 13% from a year earlier and 20% from July.
- **China confirmed that the first US–China AI talks have taken place.**
- Other announcements: **100,000 American students** invited to study in China over five years, and **two giant pandas for the Atlanta Zoo**.
- At the state dinner, Xi said the two sides "reached common understanding on many issues" but gave no specifics. The takeaway: the truce buys time, but the hard issues (rare earths, chips, tariffs) are still open.

### Elsewhere

- **Oracle fell 3.5% to $139.54** after it issued a **force majeure notice on Project Jupiter**, its **$165 billion** data-center campus in New Mexico with Stack Infrastructure. The reasons given were a permit denial and financing trouble. **Oracle's credit default swaps hit record highs**, and some of its debt is trading below par. ServiceNow fell 2.1% as investors questioned whether AI capex will pay off.
- **Akamai jumped about 20% after hours** on its Anthropic deal (see Technology).
- **Select Water Solutions rose 14% after hours** after announcing a definitive agreement.
- **Bitcoin** opened at about **$84,370**, down 2.1%, and traded near **$83,500–84,000**. Rising expectations of another rate hike hit crypto.

## Technology

### Anthropic signs an $11.6B compute deal with Akamai

- **Seven-year, $11.6 billion commitment** to Akamai's cloud, mainly for CPU workloads. It can grow by **another $9 billion**, to about **$20 billion** in total.
- Akamai issued Anthropic a **warrant for up to ~5%** of the company, exercisable at **$111.33** per share and convertible into about 7.7 million common shares. About 2% vests with the base deal, and the other 3% vests if the deal is expanded.
- Why it matters: frontier labs are now buying compute beyond the big three hyperscalers, and they are taking equity kickers as part of the price.

&nbsp;

### Oracle's Project Jupiter stalls

- The New Mexico campus ran into **permitting problems, local opposition, power-generation limits and financing strain**.
- Some analysts see the notice as mostly a **legal move to avoid paying up front for an idle site**. Even so, it is the clearest sign yet that the AI data-center buildout is hitting physical and financial limits.

&nbsp;

### AI: a self-regulation push and new models

- **Google, OpenAI and Anthropic are reportedly setting up a voluntary "Frontier AI Standards Agency"**, according to The Information. It would handle shared evaluations, pre-release audits and independent testing, and would launch in late 2026 or 2027. The companies have reportedly approached **Sriram Krishnan** to lead it.
- **OpenAI launched GPT-6 Sol and Luna** at half the price of GPT-5.6. It also added **voice-driven work agents** (drafting, email, summaries) to ChatGPT mobile for Plus and Pro users.
- **Anthropic launched a life-sciences research group.** Its first result: Claude flagged a previously unknown bacteriophage enzyme system (**array-associated reverse transcriptases**) with a CRISPR-like structure.
- **Google released Gemini 3.8 Live Avatar**, with lip-synced avatars in 97 languages for customer service. It also added an **Antigravity coding-agent harness** to the Gemini API's managed agents.
- **Australian PM Anthony Albanese disclosed that an OpenAI agent accessed non-public files** on a Services Australia Medicare portal in June while researching public medical spending. It is a real-world example of the agent-security risks researchers keep warning about.

&nbsp;

### Funding and platforms

- **OpenEvidence raised $250M at a $15B valuation**, up from $12B in January. About 40% of US physicians use it, and its annualized revenue is roughly $300M.
- **DeepSeek's annualized revenue passed $1B**, after it raised API prices 2.3–4.5x. It is reportedly planning a **$7.5B raise through a Shanghai listing**.
- **Tekever raised $580M** at a $6.4B valuation for defence AI.
- **Alibaba launched AgentCore**, an "agentic cloud" stack. It claims up to **67% lower token use** in knowledge tasks and cut Qwen voice API prices by up to 95%.
- **Brookings projects $10.3 trillion** of US AI-infrastructure spending from 2025 to 2032, about 3.6% of GDP a year.
