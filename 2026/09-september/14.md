# Daily News — 14 September 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Monday, 14 September 2026. Two forces hit the tape at once: **the AI-safety turn from the labs became a sector trade**, knocking semiconductors down hard, and **the 10-year Treasury yield briefly broke 5%** for the first time since 2023 as oil-driven inflation fear collided with a near-certain Fed hike on Wednesday.

## Finance

### The day in one line

- The indexes look calm. The internals do not. A **5.9% drop in a semiconductor gauge** sat inside an S&P 500 that fell less than half a percent.
- Every major asset was trading one story: the Fed hikes Wednesday, and oil is why.

| Nasdaq Composite | 26,186.41 | −0.56% |

- The damage was concentrated. Chips took it: **Micron −5%**, **Broadcom and AMD both −4%**, **Nvidia −3.4%**. The semiconductor index fell **5.9%**.
- Cause is not subtle — if the frontier labs pace themselves, the capex schedule that underwrites chip demand gets an asterisk on it.

### Rates: the hike is priced, the yield is the story

- The **10-year Treasury yield briefly punched through 5%**, the first time since 2023.
- Markets price a **25bp hike to 4.00% at roughly 92%** for the 15–16 September FOMC, up from ~85% at Friday's close.
- Friday's August CPI did the work: **+0.4% m/m**, **3.4% y/y**, both in line, with core a tenth hot.
- The decision is settled. The dot plot is not — that is the only part of Wednesday worth watching.

### Oil and the Middle East

- **WTI traded near $104, up about 4%**, on worsening disruption to Middle East oil flows.
- This is the transmission line for everything else: higher crude → higher inflation expectation → higher yields → pressure on long-duration equities. AI names are the longest-duration equities there are.

### Gold and crypto diverge

- **Gold held its decline near $4,290/oz**, after dropping more than 1% to a five-week low. Rate-hike odds beat safe-haven demand.
- **Bitcoin climbed about 1.5% to roughly $78,300** — the one risk asset to shrug off the defensive mood, and a break from the rates-asset pattern it traded on all weekend.

## Technology

### The AI slowdown turns into policy

- The weekend's pacing call from **Anthropic's Dario Amodei** — backed publicly by **Sam Altman**, **Demis Hassabis** and **Elon Musk** — moved from essay to institution within a day.
- **Anthropic, OpenAI and Google are in talks to form an industry-led standards body** to police AI development. Competitors writing shared rules for a lucrative product line is exactly the structure antitrust regulators ask questions about.
- **Microsoft published a draft code of conduct** for its AI systems. The substance: models may not resist human correction, may not conceal their reasoning, and may not assist weapons manufacture. Violations are treated as **system failures**, not edge cases. Six-week public comment, then it folds into future model training; final version in 2027.
- The short version of Microsoft's principles: **people matter more than AI**.
- **Trump publicly attacked Amodei** over the slowdown push — the safety turn now has a political opponent, not just a commercial one.

### Products shipped anyway

- **OpenAI launched GPT-Live**, a native voice model powering ChatGPT Voice with **sub-300ms latency** and emotional nuance. Native voice removes the transcribe-think-speak relay that made earlier voice modes feel like a phone call with lag.
- **AWS added MiniMax models to Bedrock** — **4M-token context windows** and a mixture-of-experts architecture aimed at agentic workloads.
- **Apple**: code in the **iOS 27 and macOS "Golden Gate" betas** shows Siri being set up to let users **swap in an alternative assistant such as Claude or ChatGPT**. The design keeps Apple as the orchestration layer — it holds the interface, permissions, personal data access and system actions, and routes the thinking to whichever model fits. That is a meaningful shift: Apple stops trying to win the model race and starts renting it out.

### Business of AI

- **Anthropic told investors it expects to be profitable in Q2**, on **$11.5B revenue** and **80%+ gross margins**. Profitability at a frontier lab is new.
- On capability benchmarks, **Anthropic's Fable 5.1 led at 38.8% resolution**, ahead of **OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra at 33.8%** and **Google's Gemini 3.8 Flash at 31.2%**.
- **SoftBank secured an $11.87B two-year loan** from about 20 banks to fund its OpenAI investment — an upsized facility, and a clear read on how aggressively it is pushing into AI infrastructure.
- **OpenAI projects 220M paying ChatGPT users by 2030**, against **35M paid subscribers as of July**.
- **Revolut hit a $75B valuation** on completion of a long-running secondary share sale.

### Jobs

- **Tech layoffs passed 6,300 in the first 10 days of September.**
- **Uber is cutting about 3,300 corporate roles — roughly 10% of its corporate workforce**, its largest since 2020. The stated reason is flattening management to redirect spending toward robotaxi partnerships. Uber shares are down 6% on the year.

## What to watch

- **15–16 September: FOMC.** The 25bp hike to 4.00% is ~92% priced. The dot plot and the statement carry all the information.
- **The 5% line on the 10-year.** A brief break is noise; a close above it repriced every discounted cash flow model on the street.
- **Whether the chip selloff spreads.** One day of −5.9% on a semiconductor gauge is a reaction. A week of it is a multiple compression.
- **The standards body.** Who joins, what it can actually enforce, and how fast regulators respond to three rivals coordinating.
