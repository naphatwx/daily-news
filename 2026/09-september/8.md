# Daily News — 8 September 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Tuesday, 8 September 2026. U.S. markets reopened after Labor Day into a three-way squeeze: **oil above $100**, **10-year yields at a two-decade high**, and **Canada's retaliatory tariffs taking effect at midnight**.

## Finance

### The day in one line

- Stocks fell across the board as an energy supply shock landed on top of rising Fed hike bets.
- Houthi drones hit Saudi energy sites, Brent touched **$100.03** for the first time in three months, and the **10-year Treasury yield topped 4.8%** — the highest since October 2023.

### Oil breaks $100

- **Brent touched $100.03/bbl** intraday, the first print above $100 in three months, before settling back near **$97**.
- Trigger: Iran-backed **Houthi** militants claimed attacks on Saudi energy infrastructure, including the **400,000 barrel-a-day Jazan refinery**. Drones hit four Saudi cities; **73 people were wounded** at Aramco sites.
- This stacks on last week's U.S.–Iran exchange in the Strait of Hormuz. Two separate fronts now threaten the same supply route.
- **Goldman Sachs** raised its December 2026 forecasts by $5, to **Brent $85** and **WTI $80**, and to **$80/$75** for 2027.
  - Goldman's warning case: Brent above **$120 in 2027** if Gulf output stays 4 million barrels a day below prewar levels.

### Rates — the real pressure point

- **10-year Treasury yield above 4.8%**, highest since October 2023. The **2-year hit a 52-week high**.
- Markets now price roughly a **60–70% chance of a 25bp Fed hike** at the 15–16 September meeting, after Friday's payrolls came in at **+162,000** — about three times the expected number.
- **CPI and PPI land this week.** Headline inflation is expected to hold at **3.4%**, core to ease slightly to **2.4%**.
  - The risk is obvious: an oil spike and a hot CPI in the same week would remove any doubt about the hike.

### Other assets

- **Gold: $4,400.70 (-0.86%)** — the safe-haven bid lost to higher real yields and a firm dollar for a second session.
- **Bitcoin: ~$78,830 (-0.73%)** — dipped about 3% below $77,000 on the rate repricing before recovering. Crypto is trading as a rates asset here, not a haven.

### Canada tariffs take effect

- Canada's retaliatory tariffs on U.S. goods started just after midnight Tuesday, after talks collapsed last month. PM **Mark Carney** pressed ahead.
- Scope: roughly **$20 billion** of U.S. imports — dairy and cheese, wood products, paper goods, metal items.
- **Steel tariffs doubled to 50%** from 25%.
- Sector effect: capital rotated into energy producers and out of fuel-heavy consumer names — airlines and cruise lines take the hit from both higher jet fuel and weaker cross-border demand.

## Technology

### Mistral raises €3B — Europe's largest tech round ever

- French AI firm **Mistral** closed a **€3 billion (~$3.5B) Series D** at a post-money valuation above **€21 billion (~$24.4B)**.
- **Samsung Electronics led.** Co-leads: the EQT-managed Scaleup Europe Fund and existing backer PSG Equity.
- New money: **Advent**, funds managed by **BlackRock**, and the **Grand Duchy of Luxembourg**. Returning: a16z, ASML, General Catalyst, NVIDIA, Salesforce Ventures, Lightspeed.
- Use of funds: expanding compute for training plus an international inference footprint. CEO **Arthur Mensch** said Mistral will build and own data centers as well as rent capacity.
- Why it matters: this is the "sovereign AI" thesis getting priced. Europe now has one credible privately held answer to the U.S. labs, and a Korean chaebol is paying for it.

### China commits $532B to AI compute

- China's **MIIT** published a 2026–2030 plan to more than quadruple national intelligent computing capacity to **9,800 exaflops by 2030**.
- Cumulative IT infrastructure spend: **¥3.8 trillion (~$532 billion)**.
- Read alongside Huawei's same-day disclosure below — the strategy is to out-build the export controls rather than out-manufacture them.

### Chips

- **ASML** is developing larger photomasks for High-NA EUV, which would allow bigger AI chips and lift system productivity by roughly **40%**.
- **Intel** has processed over **one million wafers** on High-NA EUV — a real manufacturing lead over TSMC and Samsung in that specific tool.
- **Huawei** tested a processor using a **LogicFolding** architecture, squeezing more performance out of restricted process nodes.
- **Arm** launched **Neoverse N4**, a 128-core platform for cloud and AI data centers with hyperscaler customization.
- **Memory prices are rising for consumers.** AI data center demand is pulling fab capacity toward high-bandwidth memory, and phones and laptops pay for it.

### Security — two bad ones

- **WeChat worm proof-of-concept.** Researchers demonstrated a memory-corruption bug exploitable through missed VoIP calls. A live version could plausibly compromise hundreds of millions of accounts within hours.
- **220 million airline passenger records** exposed via a publicly reachable database linked to Vietnam — passport numbers and travel histories included.
- **Multi-agent AI cybercrime** is now operational: criminal groups are running coordinated agent sets for reconnaissance, phishing, and execution in automated credential-theft campaigns.

### Platform and policy

- **Google's EU search redesign** went live, placing rival search engines at the top of results to satisfy EU rules. A Google executive called it "the largest reduction in quality of service" in the platform's history.
- **Australia** drafted a law requiring platforms to offer users over 16 a choice between an algorithmic feed and a follow-only feed. Penalties above **A$100 million**.

### Also on the tape

- **OpenAI** signed a multiyear deal with **Firmus** for compute capacity from Malaysian data centers, spreading its infrastructure geographically.
- **Stoke Space** closed an initial **$1 billion Series E** for its Nova reusable orbital rocket.
- **Xiaomi** launched the **18 Fold** on its in-house **Xring O3** chip, ahead of Apple's expected foldable entry.
- **PyTorch Foundation** added **Alibaba Cloud** and **Cambricon** as Platinum members at KubeCon + PyTorch Conference China in Shanghai.
- **BrainChild Bio** raised a **$116M Series A** for CAR-T therapy targeting pediatric brain tumors.
