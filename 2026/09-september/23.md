# Daily News — 23 September 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Wednesday, 23 September 2026. The bond market took the wheel. **The 10-year Treasury yield jumped to about 5.135%, its highest since July 2007**, after a red-hot business-activity survey and a rebound in oil. **Stocks slid, the Nasdaq worst hit at −1.1%**, two days after a record high. **Brent jumped 3.9% back above $103**, ending a five-day losing streak. Meanwhile **Xi Jinping arrived for his first US state visit in 11 years**, and **Meta used Connect to put its Muse agent into glasses, a headset and a pendant**.

- The 10-year's jump was its **biggest one-day move since 7 April 2025**, the tariff-shock week.
- Utilities and consumer discretionary led the decline, each down more than 1%. Small caps (Russell 2000) sank alongside the Nasdaq. Both groups are the most sensitive to rates.

&nbsp;

### The PMI that spooked the bond market

- **S&P Global's flash US Composite PMI rose to 58.4** from 56.0, the fastest private-sector expansion since **July 2021** and the fourth straight month of acceleration.
- **Services 58.7** (expected 56.0). New orders grew at the fastest pace in nearly four and a half years. Hiring was the strongest since February 2021.
- The bad part: **input costs rose at the sharpest pace since October 2022**, and supply-chain delays were the most widespread since mid-2022.
- Why it matters: last week the Fed raised rates to **3.75–4.00%**, its first hike in over three years. An economy this hot, with costs this sticky, makes another hike more likely. Traders sold bonds on that view, and stocks followed.

&nbsp;

### Oil puts the war premium back

- **Brent +3.9% to $103.08**, after falling five days in a row, including a 1% drop to $99.25 on Tuesday.
- The trigger was **Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian vowing Iran "will not surrender"** to the US.
- It came despite a diplomatic opening. **Trump called a three-hour meeting with Iranian envoys on the UN sidelines "very productive"** and said more talks are planned. Envoys **Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner** talked with Foreign Minister **Abbas Araqchi** through mediators. It was the first contact since the interim ceasefire collapsed in July, and it came hours after Trump again threatened to "annihilate" Iran.
- Supply was still getting better: Saudi Arabia's East-West pipeline has partly restarted after the drone attacks. So Wednesday's rally was about headlines, not barrels.

&nbsp;

### Xi arrives in Washington

- **Xi Jinping's state visit runs 23–25 September.** It is his first US state visit in 11 years.
- The main question is whether the **Busan truce of October 2025** gets extended before the tariff suspension expires on **10 November**. That would mean capped US tariffs, continued Chinese rare-earth shipments, and no major new export controls from either side.
- NPR reports that **mutual distrust sank a Beijing offer to extend the rare-earths truce to January 2029**, the end of Trump's term. Watch for whether that offer comes back.
- AI is on the agenda too, after the US proposed a standing US–China AI dialogue in this week's Bessent–He Lifeng talks.

&nbsp;

### Elsewhere

- **IonQ** rose about **6%** (up 11% premarket) after announcing a real-time quantum error-correction decoder. **Securitize** gained about **13%**.
- **Bitcoin** opened at about **$86,200** and slipped to around $85,600–85,700. It held Monday's short-squeeze gains even as yields climbed. Crypto commentators are asking again whether the crypto winter is over.
- **Meta** rose about 2% early ahead of Connect. It is up nearly 20% in September, and **Jefferies raised its target to $875** from $710.
- **Earnings on deck:** Cintas, General Mills and Cracker Barrel.

## Technology

### Meta Connect 2026: Muse everywhere

- **Meta's new VR glasses: $1,299.** They weigh about 100 grams, use hand and eye tracking with no controllers needed, and have Muse AI built in. They run the Quest library, support Xbox Cloud Gaming, and ship with 75 launch titles. Coverage frames them as a lighter rival to Apple's Vision Pro.
- **Ray-Ban Meta Gen 3: from $449.** Slimmer Wayfarer frames, new aviator and ultra-thin Clubmaster styles, and Dolby Atmos spatial-audio recording.
- **Camera-free, audio-only glasses: from $249.** They have the longest battery life in the lineup and an **FDA-cleared hearing-enhancement feature** for a $149 one-time fee.
- **Muse Charm:** a pocket pendant for talking to the Muse agent, due in time for the holidays.
- **Muse commerce partners** now include **Walmart and Instacart**, plus a separate virtual space that keeps Muse interactions apart from the rest of Meta.
- The takeaway: Monday's 11% jump was about Muse's early adoption. Connect is Meta's plan to put that agent on your face, in your ears and in your pocket, with shopping built in.

&nbsp;

### AI at the UN Security Council

- **France**, which holds the Council presidency, convened a session on **AI and international security** with **Sam Altman**, senior **Anthropic** representatives, and **DeepSeek** and **Moonshot** representatives expected.
- It is the first time the Council has hosted frontier US and Chinese AI labs together on shared safety concerns. It came the same day Xi landed in Washington with AI guardrails on the summit agenda.

&nbsp;

### Robotics: Alphabet opens up Intrinsic

- **Alphabet's Intrinsic open-sourced its full robotics stack** at ROSCon 2026 in Toronto. **Intrinsic Core** is a ROS-compatible environment covering real-time control, Nvidia FoundationPose-based pose estimation, motion and grasp planning, simulation and calibration.
- Giving the stack away is a bid to become the default software layer for robots before hardware makers settle on one.

&nbsp;

### Apple notes

- Apple is **testing an iPhone anti-theft feature** that detects snatch-and-grab theft and locks the phone before the thief can use it.
- The new **Texture and Grain Photographic Styles controls** are limited to the **iPhone 18 Pro** models and the upcoming **iPhone Duo**.
- UBI Research expects Apple to adopt **CoE OLED** displays for the **2029 iPhone Pro**.
- Some users are reporting a notification glitch on **iOS 27**.
