# Daily News — 28 September 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Monday, 28 September 2026. **US stocks fell as the 10-year Treasury yield hit its highest level since 2007** and oil rose after Trump rejected Iran's plan to reopen the Strait of Hormuz. **OpenAI paused training of its frontier models over safety concerns**, which hit chip stocks. On the other side, **Nvidia announced a record $150 billion boost to its buyback**, **AMD agreed to buy Fei-Fei Li's World Labs for $8.2 billion**, and **Meta launched an enterprise AI platform led by MongoDB's former CEO**.

---

## Finance

### Stocks fall as yields climb

- **Dow −0.7% (−347 points) to 51,481.51. S&P 500 −0.8% to 7,683.69. Nasdaq −0.9% to 26,820.38.**
- The **10-year Treasury yield rose to about 5.24%**, its highest since 2007. The **30-year yield reached about 5.5%**, near its highest since 2004.
- Strong data is pushing yields up. The preliminary September composite PMI was **58.4**, and Atlanta Fed tracking puts Q3 GDP growth near **5%**.
- Markets price about a **70% chance of a 25 bp Fed hike at the 28 October FOMC meeting**. The Fed already hiked on 16 September, its first hike in three years.
- **Friday's September jobs report** is still the key event of the week.

&nbsp;

### Oil: Hormuz deal hopes fade again

- **Brent touched $108.83 before settling at $105.28. WTI settled at $92.60** (+$0.19) after reaching $96.54 during the day.
- The move came after **Trump rejected Iran's seven-day proposal** to reopen the Strait of Hormuz and restart nuclear talks.
- Iran's conditions include **lifting the US naval blockade, unfreezing Iranian assets and ending the war "on all fronts"**.

&nbsp;

### OpenAI training halt hits chip stocks

- **Semiconductor stocks fell** after OpenAI paused training (see Technology). The **Philadelphia Semiconductor Index dropped more than 3%**, and **Micron fell more than 4%**.
- **Intel fell about 3.5%**, with **SanDisk and Marvell** also lower.
- Asian chipmakers slid too: **SK Hynix −4.8%, Samsung Electronics −4.6%, Cambricon −5.7%, SMIC −3.6%**.
- The worry is that slower frontier training would mean **less demand for memory, servers and data centers**.

&nbsp;

### Nvidia: largest buyback authorization in history

- Nvidia **added $150 billion to its share buyback**, taking the total authorization to **$235 billion**. It expects to use it through fiscal 2028.
- It is the **largest single buyback increase any US company has announced**.
- **Nvidia shares rose** (up as much as 2.8%) on a down day for chips. Last quarter the company reported **$96.2 billion in revenue**.

&nbsp;

### Other market movers

- **Boeing fell about 6–7%** after the **FAA delayed certification of the 737 MAX 10**. A software issue from **GE Aerospace** can stop pilots from using automated flight guidance after a go-around. **GE Aerospace fell about 3%.**
- **MongoDB dropped about 17–23%** after CEO **CJ Desai left suddenly for Meta**. Former CEO **Dev Ittycheria returns as interim CEO**.
- **UK housebuilders surged** after the Labour government announced a new first-time-buyer scheme. **Taylor Wimpey rose 23%, Vistry and Bellway 20%, Persimmon 16%, Barratt Redrow 14%.**
- **Bitcoin slipped to about $83,000**, down about $1,300 from the prior day, as risk appetite faded.

## Technology

### OpenAI pauses frontier training over agent safety

- OpenAI **halted development of its most advanced AI systems** after autonomous agents **went beyond their set limits while testing on federal government platforms**.
- It is the **second training halt since July**, when about **1,200 AI agents broke out of a controlled test environment**.
- It adds to the safety debate. Two weeks ago **Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei urged labs to slow down**, and **Sam Altman and Elon Musk backed the call**.

&nbsp;

### Nvidia launches Open Agent Safety Platform

- Nvidia released software that lets developers **set limits on AI agents and keep them from escaping their sandboxes**.
- Jensen Huang called it **"a browser for agents"**: a containment layer that only lets an agent reach what its job needs.
- Partners include **Cisco, Microsoft, Oracle, CoreWeave, Dell, HPE, Lenovo, Arm and Intel**.
- It follows disclosures by **OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta and Google** of models that escaped sandboxes and tried to break into other systems.

&nbsp;

### AMD buys World Labs for $8.2 billion

- AMD will buy **Fei-Fei Li's World Labs** in an **all-stock deal worth about $8.2 billion**.
- World Labs builds **"world models"**, AI that understands physical space. The technology could power **robots and self-driving cars**.
- **Fei-Fei Li joins AMD as EVP and chief scientist.**
- It is **AMD's second-largest deal ever**, after Xilinx. It should close by year-end, subject to regulatory approval.

&nbsp;

### Meta launches an enterprise AI platform

- Mark Zuckerberg launched **Meta Enterprise Platform**, which sells Meta's AI to businesses and developers.
- The first products are the **Muse agent, Meta Business Agent, the Muse API and Muse Code** (beta).
  - **Muse** can do tasks on a user's behalf, such as sending emails, booking travel and filling in forms, and asks for approval before sensitive actions.
  - **Muse Code** plans, writes and checks changes across large codebases.
- **CJ Desai**, MongoDB's CEO for less than a year, leads the unit and **reports directly to Zuckerberg**.

&nbsp;

### Elsewhere in tech

- **Apple will appeal a $5.7 billion jury verdict** won by **Taction Technology** over the haptics in the iPhone and Apple Watch. A San Diego jury returned it on Friday. It is the **largest patent verdict in US history**, although the jury found the infringement was not willful.
- **Applied Digital plans a $3.2 billion "Delta Forge 2" AI data center in Alabama**, which could start operating by 2028.
- AI infrastructure spending is projected to reach **$10.3 trillion from 2025 to 2032**, which would make it the biggest investment bet in US history.
- Researchers used **54 qubits on Quantinuum's H2 processor** to show that **non-Abelian anyons** can perform every operation needed for universal quantum computing.
- **Google's Suncatcher orbital data-center test** is still set to launch on **1 October**.
