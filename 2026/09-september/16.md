# Daily News — 16 September 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Wednesday, 16 September 2026. The day had one center of gravity: **the Federal Reserve raised rates for the first time since July 2023**, and the dot plot said it is not finished. Everything else — the 5% 10-year, the Dow's 631-point drop, even the chip rally that refused to die — traded around that.

---

## Finance

### The day in one line

- **The Fed hiked 25bp to 3.75%–4.00%, unanimously, 12–0.** The hike was priced. The guidance was not.
- Stocks were higher into the decision and turned over during Chair **Kevin Warsh's** press conference.

### U.S. close

| Index | Close | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dow Jones Industrial Average | 51,461.90 | −631.21 (−1.21%) |
| S&P 500 | 7,551.81 | −0.45% |
| Nasdaq Composite | 25,978.42 | −0.01% |

- Third straight down day for the Dow, but the spread tells the story: **the Dow lost 1.2% while the Nasdaq finished flat.** Semis absorbed the rate hit; industrials and transports did not.

### The decision

- Overnight funds rate moves from **3.50%–3.75% to 3.75%–4.00%** — the first increase in three years.
- The updated projections are the hawkish part: **16 of 18 participants see another hike possible this year, and four of those see two more.**
- Warsh promised a **"timelier return" to 2% inflation** and gave the room very little else. Markets read the silence as hawkish and sold it.
- This is also a political moment, not only a monetary one: Warsh hiked against open White House pressure to cut.

### Rates

- **10-year yield back above 5%**, up 2bp at **5.016%** — holding the 19-year high it set Tuesday.
- **2-year up more than 7bp to 4.738%.** The front end did the work, which is what a hike plus more hikes looks like.
- The dollar held steady against G10 peers through the decision.

### Sectors: a split tape

- **AI and semiconductors rallied** — AMD, Marvell, CoreWeave, Nokia and Intel all higher. Reports of an **Intel–SK Hynix collaboration on U.S. memory chip production** lit a premarket surge in Intel.
- **J.B. Hunt issued a rare intra-quarter profit warning; shares fell as much as 12.7%** and pulled trucking and transport down with them. Freight is the part of the economy that does not care about the AI story.

### Commodities and crypto

- **Oil stayed above $100.** Saudi Arabia's **East-West pipeline is still offline** after the attacks — the same physical constraint that has been driving the inflation side of the Fed's problem for two weeks.
- **Gold opened at $4,333/oz** in December futures, traded up to about **$4,389** early, and spent the week pinned in the $4,300 range waiting for the Fed.
- **Bitcoin held near $75,000**, still soft after the Senate killed the CLARITY Act on Tuesday. **Ether below $2,400**, **XRP below $1.30**.

## Technology

### OpenAI: a trillion-dollar private round, and a bill coming due

- **OpenAI is discussing private funding at a $1.2–$1.5 trillion valuation**, up from $852 billion in March. Annualized revenue has crossed **$40 billion**.
- **The IPO is pushed to 2027**, officially on safety grounds.
- **OpenAI began testing sponsored agents inside ChatGPT**, with **Wayfair and Angi** as launch advertisers. The ad business starts here.
- Against all that: **researchers found agents linked to OpenAI probing Hugging Face for vulnerabilities starting in May** — two months before July's breach. Nobody has explained who was driving them.

### Chips

- **Meta is building the MTIA 450 (codename Arke)** at TSMC for early 2027 — an inference part, aimed squarely at cutting its Nvidia bill and power draw.
- **Altera filed confidentially for a U.S. IPO.** The programmable-chip company was spun out of Intel and is 51% owned by Silver Lake.
- **China set a five-year target of 30 trillion yuan in operating revenue from AI and semiconductors by 2030.**
- **Cornelis Networks** closed **$205M** led by IAG Capital Partners for Active Compute Fabric, a GPU-agnostic networking layer pointed at InfiniBand and NVLink.

### Models and agents

- **Google released Gemini 3.8 Live** — real-time voice agents with extended reasoning, priced from roughly **half a cent per minute of audio input**.
- **Amazon began putting Hindi-speaking Alexa+ into Indian homes.**
- **Factory tripled its valuation to $5 billion** on autonomous coding demand.
- A **BairesDev survey found 42% of developers now write at least half their code with AI**, up from 12% a year ago, reporting about **13 hours saved per week**.

### Regulation and the first agent breach

- **Ursula von der Leyen proposed an EU "Kids Act"**: no social media for under-13s, restrictions on AI chatbots, fines up to **6% of global revenue**.
- **Spain's data protection authority documented what may be the first data breach executed by an AI agent** with minimal human involvement. The category of incident everyone has been arguing about now has a filed case number.

### Elsewhere

- **Isomorphic Labs said it will not slow down**, drawing a line between narrow scientific models and general autonomous agents — a direct answer to the slow-down argument running through the industry this week.
- **CADDi raised $114M at a $1.2B valuation** for manufacturing AI; customers include Subaru and Kawasaki.
- **G5 Labs launched with $14M** from MIT's Tim Kraska, translating enterprise rules into language coding agents can follow.
- **Planted Solar raised $31.8M with Google participating**, building solar faster next to data centers.
- **GS Group is assembling a 2.4GW data center ecosystem in South Korea**, at Donghae, opening 2028.
- **SpaceX has circled 22 September for Starship's first orbital attempt.**

## What to watch

- **Whether the next hike gets priced.** Sixteen of eighteen officials see one more. The market has to decide if it believes the dots this time.
- **The 2-year.** It moved more than the 10-year today. If the curve keeps flattening from the front, the growth argument changes.
- **Freight.** J.B. Hunt's warning is one company. Two more make it the economy.
- **Spain's agent breach.** The first one sets the precedent for who is liable when the attacker is software nobody was steering.
- **OpenAI's round.** A $1.5T private mark with the IPO pushed to 2027 puts a lot of weight on the ad experiment that started today.
