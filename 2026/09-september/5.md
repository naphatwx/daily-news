## Technology

### Claude formalizes Fermat's Last Theorem

- **Anthropic** published the first end-to-end, computer-checked proof of **Fermat's Last Theorem** in the **Lean** language.
- Claude ran largely autonomously for **11 days**.
- Output: **13 million lines of Lean**, **30,300 theorems proved** (29,500 used in the final proof), about **6 billion output tokens**.
- The run used **Prove2Me**, an open-source harness for long agent workflows from Tianyi Peng's group at Columbia.
- Caveats worth keeping: the first attempt failed without Prove2Me, and the 11 days reflect several dozen agents in parallel, not one sustained agent. This is a **formalization** of Wiles' 129-page proof — a translation a computer can verify line by line — not a new proof.

### AI, money and politics

- AI companies have committed roughly **$265 million** to political groups ahead of the **2026 midterms**, against rising local opposition to data centers.
- **President Trump** defended data center buildout as creating "millions and millions of jobs" while acknowledging community pushback.

### Data centers as an insurance market

- Flagged risk: geographic concentration in tornado- and hail-prone regions.

### Deals and hardware
