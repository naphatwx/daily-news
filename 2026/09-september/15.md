# Daily News — 15 September 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Tuesday, 15 September 2026. Three things moved at once: **the 10-year Treasury yield hit its highest level since 2007**, **the Senate killed the crypto market-structure bill**, and **the AI-safety argument split the industry in public** — with Nvidia's CEO calling the doom talk made up.

---

## Finance

### The day in one line

- Everything traded off the Wednesday Fed decision and a crude price that will not come down.
- The equity loss was modest. The bond move was not: **5.04% on the 10-year is a 19-year high**.

- Second straight down day, and the second straight day the Nasdaq led the fall.
- Against the AI-slowdown mood, **Bank of America raised its 2030 semiconductor market forecast to $3.2 trillion**, arguing data center demand holds even if frontier labs pace themselves.

### Rates: a 2007 high on the eve of the decision

- The **10-year yield touched 5.041% intraday**, the highest since 2007, and closed the New York session back at **5.00%**.
- A **25bp hike to 4.00% is priced at roughly 92–93%** for Wednesday — it would be the first increase since July 2023.
- The expected framing: the Fed signals the move is discrete and does not pre-commit it to a path. That framing is the whole trade. A hike plus a hawkish dot plot is a different market than a hike plus "one and done."

### Oil keeps the pressure on

- **Brent near $107–108**, **WTI near $105**, up again.
- The cause is physical, not speculative: **Saudi Arabia's East-West pipeline is still shut** after attacks, and Houthi activity keeps the risk premium in the price.
- Oil is the reason the yield is at 5%. Until the pipeline restarts, the inflation side of the Fed's problem does not improve.

### Crypto: the Senate said no

- **The CLARITY Act failed its cloture vote 49–50**, eleven short of the 60 needed. All Democrats voted no, joined by four Republicans — **Collins, Hawley, Moran and Tillis**.
- The bill would have divided crypto oversight between the SEC and CFTC, with a larger role for the CFTC. Its failure effectively ends market-structure legislation in the Senate for 2026.
- **Bitcoin fell about 2.7%**, sliding from near $80,000 to a session low around **$75,000** before settling near **$76,000**. **Circle, Bullish and Coinbase** all extended losses.
- Years of lobbying and hundreds of millions of dollars bought the industry a vote it lost by one senator on the floor.

### Gold

- **Gold slipped 0.23%** — still losing the tug-of-war between safe-haven demand and a rate hike that raises the cost of holding a metal that pays nothing.

## Technology

### The safety fight goes public

- **Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang said on the All In podcast that AI doomsday fears are simply made up.** At a Goldman Sachs conference he went further, pointing at the cybersecurity industry: it has products to sell, and "what better way to create demand than to create a problem?"
- That is a direct answer to **Dario Amodei's essay** calling on labs to slow frontier development, which **Sam Altman** publicly agreed with.
- The trigger underneath both: an **Anthropic researcher resigned** saying the company was not doing enough, and Anthropic's alignment science lead **Evan Hubinger** put the chance that AI kills all humans at **greater than 10%**.
- A **draft Code of Conduct** introduced the term **Humanist Superintelligence (HSI)** — human control, safety ahead of task completion, no independent AI goals.
- **China's Foreign Ministry attacked Amodei** over his support for continued US chip restrictions, calling it fearmongering.
- The split is now clean: the people who sell the chips say the risk is invented; the people who train the models say it is not.

### The money has not slowed

- The four major hyperscalers spent **$293 billion in the first half of 2026** and are on pace for **nearly $600 billion of AI infrastructure spending this year**.
- **Sam Altman told Fortune OpenAI will not go public in 2026**, citing safety work still to do and bad timing.
- **Firmus**, an Nvidia-backed AI data-center company, is seeking up to **A$7 billion (about $5B)** in an October ASX listing.
- **Cornelis Networks**, an Intel spinoff, raised **$205 million** for Active Compute Fabric — an open, GPU-agnostic networking layer for AI clusters, aimed squarely at Nvidia's interconnect lock-in.
- **SemiAnalysis** reported Nvidia's **Rubin NVL72 running 2.1x faster than Blackwell** at 100 tokens per second in preliminary tests.

### Products

- **Apple shipped a public beta of the rebuilt Siri, powered by custom Google Gemini models**, splitting work between the device and Apple's Private Cloud Compute. Apple keeps the interface and the private data; Google supplies the thinking.
- **Sakana AI released Fugu Max**, a router that sends each request to the right open model — **$2 per million input tokens, $6 per million output**.

### Security and privacy

- A threat actor built exploits for two **PaperCut NG/MF** flaws and used **AI agents to automate the intrusions**, hitting at least **395 organizations across 48 countries in 48 hours**. This is the argument about agentic attack speed ending: it already happened.
- **404 Media reported "Project Lily"** — hundreds of contractors reviewing ChatGPT conversations and scoring responses that can contain sensitive personal details.
- **Cloudflare's default block on "mixed-use" AI crawlers** on ad-supported pages took effect, covering new customers, newly added sites and all free-plan users.

## What to watch

- **Wednesday's FOMC.** The hike is decided. The statement language on whether this is a one-off is the only live variable.
- **The Saudi East-West pipeline.** Crude is driving the yield, and the yield is driving equities. A restart unwinds the chain.
- **Whether crypto has a Plan B.** With the Senate route closed for 2026, regulation reverts to the agencies and the courts.
- **The Huang-versus-Amodei split.** One side is priced into capex schedules, the other into policy drafts. They cannot both be right.
