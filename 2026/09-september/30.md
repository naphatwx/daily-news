# Daily News — 30 September 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Wednesday, 30 September 2026. **The Dow fell 0.9% and the S&P 500 ended September with a loss**, as Q2 GDP was revised up and kept Treasury yields high, even though **PCE inflation came in well below forecasts**. After the close, **Micron posted a record $54.2 billion quarter** and guided far above estimates. In tech, **OpenAI held DevDay** and launched **GPT-6.1 Sol and always-on "Dots" agents**, the **FTC opened a probe into OpenAI and Anthropic** a day after the White House AI accord, and **Apple Pay finally launched in India**.

---

## Finance

### Dow drops 444 points; September ends in the red

- **Dow −0.87% (−443.87 points) to 50,906.05. S&P 500 −0.25% to 7,651.54. Nasdaq +0.24% to 26,861.06.**
- Stocks **rose in the morning** on soft inflation data, then **turned lower** after the GDP revision.
- **September results: Dow −4.9%, S&P 500 −0.7%, Nasdaq +1.7%.**
- The **10-year Treasury yield was about 5.29%**.
- **Alphabet rose about 3%**. **Mattel fell about 3%** after announcing a CEO change.

&nbsp;

### Inflation cooler than expected

- The **PCE price index**, the Fed's preferred gauge, **rose 3.4% year on year in August**, below the **3.7%** forecast.
- **Core PCE was 3.0%**, also much lower than expected.
- **Fed hike odds for 28 October fell to about 37%**, down from **over 70% a week ago**.
- **New York Fed President John Williams** said there was **"no need for urgency"** on an October hike.

&nbsp;

### Economy stronger than thought

- **Q2 GDP was revised up to 2.2% annualized**, from **1.5%** in the earlier estimate.
- The revision came from **consumer spending, government spending and AI-related business investment**.
- **ADP: private employers added 90,000 jobs in September**, above the **68,000** expected and up from **36,000** in August.
- **Friday's official September jobs report** is next.

&nbsp;

### Micron smashes records after the close

- **Fiscal Q4 revenue was $54.23 billion, up 379% year on year.**
- **Adjusted EPS was $33.42**, above the **~$31.6–31.8** expected. **Gross margin was 87%.**
- **DRAM revenue rose 343% to $39.8 billion**, 73% of sales. **Data center revenue rose about 11-fold.**
- **Full fiscal 2026 revenue was $133.2 billion**, up 256%.
- **Q1 FY2027 guidance: revenue of about $61.5 billion and EPS of about $38.15**, well above the **~$57 billion** and **~$35–36** analysts expected.
- **The stock barely moved after hours** (from about **$1,065**), a sign that much of the AI memory boom was already priced in.

&nbsp;

### Commodities and crypto

- **WTI closed at about $90.60.** **Brent was about $97.5** in the evening, after the **November contract expired**.
- **Gold was about $4,192** (+0.1%). **Bitcoin was about $83,500** (+0.1%).

## Technology

### OpenAI DevDay: GPT-6.1 Sol and "Dots" agents

- OpenAI announced **more than 20 launches** at DevDay.
- **GPT-6.1 Sol** arrives a week after GPT-6 Sol:
  - It offers **close to GPT-6 Astra's intelligence** for agentic coding and computer use, at **about one-fifth of Astra's token prices**.
  - **API price: $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens**, the same as **Claude Sonnet 5.5**.
  - The share of answers with **factual errors fell to 7.7% from 11.4%**.
- **Dots** are **always-on agents** built on GPT-6 Astra:
  - Each has its **own cloud computer** and keeps working after a chat ends.
  - They connect to **4,000+ apps plus Slack and Teams**.
- **New ChatGPT Pro tiers: $100, $200 and $500 a month.** The $500 tier gets an **"Ultrafast" mode** of up to **300 tokens per second** in Codex.
- Other launches: **hosted computer use, Sign in with ChatGPT, a Marketplace, a Decisions API and Codex Code Review**.

&nbsp;

### FTC probes OpenAI and Anthropic, a day after the AI accord

- On 29 September, **Meta, Google, Nvidia, OpenAI, Anthropic and Elon Musk's companies** signed the **White House Accord on Super Intelligence**.
  - They agreed to **internal controls, outside audits and board reviews** of their most advanced models.
  - Critics note there is **no penalty** and **no need to publish audit results**.
- On 30 September, the **FTC announced an investigation into OpenAI and Anthropic** over risks to consumers. AI evaluator **METR** is also a target.
- **FTC Chair Andrew Ferguson** is preparing **civil investigative demands** to get documents and testimony on model safety.
- It is the **first major US regulatory action** after recent reports of AI agents "going rogue".
- **Trump also ordered federal agencies to use the term "Super Intelligence" (SI)** instead of AI.

&nbsp;

### DeepSeek and Huawei team up against Nvidia

- **DeepSeek open-sourced Huawei Ascend versions** of its AI tools: **TileLang, DeepGEMM, DeepEP, TileKernels, FlashMLA and DeepSelect**.
- Each tool **matches its Nvidia version one to one**, so developers can use the same tools on both platforms.
- Huawei showed a jointly designed **128-chip "SuperPoD Flex"** built on **Ascend 950** chips.
- The aim is to fix China's biggest AI gap: **software that gets the most out of domestic chips**.

&nbsp;

### Apple Pay launches in India

- **Apple Pay went live in India**, one of the **last major markets** without it.
- It starts with **Axis Bank** Visa and Mastercard cards, on **iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch**. **Mac support is coming soon.**
- It enters a market **dominated by Google Pay** and UPI apps.

&nbsp;

### Elsewhere in tech

- **Meta released new VR glasses** with most Quest 3 features, **eye tracking** and **5K micro-OLED displays**.
- **Samsung launched the Galaxy Tab S12 Ultra and S12+** tablets and the **Galaxy SmartTag 3**.
- A startup plans **more than 450 small reactors on 9,500+ acres in Utah** to power a large data center project.
