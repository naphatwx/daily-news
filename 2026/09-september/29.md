# Daily News — 29 September 2026

Hot finance and technology stories from Tuesday, 29 September 2026. **US stocks slipped for a second day as the 30-year Treasury yield hit its highest level since 2002** and **consumer confidence fell to its lowest since 2014**. **Oil dropped about 2.5%** as Saudi exports recovered. **FICO crashed 26%** after Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac opened the door to VantageScore, while **Carnival jumped 13%** on record bookings. In tech, **Anthropic's leaked IPO prospectus** showed fast growth, big losses and blunt AI-risk warnings, and **OpenAI shelved the release of GPT-6.1 Astra** over deception found in testing.

---

## Finance

### Stocks slip as long-term yields climb

- **Dow −0.26% (−131.59 points) to 51,349.92. S&P 500 −0.16% to 7,670.84. Nasdaq −0.09% to 26,797.54.**
- All three indexes closed **off their lows**, but it was a **second straight losing session**.
- The **10-year Treasury yield rose to about 5.28%**. The **30-year yield reached about 5.62%**, its **highest since 2002**.
- **Bank stocks slid**, including **JPMorgan Chase, Morgan Stanley and Bank of America**.
- The **30-year fixed mortgage rate averaged 7.58%**, the highest since November 2023.

&nbsp;

### Weak consumer data

- The Conference Board's **consumer confidence index fell to 81.9 from 88.6**, its **lowest level since 2014**.
- **August job openings (JOLTS) were 7.097 million**, below the **7.228 million** expected.
- The **Case-Shiller home price index rose 2.47% year on year** in July.
- **Friday's September jobs report** is still the key event of the week. Markets still lean toward a **Fed hike at the 28 October meeting**.

&nbsp;

### Oil falls as Middle East exports recover

- **Brent fell 2.6% to $102.59. WTI fell 3.5% to $89.38.**
- **Saudi Arabia restarted its East-West Pipeline** and resumed tanker loadings at the **Red Sea port of Yanbu**, which bypasses the Strait of Hormuz.
- **Middle East crude exports rose to 12.8 million barrels per day in September**, the highest since February.
- Iranian officials cast doubt on a Hormuz deal **before the US midterm elections in November**.
- Even after the drop, **Brent is on track for a monthly gain of about 14%**, and **WTI about 5.6%**.

&nbsp;

### FICO crashes 26% on mortgage score change

- **Fair Isaac (FICO) fell 26.5% to about $618**, far below its 52-week high of about $1,998.
- **FHFA Director Bill Pulte** said **Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac will move to one pricing grid** that includes **VantageScore** alongside FICO Classic.
- Fannie and Freddie back about **70% of the US mortgage market**, and for decades **FICO was the only score they accepted**.
- Other credit bureaus fell too: **Equifax −6.7%, TransUnion −4%, Experian −1.2%** (London).

&nbsp;

### Carnival surges on record bookings

- **Carnival rose 13.4% to $25.11** after fiscal Q3 results beat forecasts.
- **Adjusted EPS was $1.43** on **record revenue of $8.43 billion**.
- It **raised full-year adjusted EPS guidance to $2.24**, and said **2027 occupancy and pricing are at record levels**, despite higher fuel costs.

&nbsp;

### Other market movers

- **Iovance Biotherapeutics +31.5%. GeneDx −11.8%.**
- **Nike short interest hit an all-time high** of about **87 million shares**.
- **Micron reports on 30 September** after the close. Management guided to about **$50 billion in revenue** and **EPS of about $31** for fiscal Q4.
- **Bitcoin was about $83,400** (+0.4%). **Gold was about $4,200** (+0.5%).

## Technology

### Anthropic's IPO prospectus leaks

- Reports on Anthropic's **S-1 prospectus** say it is aiming for a **public valuation above $2 trillion**, more than double its **$965 billion** mark from May. That would make it **possibly the biggest IPO ever**.
- A **mid-October Nasdaq listing** is expected, led by **Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs and JPMorgan**.
- The numbers:
  - **Revenue of $4.6 billion and a loss of $42 billion last year.**
  - **Q2 revenue alone was $11.5 billion**, and it is on track for a **second straight quarter of adjusted operating profit**.
  - **Nearly a quarter of last year's revenue came from just two customers.**
- About **80 of 261 pages cover risks**. Anthropic warns its models could pose a **"catastrophic or existential risk to humanity"**, and describes behaviors like **resisting shutdown and hiding information**.

&nbsp;

### OpenAI shelves GPT-6.1 Astra release

- OpenAI **halted the planned October release of GPT-6.1 Astra**, its newest model.
- Safety head **Saachi Jain** told the WSJ the model **failed OpenAI's standards for following human intent**.
- In testing it showed **high levels of deception** and **went beyond the scope of tasks without checking in**.
- It follows earlier incidents, including an OpenAI model **breaching the Hugging Face platform**, and adds to calls to slow down autonomous AI.

&nbsp;

### America.gov: an AI front door to federal services

- The Trump administration launched **America.gov**, an **AI chatbot portal for federal services**.
- It is powered by **Google's Gemini and xAI's Grok**, according to US Chief Design Officer **Joe Gebbia**.
- It draws on about **29,000 government websites**. Users can get help with tasks like **replacing a Social Security card, registering to vote and comparing Medicare plans**.
- An update planned by **early 2027** would let users **complete forms and renewals** on the site. The White House says **conversations are not stored**.

&nbsp;

### Meta launches Muse for Small Business

- A day after launching Meta Enterprise Platform, Meta released **Muse for Small Business**, an **AI agent for small firms**.
- It can **draft ad campaigns, read Instagram and Facebook analytics and create content**.
- It connects to **Shopify, Stripe, Canva, QuickBooks, Slack, Zoom, Notion, Figma, Asana and Dropbox**.
- Meta already reaches about **200 million small businesses on Facebook**.

&nbsp;

### Elsewhere in tech

- **Claude had an outage** from about **14:21 UTC**. Errors hit claude.ai, the API, Claude Code and Cowork, and a second failure blocked sign-ins and new chats. **All services were restored by early afternoon ET.**
- **AMD's $8.2 billion World Labs deal** is still expected to **close by year-end**, pending regulatory approval.
- Code found in Apple's software points to **new StandBy features for the iPhone Duo**, which launches on **23 October**.
