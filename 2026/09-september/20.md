Hot finance and technology stories from Sunday, 20 September 2026. - **Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and Vice-Premier He Lifeng convened at JPMorgan Chase's midtown headquarters at about 10:30 a.m. ET**, with talks expected to run all day. **US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer** joined.

## Technology

### Plugin4Shell: four agents, one bad assumption

- Disclosed **18 September** by AIR researchers: a **zero-click remote code execution flaw** in **Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, GitHub Copilot and Google Gemini CLI**.
- The mechanism is embarrassingly simple. **Marketplaces pin each plugin to a reviewed commit hash — but the agents fetch the snapshot and never verify that the code they received matches it.** A repository owner can swap in malicious code after review.
- **No click, no approval, no reinstall.** The update lands and runs.
- **Patched: Claude Code 2.1.179 and Codex 0.146.0.** As of the weekend, **Copilot and Gemini CLI remained exposed**.
- This is being called the first real AI supply-chain vulnerability, and the shape of it matters more than the bug: four independent teams shipped the same unchecked assumption because they all copied the same pattern.

### Anthropic moves the IPO

- **Anthropic pushed its IPO from October to November.** Advisers say the extra weeks let it put **third-quarter financials** in front of investors.
- Reported targets: **~$2 trillion valuation** and a raise of **up to $100 billion**.
- Revenue trajectory cited: **$9B run rate at end-2025 → $65B at end-July → projected past $110B annualized by year-end**, driven by Claude Code and enterprise adoption.
- The delay is not neutral. It lands the listing after an **industry slowdown narrative** and the **antitrust suit filed Friday** — both of which now have to be priced into the book.

### Models, prices and a tracking pixel

- **OpenAI runs an ad tracking pixel** that links web browsing to ChatGPT accounts, collecting **page content, hashed contact information and location** across advertiser sites that embed it.
- **Alibaba shipped Qwen-Image-2.1** — a 7B DiT model with native **2048×2048** output — and **moved licensing from Apache 2.0 to research-only**, with commercial use requiring a separate licence. Another open-weights door closing.
- **StepFun launched Step 5 Preview**: a **600B sparse MoE with a 1M-token context**, at **$1 per million input tokens and $2.70 per million output**. Materially cheaper than the Western frontier tier.
- The pattern across all three: Chinese labs competing on price and context length, US labs competing on distribution and monetisation.

### Clinicians push back

- The day's biggest non-market tech story: **clinicians rejecting medical AI outside diagnostics**, citing thin performance data.
- Top concerns in recent surveys: **deskilling — flagged by 74% of clinicians — hallucinations, and unclear governance**. Hospital AI policies remain in flux.
- Deskilling ranking first is the notable part. The objection is not that the model is wrong; it is that the doctor stops being right.

### Washington's answer: an "AI Force"

- In a Truth Social post on Friday, **Trump said he will appoint an AI czar** and stand up a new **"AI Force"** modelled on the Space Force to oversee the industry — adding that *"only High I.Q. individuals need apply."*
- Set against California's kill-switch executive order from the day before, the two levels of government are now building opposite things at the same time.

### The labour number

- **2026 year-to-date: 383 layoff events, 210,741 workers — about 801 job losses per day.**
- **49% of those events explicitly cite AI, automation or machine learning**: 173,465 workers across 188 companies.
- Recent headline cuts: **Oracle 30,000** (the year's largest, notices sent 14 September, to offset AI chip and data centre spending), **Uber 3,300** (~10% of staff), **Nike 1,400**.

## What to watch

- **Monday's oil open.** Friday said Saudi risk was overstated; Saturday hit Riyadh. One of those is wrong and the repricing happens in a single session.
- **24 September.** Trump–Xi at the White House. Sunday's JPMorgan session either produced a deliverable or it did not — and the 10 November truce deadline sits right behind it.
- **Copilot and Gemini CLI.** Two of four agents were still unpatched over the weekend. The clock on a disclosed zero-click RCE runs fast.
- **Anthropic's Q3 numbers.** The whole reason the IPO slipped. They now arrive into a slowdown narrative and a live antitrust case.
- **The hiking path.** Futures want ~4.2% by December. Equities have not traded like they believe it.
- **Open weights.** Qwen's move to research-only licensing is the second major Chinese model to pull back commercial rights this year. Watch whether it becomes the default.
